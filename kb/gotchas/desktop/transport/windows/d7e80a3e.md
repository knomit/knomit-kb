# Windows 10 22H2 / WebView2 154.0.4258.62 / Wails beta.28: the Streams carrier measured fine (about 5 ms per event, close about 5 ms, resume at +508 ms, 65 s idle survives, bursts split into at most 256 frames / 1 MiB per answer without loss) while a handler-written 304 reaches the page as 500 — the loopback-proxy fallback was not needed

OBSERVED BEHAVIOUR on one Windows machine, 2026-10-12: Windows 10 Pro 22H2 (build 10.0.19045.6466), WebView2 runtime 154.0.4258.62, Wails v3.0.0-beta.28, build at the #488 merge 2104fd6d, a spike page driving the Wails Stream wire against a scratch server. The figures are DATED SUPPORT for the observed behaviour, not constants of the code; do not derive a timeout or limit from them.

- Per-event latency (Go 'sent to page' to page onmessage): 0.3 to 12 ms over 11 matched events, median about 5 ms; frames in order (open, ready, index frames per repo; branch stream status); 4 concurrent streams (repo-events, logs, branch) worked.
- Close: page close() to upstream connection closed and handler ctx cancelled took about 4 to 5 ms.
- Resume after a dropped upstream: error frame at the page 4 ms after the drop; reconnect after the 500 ms backoff, open at +508 ms, ready at +515 ms; events created after the resume reached the page.
- Idle: a stream held 65 s idle (at least three 20 s hold timeouts) stayed alive with no close or error and delivered the next two events 5 ms after the desktop forwarded them. The 204 hold cycle was inferred, not directly instrumented.
- Burst: SYNTHETIC at the Streams layer, not a real knomit backlog (the real logs backlog was only 58 frames / 9977 B): 600 frames x 4096 B (2.46 MB) arrived 600/600, 0 missing, duplicate or out of order, in 4 arrival batches [1, 253, 253, 93] over 81 ms (about 1 MiB per poll answer); 3000 frames x 1024 B arrived 3000/3000 in 13 batches capped at 256 frames per answer. Matches the source-derived streamMaxResponseBytes (1 MiB) and the 256-frame queue.
- POST through the proxy: bodies need the ContentLength = -1 fix on Windows too (see kb/gotchas/desktop/wails/asset-server/fcfd2da1).
- 304: a handler that writes 304 reached the page as 500, with or without If-None-Match.
- In the merged desktop on the same machine: exactly one repo-events stream opened at boot (even with the window hidden) plus one branch-events stream, one logs/events stream when the Logs tab was visited, no reconnect and no proxy warning; a repo created over loopback appeared in the rail 40 ms after the POST returned (about 2.5 min idle, frame-by-frame capture), within 2.1 s in another run, and was already present when the hidden window was shown again.

CONSEQUENCE: the Streams carrier needs no Windows-specific fallback; the loopback proxy listener recorded as a fallback in the design was not needed and stays unbuilt.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: 'Windows works' does NOT cover the connect-to-remote wizard's apply/commit progress, which is delivered only at the end (kb/gotchas/desktop/transport/06f9a76c) and was not run through the real wizard; the >1 MiB burst was synthetic; the UI's own '+' create was not driven through the merged proxy (the repos were created with curl on the loopback port); and Linux was not run.
