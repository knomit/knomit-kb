# CodeMirror 6's basicSetup keymap does NOT bind Tab to indent — Tab moves focus out of the editor unless you add indentWithTab

basicSetup (the codemirror package's bundled extension set, used by web/src/OntologyEditor.tsx) does not include `indentWithTab` from @codemirror/commands. This is CodeMirror 6's documented, deliberate accessibility-first default: without indentWithTab in the keymap, pressing Tab while focused in the editor moves keyboard focus to the next focusable element on the page rather than inserting an indent. Verified experimentally in the Task 6 create-repo-wizard spike by driving a real WebKit browser (Playwright webkit, not Chrome) against the OntologyEditor probe: after `document.activeElement` was `.cm-content`, pressing Tab left `document.activeElement.classList.contains('cm-content')` false, and subsequently typed characters were dropped (went nowhere), confirming focus had left the editor.

Consequence: Task 9 (the real ontology editor built on this spike) must decide deliberately whether to add `indentWithTab` to the keymap (trading keyboard-navigation-out-of-editor for in-editor indent-on-Tab) or keep the default. Do not assume Tab-to-indent "just works" because basicSetup is wired in — it doesn't, and this directly answers the create-repo-wizard plan's Task 6 brief manual-check item 1 ("does Tab indent, or move focus?" — it moves focus).
