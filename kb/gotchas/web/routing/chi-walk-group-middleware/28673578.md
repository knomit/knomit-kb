# chi.Walk drops r.Group middleware for subrouters mounted inside the group, so it reports /repos/{repo}/origin-sessions routes WITHOUT RepoMiddleware though every request runs it

go-chi/chi v5.2.5's Walk reports a route's middleware as the routers' own Middlewares() plus, for a LEAF route, the *ChainHandler middlewares of an inline group. For a subrouter mounted inside r.Group (r.Route or r.Mount there), the group's chain sits on the mount route's *ChainHandler, and Walk recurses into SubRoutes without reading it. So Walk reports every /repos/{repo}/origin-sessions route WITHOUT RepoMiddleware, though every request runs it.

Consequence: a route-tree diff or guard built on chi.Walk cannot see a subtree moved out of a group; read the mount route's ChainHandler too (walkFull in internal/edge/web/origin_sessions_mount_test.go does). DOES NOT MEAN Walk is wrong for leaf routes in a group: those it reports correctly. Dated support: chi v5.2.5 (go.mod at PR #624); line numbers in chi's tree.go are not pinned here.
