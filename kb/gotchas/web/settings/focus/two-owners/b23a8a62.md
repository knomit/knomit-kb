# RepoSettings has two focus owners; a consumed or dropped focus must clear every owner naming it

RepoSettings can be asked to focus a block by TWO independent owners: the app's view.focus (passed as `focus`, cleared through `onFocused` -> clearFocus -> CLEAR_FOCUS in facets.ts) and the local `returnFocus`, set to 'remote' when the connect wizard hands back. The page's effective focus is `focusEff = returnFocus ?? focus`. SettingsPage reports a focus to onFocused only ONCE, guarded by `focused.current === focus`, and only when it finds a matching block. So if a Reconnect sets the app focus to 'remote' while the connect wizard is open, and the hand-back then sets returnFocus to 'remote', SettingsPage consumes the focus once and clears only what its onFocused clears. If onFocused cleared just returnFocus, the app's view.focus stayed 'remote' and later pulled the page to Remote long after the click.

Rule: when a focus is consumed OR dropped, clear every owner that names the same block. RepoSettings' `onFocusedEff` always clears returnFocus and calls the app's onFocused when `focus === focusEff`. Clearing an already-clear owner is a no-op (CLEAR_FOCUS returns the same state when focus is null), so this cannot loop.

Unsatisfiable 'remote' focus: with no Remote block (creating, or hideRemoteConfig) the focus is never consumed. RepoSettings drops it itself with an effect keyed on [focusEff, hasRemoteBlock]. Do NOT replace this with a generic 'drop a focus whose block is missing' rule in SettingsPage: sections such as Licence arrive late on purpose (RepoDetail pushes `license` after its GET resolves) and rely on the focus waiting.

Does NOT mean: that every missing-block focus should be dropped, or that onFocused alone clears the local returnFocus (it does not; RepoSettings must).
