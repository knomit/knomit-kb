# One events stream per mounted chat means a browser on plain HTTP/1.1 runs out of its per-origin connections at about six live chats, and every other API call (including DELETE) then stalls

App keeps one ChatFacet mounted per listed chat and each opens its own SSE stream. Browsers cap HTTP/1.1 connections per origin (Chromium: 6); with six or more streams attached, a fetch from the page never completes, a reload queues behind the old page's streams, and the rail's x cannot send its DELETE. Observed with 9 chats in headless Chromium against a plain-HTTP server; the number is the browser's, not the code's. Consequence: do not assume the rail can offer more than five working chats over HTTP/1.1 until the streams are multiplexed (one chats stream) or served over HTTP/2.
