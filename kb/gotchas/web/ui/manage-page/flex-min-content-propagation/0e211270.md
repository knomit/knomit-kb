# A card at min-height:auto grows to its scroller's WHOLE content — min-content propagates up a flex column, and the descendant's minHeight:0 is a floor that does not stop it

On the Manage detail panes, a card that holds a scrolling body MUST carry `minHeight: 0`, and its small-window floor MUST live on an ancestor rather than on the card.

**The mechanism, and why the obvious reasoning is wrong.** `min-height: auto` — the flex default for a flex item — floors that item at its MIN-CONTENT height. Min-content propagates UP a flex column from the content: a scrolling descendant's own `min-height: 0` is a FLOOR, not a ceiling, so it does not reduce that descendant's min-content size, and the full scrollback/table height travels up through any intermediate wrapper into the card's min-content. The intuition that `min-height: auto` merely "floors the card at its own chrome" is false — it floors the card at chrome PLUS the entire content, which is unbounded.

**Measured**, at a 1400x1200 window on the Logs tab: the card rendered 1570px tall inside a 1056px pane, the pane scrolled instead of the log, and the status line sat off screen. Isolating it, with nothing else changed: card at `min-height: auto` → 1570px; card at `minHeight: 0` → 1056px, scroller 940px, scroller scrolls, zero dead space.

**So:** `minHeight: 0` on the card is what lets `flex: 1` win and pins the card to the pane. The floor that stops a very short window drawing content outside the card then goes on the ROOT (`CARD_FLOOR` in both files) — on the card itself an explicit floor would contend with `flex: 1` for the same axis. Below the floor the root outgrows the pane and `detailCol` (`overflowY: 'auto'`, web/src/RepoManager.tsx) scrolls the whole card, which is the intended degradation.

**COROLLARY, equally load-bearing:** such a card must then carry NO `overflow` property of its own. That degradation works by letting the card overflow the root; a scroll container on the card absorbs exactly that overflow, the root never outgrows the pane, and the floor silently stops doing anything — besides nesting a second scrollbar inside the body's. The Sessions card carried `overflowX: 'auto'` before PR #202; it moved to the table wrapper rather than staying. The card is also the obvious place a later reader reaches when a table needs scrolling, which is the reintroduction path.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN.** Not "always set minHeight:0 on flex items" — that is a different and much-repeated rule about shrinking. The claim here is specifically about min-content PROPAGATION from a scrolling descendant, which is why a `minHeight: 0` already present on the scroller does not make the card safe. Nor does it license replacing the bound with a viewport fraction: `maxHeight: '58vh'` is what this replaced, and it was wrong because the pane is not the viewport — it left dead space at every size and did not track window resizes.

**Not verifiable by unit test.** jsdom does not lay out; the whole suite passed against both the broken and the fixed version.
