# shortHash (the first 8 characters of a commit id) is declared separately in the repos parent (consensus.go) and in the trigger child because the dispatcher's copy moved with triggers.go; a third copy exists in synthesize and store/merge

At ee850fd6 (PR #632). shortHash was declared in triggers.go and used by consensus.go, fleet_activity.go, fleet_move.go and fleet_peers.go. When triggers.go moved into trigger/, the parent got its own copy (consensus.go) and the child kept its own. The same 5-line helper also exists in synthesize and in store/merge. CONSEQUENCE: changing the abbreviation length means editing every copy; a child may not import the parent to share it.
