# TestScript_NoIOPrimitives's old 1.5M-iteration clock-probe loop overran the 5 s script budget under `go test -race` (goja ~14× slower: 0.85 s → ~12 s on arm64), so the fire timed out before knomit.emit and the test reported '1 payload(s) never arrived' — deterministic on Apple silicon, 5 of 8 Race workflow runs on ubuntu; fixed on fix/race-workflow-flakes by pinning the run clock through triggerHooks.now instead of spinning

Observed 2026-09-29 while verifying F07 PR 5: `go test -race ./internal/repos/ -run 'TestScript_NoIOPrimitives$'` failed 3/3 at the PR's head and 2/2 at origin/dev 854709848e1e (a clean detached worktree), idle machine, macOS arm64. Without -race it passes in about 1 s. The probe script spins `for (i < 1500000)` between two Date.now() reads to prove the clock is pinned; under the race detector goja's interpreter is several times slower, so the fire hits fact.ScriptEvalTimeout (5 s) → `script-timeout`, and no payload is emitted. On the Race workflow's ubuntu runner (checked 2026-09-29) it is borderline rather than deterministic: FAIL in runs 36457474102, 36473901629, 36508768596, 36565331045, 36601821120; pass in 36441730126, 36583365116, 36614313171. The log line just before the failure is `trigger script exceeded its budget budget=5000`, and the test takes 15.2 s (the 5 s budget plus the 10 s wait). The bare loop, measured in isolation with the repo's goja version, takes 0.85 s normally and about 12 s with -race on arm64. Before fix/race-workflow-flakes, a local -race run of internal/repos was red on this test whatever change was under test.

Fix (branch fix/race-workflow-flakes, reviewer's variant): the test no longer spins. It sets `triggerHooks{now: func() time.Time { return pinned }}` with pinned = 2001-02-03T04:05:06Z. The dispatcher's clock() honours hooks.now, rs.now feeds RunScript, and RunScript feeds newSandboxVM's SetTimeSource. The test asserts that Date.now() and new Date().getTime() both equal pinned.UnixMilli(). It runs in about 0.2 s with or without -race. Sabotage: dropping vm.SetTimeSource in fact.newSandboxVM makes it fail with and without -race (actual is the wall clock, about 1.79e12 ms). Lesson for sandbox-clock tests: prove a pinned clock by pinning it to an instant the wall clock cannot produce. Do not prove it by spinning long enough to cross a millisecond; that length is sized from a fast machine.
