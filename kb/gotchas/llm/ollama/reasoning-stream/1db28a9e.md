# The Ollama adapter used to drop thinking that arrived after the first content or tool-call line

Before commit 82ec20ba, OllamaChat.Stream flushed its reasoning block once, at the first content or tool-call line (guarded by a reasoned flag). Thinking that streamed in after that flush was appended to the thinking buffer and never emitted: it reached neither the response blocks, the EventReasoning sink, nor the KNOMIT_LLM_TRACE file. Test TestOllamaChat_ReasoningAfterFirstFlushIsKept (thinking, content, thinking, tool_call, thinking) is the regression anchor. Consequence: any reasoning written after the first content line is absent from the trace and from replay history. Do not reintroduce a single flush point.
