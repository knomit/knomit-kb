# A member's record_state, revoked state and last-sync fields follow ITS OWN copy of main on the sync interval (300 s default, GET /fleet sync_interval_seconds), and a revoked member on a knomit-hosted fleet may never show `revoked`: the Fleet page is not a live view

Observed building the Fleet pages (PR #528, verified at merge e417fd76). A member's chip (`record pending`, `revoked`, `member`) and Settings are derived from the member's own copy of the fleet repository: they change only after its next sync round (sync_interval_seconds = the copy's origin loop interval, 300 s by default). So after a join the record shows pending until the master has merged the member's record AND the member has synced; the prototype's "a few seconds" does not hold. The pages say "this page shows it after this instance's next sync (every N min)" from that field.

A member REVOKED on a knomit-hosted fleet (hub) may not be able to fetch at all: the hub's mTLS listener refuses its certificate, so the member never reads the CRL and may never show `revoked`. On a forge-hosted fleet the CRL is readable and it does. What the member CAN show is the handshake refusal: GET /fleet `last_error_refused` is derived from the recorded last_error containing pki.ErrRefusedByPeer's text (no extra state), and the page then says the fleet refuses this instance's certificate and that retrying will not help (not reproduced live, needs a revoked hub member).

CONSEQUENCE: never promise instant state on a member page, and never treat the absence of `revoked` on a hub member as proof it is not revoked. The sync loop does not yet stop retrying a refused handshake (PR8 candidate).

UPDATE (fleet PR8, PR #536, merge 67e19eb3): the hub case is closed for display. GET /fleet gains `revoked` and `last_error_refusal`, inferred from the recorded error and the member's own installed material (kb/invariants/fleet/sync/refusal-classes/), so a hub member refused at the handshake shows revoked although its record_state still says what its stale main said; and a fetch refused at the handshake is now recorded (before, a no-op push cleared last_error each round). The sync loop stops retrying for a revoked certificate (sync_stopped, kb/architecture/fleet/sync/revoked-halt/). The lag rule above still holds for everything else.
