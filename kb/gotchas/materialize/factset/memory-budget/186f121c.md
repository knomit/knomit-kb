# FactSet planning-memory waiters are woken all at once and race; a large plan can starve until its one-minute stall limit

fsReserve (internal/corpus/materialize/factset_cache.go) grants a call's planning reservation when it fits the shared budget (session.factset_memory_mib, default 128 MiB since PR #723 (64 MiB before), after evicting idle cached FactSets) OR when no other reservation is in flight. Otherwise the call waits on a broadcast channel: every release wakes EVERY waiter, they re-check in arbitrary order, and a newly arriving call can also take freed memory ahead of callers already waiting. There is no queue. Consequence: under SUSTAINED saturation a large reservation (high max_nodes, or a first call on many roots) keeps losing to smaller ones that fit sooner, until FactSetStallLimit (1 minute) passes and it fails with ErrFactSetBusy (REST 503 + Retry-After; the web stream retries a bounded number of times).

Size of a reservation (fsPlanReserve): (2*prefetchWindow+1) * max_nodes * 4 KiB, i.e. 13 * max_nodes * 4 KiB with prefetchWindow 6; for a first call on an unbuilt FactSet at least roots * max_nodes * 128 B. The per-node byte figures are MEASURED on one machine (see the code comment), not properties of the code. Rough capacity at the default 128 MiB budget, derived from those figures and assuming no cached FactSets hold budget: default max_nodes 200 -> about 10 MiB per call -> about 12 planning calls at once; max_nodes 500 (the ceiling) -> about 25 MiB (13 * 500 * 4 KiB = 25.4 MiB) -> about 5 at once. Cached FactSets share the same budget, so the real count is lower while they hold memory. These capacities are measured-machine estimates, not properties of the code: never derive a constant or a limit from them.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it is not a limit on OPEN FactSets or on users. A reservation is held only while a call PLANS (a first call, a page that plans a new unit, a seek); a page served from kept plans plans nothing, and the reservation is released before the response. Saturation needs that many plans in flight at the same instant, sustained; short bursts just wait briefly and proceed.

DECISION (user, 2026-11-02): FIFO / queued waiters were explained (strict FIFO would bound waits but make small calls wait behind a large head-of-line one; FIFO with bounded overtaking needs a tuning constant, against the no-fixed-corpus-constants principle) and NOT built. Recorded here so a future multi-user deployment that sees 503s under load knows the cause and the fix shape: a FIFO queue of waiters in fsReserve, head-only wakeup in release, and removal + wake-next on ctx cancellation or stall timeout.
