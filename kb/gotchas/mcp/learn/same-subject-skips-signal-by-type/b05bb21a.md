# The same-subject gate's `signal` and `frame` skips are NOT redundant with the synthesis seed filter's kind exclusion — deleting either as dead code silently starts refusing coordination traffic or mission frames

This fact exists to stop one specific deletion. For the gate's full contract see [[same-subject-refusal]]; this is only about why its `signal` skip cannot be removed.

`findSameSubjectCandidates` skips `signal` candidates by TYPE, at internal/edge/mcp/learn_same_subject.go:153, beside the `hypothesis` skip at :144 and, since #668, a `frame` skip at :162 (lines re-read at 1eb481b6). A reader who knows that pragmatic facts are "already excluded" will be tempted to delete it as redundant. It is not, and here is the distinction:

- The exclusion they are thinking of is `reviewStrategy.AcceptSeed` plus `SeedQuery`'s SQL clause. It governs the SYNTHESIS SEED POOL and nothing else — see [[dirty-facts-excludes-pragmatic]] and [[exclusion-differs-by-scan-path]].
- The same-subject gate runs inside `knomit_learn`, over whatever the search index returns, on the WRITE path. It has never consulted Kind at all. Nothing else in that path would stop a signal being offered as a collision candidate.

Two mechanisms guarding different paths look redundant when described at the level of "pragmatic facts are handled". They are not, and no test would catch the removal by accident: the cost is a FALSE REFUSAL, so the failure shows up as posting a second task or acking one being rejected as a same-subject collision — the lane blocked, not a fact corrupted.

Why the skip is keyed on TYPE rather than KIND, given the fact it is protecting is pragmatic: policy and heuristic are ordinary claims about the world and CAN legitimately collide with an incoming fact. A kind-level skip would wrongly exempt them. Only `signal` has the task-id-as-entity shape that makes the gate meaningless — which is the same reason the neighbouring skip names `hypothesis` rather than a kind.

The `frame` skip (PR #668, merged to dev as 1eb481b6) is the same shape and has the same reason: a frame is a node of a mission's thinking tree, worked with rather than believed, so sibling frames, or an observation about the same subject, are not restatements of one another and the refusal advice ("update the existing fact") is wrong for a tree node. It covers the CANDIDATE side only. Whether an INCOMING frame is merged, refused or allowed to settle a hypothesis is the topic's `learn_dedup` setting, not the type (see kb/decisions/fact-types/frame/type-only-scope/85d04f3b.md). Deleting the frame skip as redundant, or widening the skips to a kind-level test, are both wrong for the reasons above: policy and heuristic can legitimately collide.

The signal skip shipped in PR #247 (merged to dev as f8142735); the frame skip in PR #668. The ref below is pinned at the #668 merge commit, where both skips are present and the line numbers above were re-read.
