# The same-subject gate's `signal` skip is NOT redundant with the synthesis seed filter's kind exclusion — deleting it as dead code silently starts refusing coordination traffic

This fact exists to stop one specific deletion. For the gate's full contract see [[same-subject-refusal]]; this is only about why its `signal` skip cannot be removed.

`findSameSubjectCandidates` skips `signal` candidates by TYPE, at internal/mcp/learn_same_subject.go:145, beside the `hypothesis` skip. A reader who knows that pragmatic facts are "already excluded" will be tempted to delete it as redundant. It is not, and here is the distinction:

- The exclusion they are thinking of is `reviewStrategy.AcceptSeed` plus `SeedQuery`'s SQL clause. It governs the SYNTHESIS SEED POOL and nothing else — see [[dirty-facts-excludes-pragmatic]] and [[exclusion-differs-by-scan-path]].
- The same-subject gate runs inside `knomit_learn`, over whatever the search index returns, on the WRITE path. It has never consulted Kind at all. Nothing else in that path would stop a signal being offered as a collision candidate.

Two mechanisms guarding different paths look redundant when described at the level of "pragmatic facts are handled". They are not, and no test would catch the removal by accident: the cost is a FALSE REFUSAL, so the failure shows up as posting a second task or acking one being rejected as a same-subject collision — the lane blocked, not a fact corrupted.

Why the skip is keyed on TYPE rather than KIND, given the fact it is protecting is pragmatic: policy and heuristic are ordinary claims about the world and CAN legitimately collide with an incoming fact. A kind-level skip would wrongly exempt them. Only `signal` has the task-id-as-entity shape that makes the gate meaningless — which is the same reason the neighbouring skip names `hypothesis` rather than a kind.
