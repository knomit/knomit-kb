# The learn.go comment saying a learn_dedup: off topic is never touched by review is stale: learn_dedup governs knomit_learn only, and review still seeds knowledge facts under a flagged topic

internal/mcp/learn.go (around the LearnDedupOff skip in the dedup stage) says: 'a flagged fact also skips hypothesis subsumption below ... and review never touches the topic either'. The first half holds (the skip sits before the search, so subsumption is skipped for an incoming fact under the flagged topic). The second half does not: TestDirtyFacts_LearnDedupOffDoesNotAffectSeeds (internal/synthesize/review_kind_filter_test.go) pins that a knowledge-kind fact under a learn_dedup: off topic is still a review seed (F02's scope: the attribute governs knomit_learn only). Signals are kept out of review by KIND, not by this attribute.

CONSEQUENCE: setting learn_dedup: off on a knowledge topic (the mission KB's forecast and verdicts) does NOT hide it from knomit_review; do not rely on the comment to reason that it does, nor avoid the flag for fear of losing review. Found while choosing learn_dedup: off for forecast in knomit PR #410 (mission-template-post-run, merged at 8bbd7963); the comment was left unchanged (template-only PR).
