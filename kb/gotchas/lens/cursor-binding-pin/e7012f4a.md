# Lens/repo binding-name collision weakens the §7.3 cursor binding pin — must be closed before Phase 6 lens CRUD

Tool-session cursors are pinned to their binding by NAME plus write-mount branch (tool_sessions.binding column; checks in queryResume/explainResume). Binding.Name() is the lens name for a lens and the repo name for a lens-of-one — the two namespaces are NOT disjoint, and neither LensRegistry.Create nor Manager.ValidateLens rejects a lens named identically to a repo. Consequence: a lens named 'core' writing to repo 'core' produces Binding.Name()=='core' on BOTH the /lenses/core/mcp and /repos/core/... endpoints, so a cursor minted on one endpoint resumes on the other whenever the write-mount branch pins align — a cross-binding resume §7.3 says must be rejected. Damage is bounded today: every snapshot item is commit-pinned, and lens-minted kb://-qualified items resumed under the repo's lens-of-one fail safe at Binding.ByID with the session-expired error; only bare (write-repo) items leak across, hydrated at identical (branch, path, commit). Related bounded gap: delete+recreate of a same-name lens mid-cursor passes both resume checks and hydrates read-mount items on the NEW pinned branch (frozen commits still protect content; unreadable rows are skipped). WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it does not mean the binding pin is broken for distinct names — foreign-binding rejection is tested and byte-indistinguishable from expiry. Fix before the Phase 6 CRUD surface makes lens creation user-reachable: either reject lens-name/repo-name collisions in Manager.ValidateLens, or namespace the session pin (e.g. 'lens:eng' vs 'repo:core'); per-mount branch pins in session state would also close the redefinition gap.
