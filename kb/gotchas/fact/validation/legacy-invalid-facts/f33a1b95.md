# Legacy facts that already fail a rule block nothing until they are CHANGED: a scan on 2026-10-16 found 1 such principle in knomit-kb and 17 in agentic-engineering-kb (no `designer` entity, must-have-designer-entity); any edit of one, by REST or by the review pipeline, is refused unless the same write fixes it

MEASURED on 2026-10-16 with bare clones of knomit-kb, agentic-engineering-kb, cyberai-kb and knomit-playbooks, each repo's ontology at main (a dated observation, not a property of the code; re-scan before relying on the counts): knomit-kb 1 of 1446 facts fails at main (a review-distilled principle under kb/principles/ui/motif/, no designer entity); agentic-engineering-kb 17 of 445 (all principles written 2026-07-26 to 28 without a designer entity); cyberai-kb and knomit-playbooks 0. No pending agent branch of knomit-kb was refused by the entry judge on that date.

WHY IT BLOCKS NOTHING NOW: untouched facts are never judged (kb/invariants/store/verify/validation-net-change, kb/invariants/store/fact-write/validate-write); a replay or connect carries them over byte-identical without judging (WithCarriedOver).

CONSEQUENCE: the first time the review pipeline (prune confidence update, a merge), REST or an agent changes one of those principles, the write is refused with the rule's message (review: a warn, the legacy file unchanged, merge members kept), so review silently stops improving that fact. The change must also fix the rule (add the designer entity, or retract/replace the fact). A BRANCH that carries the unfixed edit is refused at push, fetch and `verify ci` until a later commit fixes or deletes the fact. MISREADING: 'the corpus is clean because the scan found nothing blocking': the scan found failing facts that are merely untouched.
