# Review's dedupCluster deletes the loser of any same-cluster pair above Dedup with no judge, and distinct_from does not protect a pair from it; since #638 that needs a cosine above 0.905 (gemma), which no measured distinct pair reaches

Prediction: a genuinely distinct pair that scores above the model's Dedup in one review cluster will lose one member to review's mechanical dedup, and a learn-time distinct_from for that pair will not prevent it. dedupCluster searches candidate pairs at MinSimilarity = the Dedup threshold, merges winners and DELETES the loser with no judge (its own comment: dedup is the one consumer that acts on a cluster with no judge), and it never reads distinct_from (request-only, never stored; see distinct-from-honoured-by-merge).

Since #638 the trigger needs a cosine above 0.905 on gemma (0.95 on nomic). On the 1,647-fact knomit corpus no pair judged distinct reaches it (highest 0.8915 gemma / 0.9377 nomic). The prediction is about a denser future corpus, and is falsified by finding a distinct pair above the floor that review leaves alone.

Consequence: when sibling families grow, re-sweep Dedup (method in thresholds.go) before trusting that mechanical dedup is safe; do not rely on distinct_from to protect a pair from review.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it does not say learn's merge is unsafe for text (incomingIdentityWins keeps the existing statement); this is the review path only.

UPDATE (#648, merge 2782d21c): one case is no longer a delete-the-loser-after-merging. When the loser is a HYPOTHESIS and the winner is not, dedupCluster treats it as learn's unjudged subsumption: the winner only CITES the hypothesis in its refs, and the hypothesis is deleted in the same BatchWriteFacts commit (message 'dedup: <winner> cites <loser>, retracted [recipe]'); NONE of the loser's fields transfer (no confidence, sources, entities, motifs, evidence weight) and NO Knomit-Outcome trailer is written, because nothing judged a verdict. The prediction above is otherwise unchanged: it is still mechanical, still judge-free, still ignores distinct_from, and the trigger is still the Dedup cosine. All other pairs still go through the merge-and-delete path. See kb/invariants/synthesize/settle/51a99ac3.md.
