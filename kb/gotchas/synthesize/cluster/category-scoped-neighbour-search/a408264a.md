# ScopedCluster's category fence is on neighbour EXPANSION, not the edge step — a NON-SEED cousin can never join a seed's subgraph at any cosine, but two cousins that are BOTH seeds can co-cluster via the unfenced SubgraphEdges

`ScopedCluster` builds each review's subgraph in two steps, and only the FIRST is category-fenced:

1. **Neighbour EXPANSION (fenced):** each seed's neighbours are searched with `Path: categoryDir(seed.File)` — a hard restriction to the seed's own parent directory (`cluster.go`). A fact under `.../vulnerabilities/cisco/` can only pull neighbours from `.../vulnerabilities/cisco/`. A cousin under `.../vulnerabilities/networking/cisco/` that is NOT itself a seed is **structurally unreachable at any cosine** — measured at 0.969 on the live corpus, still in a different cluster.

2. **Edge construction (UNFENCED):** `store.SubgraphEdges` (`subgraph_edges.go`) returns the SIMILAR_TO adjacency AMONG the collected paths with NO category predicate. So if two cousins are BOTH seeds (both in the dirty set), they are already both in the path set, their SIMILAR_TO edge is returned, and Louvain CAN co-cluster them — measured in-fixture, the edge was present.

**So the precise guarantee is narrower than "cousins never cluster": a NON-SEED cousin can never ENTER a seed's subgraph; two cousins that are both seeds can.** The earlier claim ("two facts filed under different freeform categories can never co-cluster — at any similarity") OVERSTATED it — corrected here (bf-1, measured against `SubgraphEdges`, 2026-08-26). Do not cite the old blanket form.

Why it still matters: `category` is freeform BY DESIGN (the ontology validates `topic`, generates the uuid). The same event ingested on a different day gets a different freeform category — that CREATES the second copy (no path collision to notice), and the Step-1 fence then hides the non-seed cousin from every consumer of clustering: prune items, distill groups, dedupCluster's mechanical merge, both bridge axes, discover.

CONSEQUENCE for anyone touching clustering or dedup: cluster membership is "similar facts whose neighbour EXPANSION reached each other," NOT "similar facts end up together." A mechanism assuming the mechanical merge catches a near-duplicate is assuming the cousin was reachable via a co-category seed, not mere similarity. The corollary a reader must NOT draw is the reverse — that cousins are always kept apart; a dirty-set that happens to contain both halves will cluster them.

STILL PRESENT at HEAD, tracked as issue #149, gated on a designer ruling. Deliberately NOT fixed alongside #127: removing the Step-1 restriction re-clusters the whole corpus and moves EffortNormal's output, which collides with the MN5 byte-identical guarantee. #127 routed around it (repairing the restatement shortlist — the vehicle for pairs whose halves cluster apart); #149's ruled fix AUGMENTS prune with a cousin-join for the leftover-bucket (non-seed) case rather than removing the fence, so its target population — the non-seed cousins — is exactly the case this correction leaves intact.
