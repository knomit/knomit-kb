# Frozen scenario worlds still carry two generator tells: planted facts cite world facts more than filler, and filler bodies are shorter; the mechanical filler-ref fix closed the first only partly and the second not at all

Measured at the 2026-10-12 end-of-wave sweep over every world in every pool (expert-scenarios TELLS.md, commit ad43783). Groups: support (role support), planted (distractor, contradiction, superseded), filler (background); hypothesis, settling, methodology and policy facts are in none of them. Two measures per group: mean body characters of the first-written spec body, and the kb-ref rate (share of facts whose refs cite at least one fact:<id> world fact; stub https:// sources do not count). Flags: filler-short when filler's mean body is more than 15 percent below planted's; filler-kb-low when filler's kb-ref rate is more than 0.20 below planted's.

Measured before the fix: training pool kb-ref rate support 0.45, planted 0.34, filler 0.18 (body chars 218 / 192 / 173); held-out families 0.35 / 0.42 / 0.11 (221 / 190 / 172); held-out ontology 0.49 / 0.50 / 0.67 (250 / 255 / 183). Worst training families before the fix: F13 filler 0.02 vs planted 0.53, F03 filler 0.07 vs 0.58, F10 filler 0.30 vs 0.89, F06 filler 0.27 vs 0.69.

Fix applied (kb-ref only, filler only): 699 fact: refs added in 158 worlds, targeting filler rate >= planted rate - 0.18, all re-validated at knomit 51ca7d6d. A filler fact was eligible to cite only when it is in no question's chain, support, forbidden or answer lists, history never updates or retracts it, and it had no world-fact ref yet (the gold-safe rule, so no question's gold can change); it cites an eligible filler fact written in an EARLIER step (lesson 5) that shares an entity, else its topic and category. Pairs were spread round-robin over a group's worlds (seed 20261012). Support and planted facts and all wording were untouched.

Residuals left for a regeneration round: filler-kb-low in training F03 (0.375 vs 0.585), F10 (0.511 vs 0.886), F13 (0.190 vs 0.527) and held-out F15 (0.494 vs 0.786), because the group ran out of eligible earlier-step pairs; filler-short in training F03, F06, F13, F20, F21, held-out F18 and held-out-ontology F01, F02, F06, F07 (and that pool overall). filler-short was NOT fixed because the only mechanical extension is a templated sentence that only filler would carry, which is itself a tell; anything else is new wording and needs regeneration. Held-out-ontology per-family planted groups hold 1 to 6 facts, so their numbers are noise. Support facts cite world facts more than filler everywhere by construction (chains run over refs); that is not a planted-vs-rest tell.

Consequence for E05's generator-tell classifier check: do NOT expect a classifier reading text and fields to be at chance on the frozen worlds. Body length separates filler from planted in the filler-short families, and ref rate still does in F03, F10, F13 and F15; a classifier that wins there is measuring these known residuals, not necessarily a new tell. Report the check per pool and family, and compare with TELLS.md before concluding the generator leaks.

What this does NOT mean: the worlds are not tell-free, and the filler-ref fix did not rewrite any fact; also a filler fact that now cites another filler fact is gold-safe by construction, so the added refs must never be treated as chain material.
