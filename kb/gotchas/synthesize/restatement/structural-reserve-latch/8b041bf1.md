# The structural pair reserve sits behind `budget >= 2`, and a defunded corpus probes with budget=1 — so a defunded corpus can NEVER offer a structural pair, even on its recovery probe

The reserved structural slot (#127) fetches path-identity and rare-token pairs only when `budget >= 2` (restatement.go). The reserve is a budget ALLOCATION, not a threshold (MN13): it changes what may be considered, never how much is spent. But a defunded corpus's recovery probe runs with budget=1, so the structural block is unreachable exactly while defunded — a second self-defunding latch, same shape as the throttle's probe latch (see the self-defunding-latch gotcha).

Consequence: measuring "0 structural offers" on a defunded corpus tells you about this latch, not about the detector or any eligibility clause downstream of it — code inside the `budget >= 2` block never executed, so changing it cannot be the fix. This is the operative cause of issue #155 on a legitimately-defunded corpus.
