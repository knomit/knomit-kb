# FIXED (#155): the structural route no longer spends the ordinary band's budget — it has a dedicated per-session allowance that fires even while defunded, breaking the latch where `budget >= 2` met a probe budget of 1

HISTORICAL SHAPE (the bug, kept because it is the diagnostic worth recognising elsewhere). The reserved structural slot fetched path-identity and rare-token pairs only when `budget >= 2`. The reserve was a budget ALLOCATION, not a threshold (MN13) — it changed what may be considered, never how much is spent. But a defunded corpus's recovery probe runs with budget=1, one below the gate, so the structural block was unreachable exactly while defunded: a second self-defunding latch, same shape as the throttle's own probe latch (see the self-defunding-latch pattern).

Measured on the live core corpus before the fix: 2,086 standing path-identity pairs and 228 rare-token, `eligible` pinned at 0 across four sessions spanning scopes of 69/4/1/24 items while standing climbed 498 → 950 → 950 → 1281.

THE FIX (merged, PR #164). The route's cost stopped being expressed in the ordinary band's currency. `structuralAllowance` (5) is a per-session RESOURCE budget spent WHATEVER the throttle says; the sweep is decided BEFORE the throttle and independently of it, so every throttle early-return below it is a decision about the title-ranked band alone. The reasoning that justifies it: the throttle governs spend on TITLE-RANKED candidates, which is what its verdict history is evidence about — it has never judged a structural pair, so it has no evidence on which to withhold one.

Three properties of the fix a consumer must not misread:
- The allowance ADDS to the budget, it does not spend from it. Emission counts budget-funded pairs separately (`budgeted`), so the ordinary band still receives every slot the throttle funded and the allowance cannot become a tax on the band it was separated from.
- `OperatingPoint` reads the budget-funded count too. It describes what the THROTTLE chose; on a defunded corpus, where the allowance is the only thing emitted, reading the last emitted pair would print a structural cosine as though the throttle had picked it.
- The scope filter is dropped on this route (a structural pair is corpus-wide evidence by construction) but the CO-MEMBERSHIP exclusion survives — "prune already sees this pair" is true whatever route found it. Dropping the scope filter is a widening, and it is announced in the health line rather than done silently.

CONSEQUENCE THAT STILL HOLDS: measuring "0 structural offers" tells you about the OFFERING PATH, not about the detector or any eligibility clause downstream of it. That diagnostic was the whole content of this fact before the fix and it survives the fix — it is why the first scoping blamed a scope clause that never executed.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the throttle is not disabled. It still decides the ordinary band completely. And the allowance is bounded — "fires even when defunded" without a bound would be a corpus ignoring its own throttle, so reachability and boundedness are two independent guarantees with two independent tests.
