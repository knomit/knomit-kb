# A zero reading on the restatement shortlist is evidence about the OFFERING PATH, never about the corpus — four independent mechanisms produce the identical observable, and three of them are self-confirming

Four separate defects in the restatement shortlist were found and fixed between #117 and #164. They live in four different stages, have four different mechanisms, and were fixed four different ways — but every one of them produced the SAME reading: `restatement candidates emitted: 0`, and in three of the four the corpus then defunded itself on that evidence. **The observable cannot distinguish them, so a zero is never evidence that the corpus has no restatements.** Read it as a claim about the offering path and walk the stages in order.

**Stage 1 — never planned (edge trigger).** `planRestatementShortlist` is called inside `reviewStrategy.Plan`, and the pipeline's empty-seed early return completes the session BEFORE reaching Plan. A quiet, caught-up corpus therefore never drained its standing backlog, and the sweep advanced only while the corpus kept changing — the opposite of the population it serves. Resolved by the `standingWorkStrategy` hook that the empty-seed branch calls; note the hook plans the STANDING channel only, reads the QUEUE rather than the selection count, and a SCOPED session never plans standing work (the pools are corpus-wide by construction).

**Stage 2 — candidates deleted before selection.** `filterByBlendedCosine` dropped every standing pair at or above the model's calibrated dedup floor as "mergeFacts already handles it" — false for exactly this population, because `dedupCluster`'s mechanical merge only ever pairs facts in the SAME cluster and the shortlist exists for restatements whose halves cluster APART. BEING A CERTAIN DUPLICATE WAS THE DISQUALIFIER. Measured on live core before the fix: all six confirmed duplicate pairs sat at blended cosine 0.83–0.97 against a floor of 0.82 and all six were ABSENT from the cache, while ~two dozen unrelated partners per fact were cached, topping out at 0.77.

**Stage 3 — funded but unreachable.** The reserved structural route fetched path-identity and rare-token pairs only when `budget >= 2`, while a defunded corpus's recovery probe runs with budget=1 — so the structural block was unreachable exactly while defunded. Measured: 2,086 standing path-identity pairs and 228 rare-token with `eligible` pinned at 0 across four sessions while standing climbed 498 → 1281. Fixed by `structuralAllowance` (5), a per-session RESOURCE budget decided BEFORE the throttle and spent whatever it says — it ADDS to the budget rather than taxing the ordinary band, and it is bounded (reachability and boundedness are two independent guarantees with two independent tests).

**Stage 4 — the funding signal itself.** The shortlist is funded by a trailing rate over its own judge verdicts, so being defunded stops it emitting, which stops verdicts arriving, which makes "a resolution refunds it" unreachable BY CONSTRUCTION: a latch, not a governor. The bounded probe path is the fix, and its slot must be consumed only when a pair was actually SERVED — consuming on SELECTED reintroduces the latch at a slower rate, which is what shipped until knomit#117b. A neighbouring instance of the same shape: counting only MERGES as success would defund a corpus succeeding by RETRACTION, and from outside that is indistinguishable from "the shortlist finds nothing" — which is why `resolved` counts merge OR retraction, while a confidence update counts as neither.

**THE DIAGNOSTIC ORDER.** Before concluding anything about a corpus from a zero: (1) was the shortlist PLANNED this session at all; (2) what did the candidate GENERATOR remove; (3) was the route REACHABLE at the session's budget; (4) only then ask about the throttle. The failures compound and each one alone is sufficient to produce the reading, so finding one explanation does not clear the others.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN.** It is NOT "cosine filtering is wrong" — the floor remains the right predicate for deciding whether a pair needs a JUDGE rather than a mechanical merge; it was applied where the mechanical merge could not reach. It is NOT "the throttle is disabled" — the throttle still decides the ordinary title-ranked band completely, and the structural allowance is bounded. It does NOT mean the watermark gates DETECTION — pair detection is corpus-wide when it runs, and growth of `restatement_pairs` is detection INVENTORY, not an offer queue. And it does NOT generalise to every zero in the pipeline: this is a claim about the restatement shortlist's four named stages, not a licence to disbelieve any empty result.
