# Claude Haiku 5.5 reading whole web pages without a selector kept essential main-point recall at 88.7 (20 pages, 14.15 facts per page) vs 25.7 for the stored Opus/Sonnet-written KB facts of the same pages (1.4 per page); the two are different products, so this is not a quality ranking of the stored facts

Measured 2026-10-27 (Haiku ingestion spike, rounds 3-5; evidence in the git-ignored .claude/future/ingestion/haiku/round4/RESULTS.md and round5/RESULTS.md, repo-relative, not pinned). Sample: 20 whole pages (12 cyberai-kb, 8 agentic-engineering; at most 30 chunks; only pages whose stored first-write facts were written from the same page text). Metric: an Opus blind answer key of 11-32 main points per page; a Sonnet matcher scores which essential points each system states (lenient: full = 1, partly = 0.5).

Results with the round-4 H55-fix prompt: essential recall lenient 88.7 (CI 85.9-91.7) vs stored KB facts 25.7 (17.4-35.9); Haiku higher on 20 of 20 pages; supported sentences 99.0% vs 45.0% (101 of the stored facts' 133 unsupported sentences classed 'judgement'). Round 3 on selected passages showed the same gap (87.1 vs 26.8). Adding the knomit placement fields (round 5) did not move recall (89.4, paired +0.7, CI -5.5 to +6.0); anchored variants in rounds 7-9 measured 89.8-91.4.

Passage vs page: on 30 selected passages the same prompt scored 96.9 with 9.0 facts per passage, on whole pages 88.7 with 14.15 facts; the answer keys differ (passage key vs whole-page key), so the two numbers are not paired. The spike-12 selector had capped any reader: on agentic-engineering only 16 of 47 reference facts were inside the three selected sections.

Why the stored-facts comparison is different in kind: the stored facts were written from the whole page with project intent, usually one selective cross-cutting lesson per page (e.g. a design lesson about monitoring requests against a declared scope), not the page's main points. A main-points key cannot reward that, and the faithfulness judge calls such synthesis 'judgement'. Haiku states the page's points and does not produce that abstraction.

What this does NOT mean: not that stored KB facts are poor, and not that Haiku replaces curated lesson-writing; it measures coverage of a page's main points only.
