# Haiku-written 'Does not establish ...' caveats: a prompt rule alone filled about 3% of facts; a required empty-by-default field filled 19.6% with 10 of 64 ungrounded 'absence' limits; restricting the field to limits the document itself states gave 0 ungrounded and 0 harmful caveats (42 and 33 caveats)

Measured 2026-10-27 (Haiku ingestion spike, rounds 6-9, 30 whole pages; evidence in the git-ignored .claude/future/ingestion/haiku/round6/RESULTS.md through round9/RESULTS.md, repo-relative, not pinned).

Measurement. A Sonnet faithfulness judge ('faith7') is shown the page text plus its title, URL, host and the date on the page; publisher and date taken from that block count as supported, and every caveat sentence is rated grounded (the document states the limit) yes/no and harmful ('would an agent trusting it believe something false or unsupported') yes/no. The earlier judge, shown neither the URL nor the rule, scored correct limit lines as 'judgement' and host-derived publishers as 'fabricated', which alone failed every round-6 candidate on faithfulness: a measurement artefact, not a writing failure.

Results. Round 6, prompt rule only: about 3% of facts got a limit (deterministic count) while an over-reading judge wanted one on 29-51%. Round 7, a required string field, empty as the stated default, folded into the body by code: 64 of 326 filled (19.6%), 10 ungrounded, 0 harmful; 9 of the 10 were 'absence' limits ('not independently verified', 'no measured dataset across other organisations'), a shape the prompt's own example ('by the crew's own count') invited. Round 8, only five document-stated shapes allowed (a scope it draws, a condition it names, something it says did not happen, a plan it marks unshipped, an attribution it makes explicit) and absence limits forbidden: 42 of 336 (12.5%), 0 ungrounded, 0 harmful. Round 9, same rule with examples naming the source: 33 of 368 (9%), 0 / 0. Supported sentences under faith7 stayed 99.2-99.5%.

Operational consequence: a caveat slot needs an explicit ban on statements about what the source lacks, and its worked examples must be of the grounded kind, because Haiku copies the example's shape.

What this does NOT mean: the falling fill rate is not lost coverage by itself, and 0 harmful does not mean caveats are complete: on four hand-written gold pages the gold had 18 limits on 32 facts, and Haiku still leaves some document-stated scope limits unwritten.
