# Fact granularity in whole-page ingestion is mostly a model trait: under the same extraction prompt Haiku 5.5 wrote 14.0 facts per page on 4 gold pages vs 6.75 (Sonnet) and 8.25 (Opus), and a lesson-oriented prompt cut each model's count by only 18-30%, so it did not make Haiku compress

Round 10 of the ingestion spike ran two extraction prompts on three models over the same 20 whole web pages. Prompt F asks for one fact per main point; prompt L is F with only the extraction instruction replaced by 'one fact per lesson, supporting specifics inside the lesson's body'.

Facts per page over 20 pages: Haiku F 12.5, Haiku L 9.6, Sonnet L 4.65, Opus L 6.65. On the 4 gold pages under F alone: Haiku 14.0, Sonnet 6.75, Opus 8.25. L reduced the fact count by 23% (Haiku), 30% (Sonnet) and 18% (Opus on the gold 4); the pre-registered bar for a prompt effect was at least 40%, and it was not met. Haiku L still wrote 70% more facts than the mean of the two larger models, and on one security-advisory page it still split the remediation into three facts.

Lessons recall (lenient, Opus-written lessons key, 20 pages): Haiku F 87.1, Haiku L 81.2, Sonnet L 86.5, Opus L 96.1. Haiku L lost 5.9 points of lessons recall against Haiku F (paired CI -10.0 to -1.8). Sonnet L matched Haiku F's recall with 4.65 facts and 40% less text at about 7.5x the per-page cost; Opus L was best on lessons recall (96.1) and detail retention (97.6 vs 90.3) at about 18.5x Haiku F's per-page cost.

Consequence: to get lesson-sized facts, change the model, not only the prompt. Do not expect a 'write lessons' instruction to make Haiku produce fewer, larger facts; it merges a little and loses some recall.

What this does NOT mean: it does not say lesson-sized facts are better. Detail retention and retrieval depend on the task (see the retrieval fact). The lessons key was itself granular (12.25 lessons per page), so lessons recall rewards coverage and not few facts. Sonnet F and Opus F were run on the 4 gold pages only (budget); the model comparison under F rests on those 4 pages. Single run per cell, same-family judges (Claude), cost figures are CLI-reported list prices at Oct 2026.
