# Whether a fact stands alone must be judged cold: a readiness judge that saw the source passage rated Haiku-written facts 94% standalone, while a cold-read judge seeing each fact alone, shuffled among other pages' facts, found 46.3% of the same facts carried an unresolved reference

Measured 2026-10-27 (Haiku ingestion spike, rounds 5-9; evidence in the git-ignored .claude/future/ingestion/haiku/round6/RESULTS.md, round8/RESULTS.md and round9/RESULTS.md, repo-relative, not pinned).

Method (cold read). Each fact, title and body only (no frontmatter, no URL), is placed in a batch of 20 facts shuffled across pages and prompt variants, with a list of the 30 candidate document titles and hosts. A Sonnet judge must (a) name the source document or NONE, scored against the true page, and (b) list every phrase whose referent the fact does not fix ('the crew', 'the lab', 'the passage', an unglossed codename or surname). The primary metric is the share of facts with at least one unresolved phrase.

Finding. The same 268 round-5 facts: 94% 'standalone' by a judge shown the passage, 46.3% with an unresolved reference by the cold read, and only 19% dated. Document identification stayed at 90% because most facts carry a product or CVE name; the failure is the unnamed frame (whose campaign, whose incident, who says so). Identification accuracy alone therefore does not measure standalone-ness.

Noise. The same facts re-judged in fresh batches moved 0 to 5 points per run (round-6 B: 8.1 / 8.1 in round 8; 10.5 / 9.5 seen and 4.6 / 9.3 unseen in round 9). Pooling facts lets one page decide: a page with 21 facts and document-coined labels carried about 3 points of a 20-page pooled rate, and the pooled gate and the per-page paired difference disagreed in round 9 (pooled 10.6 vs 10.0; paired -3.9, CI -8.2 to -0.1).

Operational consequence: measure standalone-ness with a judge that never sees the source; run it at least twice and report a per-page paired view beside the pooled rate. Do NOT read a judge's 'standalone' verdict as meaningful when that judge was shown the document.
