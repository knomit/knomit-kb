# Measured cost of Claude Haiku 5.5 writing a story from 25 and 40 facts: single call $0.0117 / $0.0048, pipeline $0.0786 / $0.0520 (CLI); direct-prompt rerun $0.0169 / $0.0094; two cheap OpenRouter models $0.0039-0.0106 / $0.0005-0.0040

Measured 2026-10-26 (materialize research spike rounds 3 and 4; evidence in the git-ignored round3/COST.md and round4/writers/COST.md, repo-relative, not pinned). Inputs: art1 = 25 facts (long bodies, 15.5k source words), art2 = 40 facts (short bodies, 3.4k words). Costs are exact: the CLI's total_cost_usd for Haiku (which prices all input at the 1-hour cache-write rate, see the claude -p cost gotcha) and OpenRouter's usage.cost for the others. All prompts were under the 100k-token price tier.

Haiku 5.5 via CLI, per article (art1 / art2): one-call writer over the raw fact bodies $0.0117 / $0.0048 (about $0.00047 and $0.00012 per fact; 34.6k and 9.5k input tokens); multi-stage pipeline (per-fact claim extraction, outline, section writing) $0.0786 / $0.0520, i.e. 6.7x and 10.9x the single call; extraction alone was $0.0619 of the $0.1307 pipeline total (47%). Pipeline average about $0.0020 per fact, single call about $0.00025 per fact; the single-call prompt grows linearly and stays under the 100k tier up to roughly 147 facts of art2's size (art1-size facts, about 1.4k tokens each, cross it near 70 facts, and are 5x dearer beyond it). A 'direct-v2' prompt rerun, Haiku $0.01686 / $0.00937 (2,059 and 2,246 words; 83 s and 55 s latency).

Cheap models, same direct-v2 prompt (art1 / art2): glm-5.3-flash $0.00391 / $0.00051 (needs reasoning effort 'minimal' because 'disabled' is rejected with HTTP 400; list price $0.15/$0.50 per MTok in/out but routed to providers charging as little as $0.04 in), deepseek-v4.1-flash $0.01057 / $0.00397 (list $0.02/$1.00). Average per fact across both: glm $0.00007, deepseek $0.00022, Haiku $0.00040.

Consequence: when costing a fact-to-article feature, the number of LLM stages dominates the bill (7x to 11x here), not the model; one call over raw bodies is the cheap shape for these set sizes. WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: cheaper is not equivalent. The single-call articles lost caveats relative to the pipeline (caveat retention 0.41 and 0.68 against 0.996 and 0.99, round 3), and the cheap OpenRouter models were measured near-par on the Artificial Analysis index (42 and 39 against Haiku's 43 at its best setting), not proven par; deepseek cited only 14 of 25 art1 facts. OpenRouter prices vary up to 10x across providers for one model. Prices are list values fetched 2026-10-26 and will date.
