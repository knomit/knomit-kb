# All eight direct-v2 articles overshot the 1,200-1,800-word target (1,973-2,817 words across Haiku, glm and deepseek), and their uncited, miscited or editorial claims concentrated in the lead and the closing takeaway, with body errors as exceptions

Measured 2026-10-26 (materialize research spike, rounds 3, 4 and 7; evidence in the git-ignored .claude/future/materialize/ files round3/grades/GRADES.md, round4/writers/grades/GRADES.md, round7-lean/grades/GRADES.md and round7-lean/VERIFY.md; repo-relative, not pinned).

LENGTH. The direct-v2 prompt asked for 1,200 to 1,800 words. Grader word counts (citation markers excluded): round 4, art1 deepseek 2,283, glm 2,450, Haiku 1,990; art2 Haiku 2,190, deepseek 2,817, glm 2,338; round 7 (Haiku, 50-fact and 48-fact packs) 1,973 and 1,977, alongside the two round-4 Haiku articles re-graded at 1,990 and 2,190. Every one of the eight distinct articles was over the 1,800 ceiling, by about 10% (1,973) to 56% (2,817). The round-3 single-call prompt, which set no word target, had produced 1,541 and 1,563 words. Against the source pack, round-4 art2 articles were 65-84% of the 3.4k-word pack (little saving over reading the facts), while art1 articles were 13-16% of 15.5k words.

WHERE THE UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS SIT. In round 3's grading, leads and closings were the weakest part of every article (meta-sentence ledes, thesis sentences cited to facts that do not state them). In the round-4 and round-7 gradings the uncited, miscited or editorial claims were found in the lead or closing takeaway of every article that had any: a lead saying the harness 'moves scores most' where the fact says 'of the same order'; an uncited thesis and a decorative closing citation; a lead citation pointing at the wrong facts; a takeaway that generalised a Brotli-only lesson to PHP and Ruby; 'record-setting' and 'unprecedented scale' framing; and a lead date, 'In the two weeks to 26 October 2026', that no source states. Round 7's mechanical number check flagged 3 and 2 numbers absent from the cited facts, all year or day numbers (each did occur in some other input fact).

EXCEPTIONS: the concentration is not exclusivity. Body faults found by the same graders include a paragraph framed 'AI tools amplified attackers' reach' that its fact does not support, 'fastest tested' attached to tokens per second where the fact means minutes per index task, a forthcoming feature called 'added', a resolved incident filed under unresolved threats, a misfiled AIUC-1 gap, and body sentences that dropped a precondition (CVE-2026-22708 without 'an attacker who can influence the agent's instructions') or stated a ranking effect slightly stronger than the data.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: a grader reading only the lead and closing would miss these body faults; and 0 inverted negations (see the writer-prompt fact) says nothing about framing claims. Eight articles, one grader per round, three models in round 4 but one in round 7; counts of faults are small.
