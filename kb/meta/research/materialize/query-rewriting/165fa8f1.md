# Rewriting 16 questions into 3-5 search queries (LFM2.5 1.2B, LFM2.5 2.6B, Claude Haiku 5.5; RRF-merged) gave 0.852 recall@10 under every condition, the same as the plain question, and lowered MRR from 0.906 to 0.858-0.875

Measured 2026-10-26 (materialize research spike, round 5; evidence in the git-ignored .claude/plans/materialize-spike/round5-queries/ directory, repo-relative, not a pinned ref). Eval set: the 16 answerable questions of round 4 (8 on the agentic-engineering repo, 8 on the cyberai-kb repo; direct, caveat and synthesis types, with 1 to 11 gold facts each). Gold = the facts each question's answer was written from. Search = the REST search endpoint on one branch, purely semantic (embedding cosine over a nearest-neighbour window, no lexical component), limit 25. Four conditions: raw (the question as the single query); lfm12, lfm26 (LFM2.5 1.2B / 2.6B Q4_K_M via Ollama, one zero-shot system prompt, JSON schema, 3-5 queries, temperature 0.1); haiku (claude -p --model haiku, same prompt, 5 queries on average). Per-query top-25 lists were merged with reciprocal rank fusion (k=60) and cut at 10 and 25.

Results over all 16 (raw / lfm12 / lfm26 / haiku): recall@10 0.852 / 0.852 / 0.852 / 0.852; recall@25 0.852 / 0.865 / 0.865 / 0.902; MRR 0.906 / 0.875 / 0.865 / 0.858. 11 of 16 questions were at recall@10 = 1.0 under every condition. The only recall gains were at rank 25: the LFM rewrites gained on one art2 question (a2-q08, a missing gold fact at ranks 11-25) and Haiku's rewrites on three (a2-q05, where its version-number queries reached a related PHP fact that the CVE-id queries did not; a2-q07, 1 of 11 gold facts; and a2-q08). MRR fell because the extra queries' off-topic neighbours are fused in ahead of the best gold fact. Survey-type questions failed under every condition: a1-q08 (3 gold facts) found 1 of 3 at rank 1 by all four; a2-q07 (11 gold facts) found 0 by raw, lfm12 and lfm26 and 1 of 11 at rank 18 by Haiku. Cost per rewrite: lfm12 0.65 s, lfm26 2.2 s (no spend), Haiku 4.9 s and about $0.0002 (CLI-reported). LFM rewrites mostly re-asked the question in other words; lfm12 sometimes drifted (it invented 'Malware incidents 2024').

Consequence: on this eval a rewriting step did not buy top-10 recall; a question that needs many facts enumerated is not fixed by better query wording because there is no single semantic target to aim at.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it does not show rewriting is useless for retrieval in general. The questions were written FROM their gold facts, so they echo the facts' wording and name their entities, a ceiling-effect setup that favours the plain question; real questions that do not share vocabulary with the facts were not tested. n=16, one corpus snapshot, one prompt, one temperature: Haiku's 0.05 recall@25 difference is spread over three questions. The ingestion lfm-fit fact rates query rewriting as a good LFM fit for generating queries; this measures the retrieval EFFECT of the generated queries and does not contradict that the models can produce them.
