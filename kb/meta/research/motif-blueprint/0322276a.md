# Motif blueprint: the Memora-derived mechanism-bridging design was a .claude/plans document that is now gone; its realization lives in PRs #102/#106 and the decisions/synthesize/motif facts

The 2026-08 research spike on Memora-style mechanism bridging produced two artifacts, both kept under .claude/plans in the knomit source checkout:
- an implementation blueprint, .claude/plans/2026-08-18-memora-bridging-analysis.md. Part I was the build spec with verbatim SHIP prompt blocks, Part II the condensed evidence from five measurement rounds, and section 13 the design-to-evidence traceability.
- a reproducible experiment harness, .claude/plans/memora-harness/: a blind bridge harness, four motif eval packs with model outputs, matching dry-run scripts, seed 42 throughout.

**Neither exists any more, and git history cannot recover them.** `.claude/*` is gitignored, so they were never committed. As of 2026-09-23 no commit on any branch contains either path, and the checkout no longer has them. Do not go looking for them in history.

What survives:
- **The shipped design**: PRs #102 (phase 2, lifecycle and surfaces) and #106 (phase 4, activation and measurement), plus the decisions/synthesize/motif/* facts that record what was built and measured.
- **The in-repo measurement driver**: tools/motifannex, the Phase-2 GATE annex that ran motif passes over copies of lab corpora. It was deleted on branch chore/drop-motifannex-refprobe once the campaign ended; its last version is at commit 9d83dd7a. See decisions/tools/drop-motifannex-refprobe.
- **Motif measurement against a live index**: `calibrate bridges --kind motif`, which calls synthesize.MotifComponentReport.

The headline design as recorded then:
- an optional motifs: field (0-3 mechanism names per fact, names only at write time);
- a derived-state vocabulary lifecycle (LLM-judged alias clustering with carrier context, blind per-name definitions);
- a matching cascade: permissive prefilter -> rank -> budget -> discover agent (~68% cross-author recall at high effort);
- operating points bound to effort level, including a verbatim-only tier at normal (cap 2);
- retrieval surfaces with a motif_match strictness knob.

The blueprint's figures cannot be re-checked against it now. Treat them as historical, and prefer the per-decision facts, which cite code.
