# Recording test stubs for lens providers must be mutex-guarded — the provider contract is one call per mount, CONCURRENTLY; classify stubs by reachability, not blanket-lock

Since the parallel lens fan-out, the stats/search/activity provider contract is "called once per mount, concurrently". Production providers are stateless empty structs mutating only their local SearchOptions copy — but a RECORDING stub's bookkeeping (maps of per-mount SearchOptions, call counters) is shared mutable state, and go test -race fails on the stub's own recording, not on the code under test. The lock must wrap the WHOLE recording: nil-map initialisation AND the write, on every entry point.

Classify by reachability rather than locking everything: the concurrently-reachable set at the time of writing is lensPivotFactsStub, lensStatsStub, lensActivityStub, lensSearchStub and motifResolvingLensStub. Stubs behind still-serial paths correctly need no lock — lensFactsStub/lensCarriersStub serve only the serial /facts collection fan-out, and lensMotifsStub.Clusters is driven by federate.BuildMotifUnion's plain serial loop. The repo-scoped single-mount stubs have no fan-out at all.

What this does NOT mean: a -race failure in a lens test does not imply the production fan-out races — check whether the racing writes are in the stub's recording first. And the serial-path exemption is only as durable as the serial paths: parallelising /facts or the motif union build moves those stubs into the must-lock set.
