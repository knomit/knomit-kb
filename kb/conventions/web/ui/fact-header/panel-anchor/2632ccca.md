# Both fact-chrome panels (ConnectionsPanel 360px, MotifPanel 420px) are position:absolute, top:100%, LEFT:0 on the edgesGroup span. They open rightward into the pane; a right anchor there hangs them off the pane's left edge, where overflow:hidden clips them

The fact header's edges row opens two panels:
- ConnectionsPanel, for 'cited by' / 'cites' (one panel whose direction switches), `CONNECTIONS_PANEL_WIDTH` = 360.
- MotifPanel, for the 'same motif' cells and the +N overflow, `MOTIF_PANEL_WIDTH` = 420.

They share an anchor and a surface style but NOT a width. Both are `position:absolute; top:100%; left:0` (+ marginTop 6). Their containing block is the edgesGroup span in RightPanel (`position:relative`). That span wraps the WHOLE edges row, the in/out cells and every motif cell, and starts at the fact pane's left padding (28px). FactBand draws the row above the fact body's scroll container, so an open panel stays put while the body scrolls beneath it.

**Consequence:** a panel anchored `right:0` on that span opens LEFTWARD by (panel width − span width). Both panels are wider than a typical row, so a right anchor puts the panel's left part outside the fact pane. The fact view's ancestors are `overflow:hidden` (RightPanel's fact-view root and its body wrapper), so that part is CLIPPED at the pane edge. It looks like the list rail covers it, but this is not a z-index/stacking problem, and raising zIndex does nothing.

**Tests pin the contract.** ConnectionsPanel.test.tsx and MotifPanel.test.tsx each have 'hangs from the menu, left-aligned', which asserts `left` is '0px' and `right` is unset. jsdom cannot see clipping, so the tests assert the anchor itself.

**Why in-tree, not portalled:** the panels are in-tree absolute children of the edgesGroup span. That is what lets the `menuRef` hover keep-open and `useDismiss` treat trigger and panel as one DOM region. Portalling to body with fixed positioning and a viewport clamp would escape every overflow:hidden ancestor. The cost is repositioning on scroll and resize, and re-homing the hover and dismiss regions across the portal. For a one-property anchor fix that trade was not taken. Revisit it if the right/bottom clip below becomes a real problem.

**What this does NOT mean:**
- *Not* that they are safe in any pane width. Left-anchored, a panel needs about its own width + 28px of pane: ~390px for connections, ~450px for motifs. Below that, the RIGHT side clips on the same overflow:hidden, and MotifPanel clips first. The bottom can clip the same way in a short pane. No width cap or clamp exists. This predates the anchor fix and is only reachable in narrow layouts, e.g. a small viewport with the list dragged wide.
- *Not* that a new panel added to the row may pick its own anchor. It hangs from the same span, so it inherits the same rule.
- *Not* that the two panels share one shell component. Each inlines its own style literal, and only the tests keep the anchors in step.
