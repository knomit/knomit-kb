# Tests hold or observe lifecycle stages through Options.Hook (point "enter" per stage, "index-job" inside the index job), and a blocking hook MUST also select on the life's ctx — never sleeps, never DisableBackgroundSync

Replaces indexHealGate and DisableBackgroundSync (issue #411). Deps.Machine carries repos.Options; Options.Hook is nil in production and is called with (stage, point, ctx) at the start of every stage Enter (point "enter") and inside the index job after its plan (point "index-job"). Helpers in internal/repos/machine_helpers_test.go: gate (hold a point until open(), waitArrived to know it is held), hooks(...) to compose, armedHook in machine_test.go (fires once when armed), waitIndexSettled / waitStatus (Watch-based, no polling sleeps).

RULE: a hook that blocks selects on ctx as well as its release channel. Unmount and rewinds cancel the life and wait for it; a hook that ignores ctx deadlocks teardown (the same trap indexHealGate had).

Options.Synchronous makes tests deterministic without disabling stages: Serve starts no experiment sweep and Sync runs one inline round and no loop; the index job still runs in the background, so a test waits for it through Watch rather than assuming it is done when Mount returns.
