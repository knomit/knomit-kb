# Template listing tests must not depend on the pinned playbooks content for fleet: TestTemplates_ListingRoutes asserts a Subset of coding/general/mission, never the full list, so the playbooks pin can move with or without a fleet template

The playbooks submodule (third_party/knomit-playbooks) is pinned separately from knomit and its pin moves in its own PR. After the fleet template left playbooks (PR #520 removes it there; the pin was NOT bumped by that PR), the knomit tests that list templates have to pass at the OLD pin (which still carries a fleet template, listed but refused at create) and at the new one. TestTemplates_ListingRoutes therefore asserts require.Subset(names, coding, general, mission) rather than the exact set; TestListTemplates and TestListTemplates_NotOpenRepoIsNotAWarning build their own source repos and need no playbooks content for fleet. Per the PR report these pass at the pinned commit and at the playbooks branch tip.

CONSEQUENCE: a template test that counts or enumerates the playbooks templates exactly will break on a pin bump either way; assert the subset you need. Evidence for the pass-at-both-pins claim is the PR author's report, not re-run here.
