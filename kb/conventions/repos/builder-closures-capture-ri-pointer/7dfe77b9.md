# Long-lived closures wired in build() capture ri (pointer), re-read svc under RLock at call time

Three closures built in repoBuilder.build() capture ri (the POINTER, not field values): the observer callback, ri.startSync, and ri.closeFn. They all re-read ri.svc under ri.mu.RLock() at call time, so after SwapStore swaps ri.svc to a new Service, these closures automatically see the new pointer. Anti-pattern: capturing b.svc directly in the closure — it would hold a stale pointer past SwapStore and the closure would operate on the closed DB. Same principle applies to ANY new long-lived callback wired in build(): capture ri (pointer), re-read svc under RLock at call time. The discipline pairs with SwapStore's contract of replacing ri.svc under withWrite — closures + SwapStore are two halves of the same correctness story.
