# CommitDrawer is a view-details affordance — it does NOT push navStack or change asOf

The CommitDrawer (web/src/CommitDrawer.tsx, 420px right-side slide-over) is a non-modal detail view, never a navigation. Opening the drawer does NOT push navStack and does NOT change asOf. Closing the drawer does not pop navStack. The drawer's 'scrub to <hash>' footer button IS what mutates asOf (and that mutation does push navStack, same as any other scrub) and closes the drawer. The underlying Library and right pane remain interactive while the drawer is open. Z-index covers the right portion of StatusFooter; the time-anchor pill on the left stays visible and clickable.
