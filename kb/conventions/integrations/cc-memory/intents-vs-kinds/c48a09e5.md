# Intents are conversation signals, NOT fact kinds — don't conflate them

The /detect endpoint scores blocks against intents (correction, discovery, decision, fix-bug, gotcha). These are SIGNALS that something might be worth capturing — they describe the conversational moment, not the resulting fact's classification. A 'correction' intent might lead to a 'convention' or 'invariant' fact; a 'fix-bug' intent might lead to an 'incident' or 'gotcha' fact. When implementing the detect handler or composing fact bodies from nudges, do not map intent name → fact kind 1:1. Choose the kind from the substance of what was learned, not from which canonical-phrase set tripped the detector.
