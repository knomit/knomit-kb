# Intents are conversation signals, NOT fact kinds — don't conflate them

Intent detection runs ENTIRELY in the bridge via regex (tools/bridge/claude/intents.go) — there is NO server-side `/detect` endpoint and no `internal/detect` package (both deleted; never re-add). Intents (correction, discovery, decision, fix-bug, gotcha) are conversational SIGNALS that a moment might be worth capturing — they describe the conversational moment, NOT the resulting fact's classification (its kind/type). A 'correction' intent might lead to a 'convention' or 'invariant' fact; a 'fix-bug' intent might lead to an 'incident' or 'gotcha' fact. When implementing capture, never map intent→kind directly; the model chooses the fact's kind/type from the content.
