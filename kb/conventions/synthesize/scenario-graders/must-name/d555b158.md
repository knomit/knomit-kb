# Scenario gold names a fact the answer weighed against in answer.must_name (format v1.1), never in must_cite: the grader checks the answer BODY names it by path or title, a named support-set fact counts toward chain recall, and CheckGold refuses a fact that is both must_cite and must_name

knomit-scenario v1.1 (compatible addition), from local commit 20434db7 on feat/expert-eval-runner: GoldAnswer.MustName (`must_name`). The grader (scenario_grade.go namedIDs) folds case and whitespace and looks for each fact's path or title in the body of the answer facts (never the gap hypothesis), adds a `must_name` validity check, and in chain recall counts a support-set fact as recalled when it is cited OR named.

Why: the ruling that a contradicting fact is never in an answer's refs (refs are why we believe it); the answer names it in its body. F7 gold had listed the contradiction in must_cite, and citedIDs counts refs only, so a correct engine scored zero (review cause Z3). The data repo (expert-scenarios a3c84de) migrated every F07 world by script: each fact a question's chain labels `contradicts` (last label stands) moved from must_cite to must_name, and each other contradicts-labelled fact of the question's support set was added there; 18 of 22 worlds changed, all 22 re-validated with TestScenarioSpecDir; FORMAT.md records v1.1.

Consequence for world authors: a fact the answer must mention but not rest on (a contradiction, an outweighed value) goes in must_name. A question's support set may still list it, and the chain labels it contradicts. Graders and replay must read the chain's own label before support-set membership (see architecture/repos/engine/replay).

What this does NOT mean: must_name is not a softer must_cite. A named fact that is ALSO cited still passes must_name, but citing a contradiction is caught by must_not_cite or forbidden when the gold lists it there. A loader older than 20434db7 refuses the key (strict decoding), so compatible means existing specs stay valid, not that old loaders read new specs. Pinned by TestGradeChain_MustNameReadsTheBody and TestEngineEval_Z1Z2Z3_ContradictionIsNamedNeverCited.

2026-10-12: re-pinned to merge commit 51ca7d6d after the eval runner merged into expert-spike (local); claims re-read there and hold (GoldAnswer.MustName in scenario.go, namedIDs in scenario_grade.go, TestGradeChain_MustNameReadsTheBody passes at the merge).

General rule (coordinator ruling at the 2026-10-12 end-of-wave sweep, recorded in the expert-scenarios FORMAT.md v1.1 note, commit e8d09f1): a chain step's support label is relative to the GOLD ANSWER's claim, not to the planted fact it argues with. A settling observation that refutes a hypothesis supports the answer 'refuted'; an observation that defeats a principle supports the answer 'does not apply here'; a refuting observation that a reflect item draws a methodology from supports that methodology; an audit verdict 'member X is contradicted' rests on the contradicting observation, which supports the verdict. So EVERYTHING in must_cite is labelled supports, and a fact that truly contradicts the answer's claim, or a principle that was defeated or outweighed, is weighed against and goes to must_name, never must_cite.

Applied across every family and pool at the freeze, by script, gold only (no fact wording changed), every touched world re-validated at knomit 51ca7d6d: (a) F20 defeat questions, both rounds: the deciding observation relabelled contradicts to supports (round 1 only; round 2 already had it) and the defeated principle moved must_cite to must_name (8 questions, e.g. f20-cs-seat-lease q2, f20r2-gsm-bend-limit q2); (b) F07 both rounds plus the two held-out-ontology law worlds the first migration missed (f07-law-notice-service q1, f07-law-quad-levy q1), so all 24 F07 worlds carry must_name; (c) 29 questions across F02, F10, F13, F16, F15 and F18 where a CITED fact was labelled contradicts, relabelled supports (F15 and F18 are held-out worlds, touched for gold only); (d) the reflect observations in f16r2-ops-ski-lifts reflect-wind-holds. Three F16 prune 'keep distinct' verdicts were deliberately left as they were: f16-code-settle-batches prune-close (i03), f16-gsm-full-stores prune-clattern-fill (c03), f16-ops-ferry-gateway prune-gateway-latency (l04), where the contradicts fact is in must_not_cite by design because the label is relative to its merge partner and the verdict names it.

Consequence for authors and for any gold-checking tool: when writing or auditing a chain, derive the label from the answer's claim; a contradicts label on a fact that is in must_cite is a gold bug (the 'contradicts' reading belongs to the planted fact's own argument), except in a prune keep-distinct verdict. What this does NOT mean: relabelling is not allowed to change which facts the chain visits, and the F16 exception shows that a contradicts label next to must_not_cite can be correct.
