# Pipeline.storeIndices() extracts all store sub-indices via a single WithRead, resolved once per engine entry point (Reviewer.storeIndices() delegates to it)

Pipeline.storeIndices() (internal/corpus/synthesize/pipeline.go) extracts ALL the store sub-indices (facts index, facts repo, search facade, pipeline index, HeadCommit, abstraction and motif indices) under a single ri.WithRead(svc) callback; the engine resolves them once per entry point through Pipeline.deps() and hands them down as a Deps bundle, and Reviewer.storeIndices() (review.go) is a thin adapter over it kept for the review tests. Callers then pass locals down to inner helpers. This pattern: (1) avoids repeated lock acquisitions in inner loops; (2) keeps Reviewer truly stateless (no field caching of indices); (3) ensures all four indices come from the SAME svc snapshot — critical for SwapStore safety since indices captured at different moments could span a store-swap window. Anti-pattern: calling r.ri.WithRead(...) inside an inner helper — extract once at the boundary and pass locals down the call chain.
