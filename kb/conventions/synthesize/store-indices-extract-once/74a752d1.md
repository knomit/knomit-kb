# Reviewer.storeIndices() extracts all four sub-indices via single WithRead at method boundaries

Reviewer.storeIndices() extracts ALL FOUR sub-indices (gs FactIndex, idx SearchIndex, pipelineIdx PipelineIndex, branches BranchIndex) under a single ri.WithRead(svc) callback at the TOP of each public method (StartSession, ContinueSession, handleWorkPhase, handleReflectPhase, maybeEnqueueReflectItem, completeSession). Callers then pass locals down to inner helpers. This pattern: (1) avoids repeated lock acquisitions in inner loops; (2) keeps Reviewer truly stateless (no field caching of indices); (3) ensures all four indices come from the SAME svc snapshot — critical for SwapStore safety since indices captured at different moments could span a store-swap window. Anti-pattern: calling r.ri.WithRead(...) inside an inner helper — extract once at the boundary and pass locals down the call chain.
