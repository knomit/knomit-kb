# Invented-name stem checks must scan the full spec text including source URL path segments: the freeze needed 46 renames and 12 id renames, all on the training side so held-out worlds stay as authored

Invented names (Quill-, Brindle- and others collided already in the first pilot batch) must not share a stem with another world's names, and above all not with a held-out world's. The end-of-wave sweep (expert-scenarios commits 90721c0 renames, 85015df audit and last rename) audited train vs held-out, names only.

The rule. The stem is the 4-letter capitalised prefix of an invented name, matched in any case, with dictionary words removed. A stem check must scan the FULL text of every spec: titles, bodies, entities, questions, gold claims AND source URL path segments, plus the SUBJECTS.md registry rows. The registry and entity scans alone missed names that appeared only in source URLs, which is why a second audit step (step 6) still found the velsby collision after the renames.

Result at the freeze: 46 renames and 12 id renames (incident and change ids such as inc-5207 to inc-5211, and a currency code VLD to VQK) across the training pool, listed in MANIFEST.md. Twelve invented train/held-out stem collisions were renamed on the TRAINING side so the held-out worlds stay exactly as authored (ambrolite, eskarrow, selvicola, vardle, varrock, lunvale, quedley, nimrowen, wyrran, sollivant, braskiana, velsby; quedley was identical to a held-out name, velsby collided with the held-out currency unit vels). After the renames no training world shares an invented name stem, or any exact registry name, with a held-out world. Remaining shared 4-letter stems are English or technical words (harbour, runbook, seagrass). Borderline by design and left: Kilnhurst (train) vs Kilnworks (held-out; English kiln), varroa (a real mite genus, used in both pools), mobbard vs mobbing.

Consequence: when adding a world or re-running the audit, rename on the training side only, scan source URLs and every text field, not just the registry, and re-validate the touched world (a rename edits the spec, so under the splits policy the old id and hash are no longer the frozen world: do this before a freeze, never after). Nothing beyond names (no fact text, claim or number) crossed between pools; the held-out worlds were opened for this audit and for the gold migrations only (LEAKAGE-LOG.md).

What this does NOT mean: a clean stem audit is not a content-leak audit; it proves no shared invented names, nothing about shared structure. The near-duplicate check is separate (highest word-3-gram Jaccard over any two worlds 0.023, median 0.001, no identical fact body or title across worlds).
