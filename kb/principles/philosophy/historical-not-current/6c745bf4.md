# Refs resolve at commit-time, not at HEAD

Knomit stores a HISTORICAL knowledge graph. Every ref, edge, and provenance link resolves at the commit point in time of the referrer — never at HEAD. Reading a fact at commit C means following its refs to whatever those targets were AT commit C, even if they've since been updated, moved, or retracted.

Why: the graph is a record of what was believed and when. Resolving against HEAD would silently rewrite history — a synthesis that cited three observations in 2024 would suddenly cite their 2026 revisions, breaking the evidence chain and making "why is this true?" non-reproducible.

Applies to: knomit_explain, the resolver, branch_commits/commit_parents traversal, derived_from edges, any code that walks refs, and any UI rendering of fact provenance.

What NOT to do:
- Resolve a fact's refs by reading the referenced file's current frontmatter
- Use HEAD-relative path lookups inside Explain/why traversal
- Cache "current target" of a ref — the target is commit-relative, not stable
- Backfill a moved/renamed fact's old refs to point at the new location
