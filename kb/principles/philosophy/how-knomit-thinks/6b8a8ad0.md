# Knomit is the mechanical embodiment of how a person accumulates knowledge: HEAD is what I know now, explain is how I came to know it, a branch is a world, refs are why I believe it

Knomit is a general, programmable knowledge platform. Facts about source code are one use among many; the same machinery holds general knowledge, engineering practice, fleet and mission coordination, and playbooks, each under its own ontology. Do not reason about knomit as a code-knowledge store.

Read it the way a person thinks:

- **HEAD of the branch you are on is WHAT I KNOW NOW.** Answer "what do we know about X" from the tip of the world you are in.
- **Explain is HOW I CAME TO KNOW IT.** A fact's history and its refs, each read at the version the referrer pointed to, are the reflection path. When "A because of B" becomes "A because of C", the new fact supersedes the old one, and explain still walks back to B. A superseded belief is history, not error: never call an older fact wrong because a newer one exists; say what changed and when.
- **A branch is a WORLD.** Every operation, every MCP tool, is bound to a repo and a branch: main, agent, or experiment. A branch encapsulates knowledge so you can play with it and enhance it, and merge it back into another world when you are happy. Know which world you are in, know you can switch, and say which world a result came from.
- **Refs are WHY I BELIEVE IT**, in the human sense of "I know F, because I read it in book B page P". Sources count the independent links into other systems. A ref is pinned to a commit and is followed at that commit; comparing it to the present only tells you whether the world moved since, which is a reason to update or supersede, not a verdict on the fact.
- **The other fields say where a fact fits and how to use it.** Kind says whether to weigh it (epistemic) or obey it (pragmatic); type is its shape; confidence is the writer's certainty in the claim; evidence weight is derived, never written; domain is its scope; entities are its subjects; motifs are the regularities it instances across subjects; context is a typed label for finding it by a property that is not its subject; expires is informational for now.

What NOT to do: verify a fact against HEAD first and report a difference as drift; treat the agent branch, main and an experiment as the same world; read a `src://` ref as "the file today"; assume the KB in front of you is about code; ask the user how knomit works when a `knomit_query` on `kb/principles/` answers it.

This is the umbrella over the temporal-graph mission, the timeline-of-belief and commit-time-resolution principles, the agent-branch rule and the coding-first misread, each of which states one facet. Stated by the designer on 2026-10-12 while briefing the expert research spike.
