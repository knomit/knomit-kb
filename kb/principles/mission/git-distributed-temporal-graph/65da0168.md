# A temporal, fact-based knowledge base

Knomit is a temporal, fact-based knowledge base.

Knowledge is captured as structured, typed facts — each carrying a
kind (epistemic/pragmatic), a leaf type (observation, policy, synthesis,
hypothesis, …), confidence, domains, entities, and provenance refs to
other facts. Facts are first-class citizens, queryable by meaning, scope,
similarity and lineage.

The KB is temporal: every fact, every edge, every ref resolves at a
point in time. The history of belief is preserved — when something was
known, by whom, under what evidence — and remains reconstructible. Facts
evolve in place; their lineage stays intact.

It is NOT: a chat-history log, a vector-store dump of raw text, a RAG
cache, or a real-time database. Those answer "what was said recently";
knomit answers "what is known, why, and since when."

Knomit is designed for AI agents as the primary consumer, with humans
as co-authors. Agents and humans contribute to the same fact graph
through the same operations.
