# Review's mechanical dedup merged a POLICY with an observation filed beside it: neighbour searches had no kind filter, so the policy entered an epistemic seed's cluster and dedupCluster deleted one into the other with no judge (knomit#308)

WHAT HAPPENED. Seeds were epistemic-only (AcceptSeed, convention 8b26a25d), but none of the three searches that grow or act on a cluster filtered kind: ScopedCluster's per-seed expansion, dedupCluster's candidate search, and the prune cousin sweep. Reproduced by the reviewer at dev e4175f7b: an observation and a policy in one directory, close in embedding space, landed in one cluster and dedupCluster merged them mechanically. Whichever won on confidence (mergeFacts), the corpus lost: if the observation won, the policy was deleted; if the policy won, the observation was deleted INTO the policy and the policy absorbed its refs and sources — and a policy that LOSES goes out through mergedFact, which carries no Kind, i.e. rewritten as epistemic.

FIX (PR #314, decision 3624365c). Two rules, deliberately different:
- Clustering neighbour searches (ScopedCluster, all five callers; joinCousinsForPrune) pass IncludeKinds from config [cluster_cache] neighbor_kinds, default [epistemic], empty refused.
- dedupCluster is NOT on that config: its search is pinned to epistemic (dedupSearchKinds) AND it refuses any pair whose kinds differ or are unknown (sameKnownKind, fails closed on ""). factForLLM gained Kind (json:"-") to make that check possible, filled at every construction site.

REGRESSION ANCHOR: TestNeighborKinds_DedupNeverMergesAcrossKinds in internal/synthesize/neighbor_kind_filter_test.go — a hand-built [obs, pol] cluster, and the real ScopedCluster -> dedupCluster path under the WIDENED list, both with a premise that the unfiltered search returns the other fact. Mutation-checked: removing the equality check fails both subtests; removing only dedup's IncludeKinds fails only the options subtest, because the search that STARTS at the policy returns observations regardless — the equality check, not the search filter, is what protects dedup under a widened config.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. It does not make judged prune or RAPTOR distill kind-safe under a widened neighbor_kinds: those still write through mergedFact/distillFact, which drop Kind. Widening is a future hook, not a supported setting.
