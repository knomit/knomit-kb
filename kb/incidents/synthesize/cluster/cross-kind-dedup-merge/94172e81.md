# Review's mechanical dedup merged a POLICY with an observation filed beside it: neighbour searches had no kind filter, so the policy entered an epistemic seed's cluster and dedupCluster deleted one into the other with no judge (knomit#308)

WHAT HAPPENED. Seeds were epistemic-only (AcceptSeed, convention 8b26a25d), but none of the three searches that grow or act on a cluster filtered kind: ScopedCluster's per-seed expansion, dedupCluster's candidate search, and the prune cousin sweep. Reproduced by the reviewer at dev e4175f7b: an observation and a policy in one directory, close in embedding space, landed in one cluster and dedupCluster merged them mechanically, with no judge.

THE HARM IS DELETION, NOT REWRITING. dedupCluster never goes through mergedFact: it re-parses the WINNER from its own file (so the winner keeps its kind), writes it back with the pooled fields, and DELETES the loser. Whichever won on confidence (mergeFacts), the corpus lost a fact across kinds: if the policy lost, the POLICY WAS DELETED; if the policy won, the observation was deleted INTO the policy, which absorbed its refs and sources.

FIX (PR #314, decision 3624365c). Two rules, deliberately different:
- Clustering neighbour searches (ScopedCluster, all five callers; joinCousinsForPrune) pass IncludeKinds from config [cluster_cache] neighbor_kinds, default [epistemic], empty refused.
- dedupCluster is NOT on that config: its search is pinned to epistemic (dedupSearchKinds) AND it refuses any pair whose kinds differ or are unknown (sameKnownKind, fails closed on ""). factForLLM gained Kind (json:"-") to make that check possible, filled at every construction site.

WHY sameKnownKind MUST OUTLIVE THE SEED FILTER. Convention 8b26a25d allows the seed filter to be removed once mergedFact/distillFact carry Kind. That change would not touch this route at all: dedup deletes across kinds whatever mergedFact does. Removing sameKnownKind alongside the seed filter would reopen #308.

REGRESSION ANCHOR: TestNeighborKinds_DedupNeverMergesAcrossKinds in internal/synthesize/neighbor_kind_filter_test.go — a hand-built [obs, pol] cluster, and the real ScopedCluster -> dedupCluster path under the WIDENED list, both with a premise that the unfiltered search returns the other fact; the real-path subtest also asserts the policy's refs name no observation, since a policy that WINS stays pragmatic. Mutation-checked: removing the equality check fails both subtests; removing only dedup's IncludeKinds fails only the options subtest, because the search that STARTS at the policy returns observations regardless — the equality check, not the search filter, is what protects dedup under a widened config.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. It does not make judged prune or RAPTOR distill kind-safe under a widened neighbor_kinds: those DO write through mergedFact/distillFact, which drop Kind. Widening is a future hook, not a supported setting.
