# Research claims enter as type=hypothesis capped at confidence 0.5 and graduate to observation only on independent replication — enforced by an ontology validation, not by instruction

**Options considered.** (a) Everything is an `observation` with a confidence ceiling (~0.4 single-paper, ~0.7 replicated). (b) `hypothesis` by default, graduating to `observation` on independent replication. (c) Split by claim class: headline results start as hypothesis, method rationale and negative results go straight to observation.

**Rationale.** Option (a) leaves nothing structural separating "one lab said this once" from "settled" — it is a number in a field that readers round off. Option (b) rides machinery that already exists: `applyDedupMerge` has exactly three outcomes per incoming fact, and one is "the match is a hypothesis this fact subsumes" — `subsumeHypothesis` stages the hypothesis for retraction and cites it as lineage in the new fact's refs, applied by the SAME `BatchWriteFacts` call, so the write and the retraction are one commit. Replication therefore promotes a claim mechanically rather than by an agent remembering to.

The pack's custom `research-literature` ontology carries this as a ROOT-level validation:

    rule: "fact.type !== 'hypothesis' || fact.confidence <= 0.5"

Verified against `ParseOntology`/`ValidateFact` on 2026-08-12: a hypothesis at 0.8 is rejected, the same fact at 0.35 is accepted, and an observation at 0.8 is accepted. That matters because the job runs unattended nightly for months — an instruction in the spec can drift, a compiled goja rule cannot.

**The choice.** Default `type: hypothesis`, confidence 0.2-0.4, body carrying the experimental setup that bounds the claim and an explicit falsifier. Graduation is by independent replication only.

**Non-scope.** The 0.5 ceiling is a starting value, not a principle. A survey paper synthesising twenty results is arguably not a single-source claim; revisit after the first week's output. Changing it means editing `ontology.yaml` AND the repo's stored copy once the repo exists — one line, but not free.
