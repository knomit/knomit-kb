# Six CC hook handlers in integration: SessionStart, PostToolUse(bash+edit), UserPromptSubmit, PreCompact, Stop

The integration ships SIX hook handlers in tools/bridge/claude/hook.go (the original design called for four; the set expanded as the integration matured). Five hook event types in settings.json.tmpl (PostToolUse fires for both Bash and Edit|Write|MultiEdit, dispatched to two different handlers). All on by default. (1) SessionStart pre-warms with invariants + top recently-updated facts. (2) PostToolUse on Bash filters for 'git commit' and nudges /knomit-remember or /knomit-decided on substantive commits (heuristic: stdout >60 chars OR markers like fix:/refactor:/decided:/invariant:/gotcha:). (3) PostToolUse on Edit|Write|MultiEdit dispatches to the post-edit handler. (4) UserPromptSubmit captures correction language. (5) PreCompact — the most important safety net, last chance before discoveries get summarized into oblivion. (6) Stop fires every turn but is rate-limited (default: at most one nudge per 5 turns) to keep noise down. Auto-write via subagent (Shape D+) listed in §5 as a future extension.
