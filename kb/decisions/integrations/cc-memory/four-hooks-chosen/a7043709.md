# Four CC hooks in initial integration: SessionStart, PostToolUse(git commit), PreCompact, Stop

The initial integration ships exactly four hooks, all on by default. SessionStart pre-warms with invariants + top 5 recently-updated facts. PostToolUse on Bash filters for 'git commit' and nudges /knomit-remember or /knomit-decided on substantive commits (heuristic: stdout >60 chars OR markers like fix:/refactor:/decided:/invariant:/gotcha:). PreCompact is the most important safety net — last chance before discoveries get summarized into oblivion. Stop fires every turn but is rate-limited (default: at most one nudge per 5 turns) to keep noise down. UserPromptSubmit correction detection, Bash failure capture, and TodoWrite nudges are opt-in extensions in §5.
