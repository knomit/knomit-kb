# FactBody (a fact's prose, tags and references) has ONE consumer, renderFact in FactView.tsx; Browse (RightPanel) and the chat side panel (ChatSidePanel) share it by both mounting FactView, not by rendering FactBody themselves

web/src/FactBody.tsx renders the CONTENT of a fact: the markdown body in a 'k-prose' block, then, ruled off below it, a property table of Domains, Entities and References (RefList, which draws each ref from the server's `kind`: url, fact, broken, foreign, source_code). Props: `fact`, `dispatch`, `onRefClick?`, `repoNames?`, `filterable?` (default true). A domain or entity click dispatches ADD_FILTER only while `filterable`; a fact ref is clickable only when `onRefClick` is given.

It is no longer the whole fact view. The type and origin chips and the Confidence/Sources boxes moved to FactMetaLine in the header band, and the band's controls (the version, the date, materialize, retract) are FactActions in FactChrome.tsx. FactBody takes no `readOnly` and no `navigate`.

**Consumers at 70826ce6.** Exactly one non-test call site: `renderFact` in FactView.tsx, which wraps it in FactPage (FactChrome.tsx) under the band. FactView is THE fact view, and two places mount it:
- Browse, through RightPanel.tsx.
- The chat's side panel, through ChatSidePanel.tsx's PanelFact, which passes a close button as `bandAction` and `hideRetract`.
ExplainView and ChatFactView no longer exist.

**Consequence:** a change to how a fact's content looks goes in FactBody, and a change to the fact view goes in FactView, once; both Browse and the chat pick it up. Do not render FactBody directly in a new surface, and do not fork FactView for the chat. FactView.tsx's header comment says so, and FactView.shared.test.tsx covers the sharing.

**What this does NOT mean:** not that FactBody is the shared unit. Sharing happens one level up, at FactView. A surface that mounted FactBody alone would lose the band, the edges row and the retract and materialize controls.
