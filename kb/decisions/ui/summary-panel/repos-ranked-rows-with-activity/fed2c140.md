# The lens summary's Repos section is a ranked, borderless table with the 7/30/90d change meter and NO share bar — the card-per-mount, repo-as-fourth-facet and chip-rail options were all rejected

Four treatments were mocked against the real panel and the user chose C, then had the share bar removed after seeing it in place.

**Options considered**
- **NOW (rejected)** — the shipped LensRepoRow: one bordered, filled card per mount, ~51px each (~330px for six). After PR #70 replaced the facet clouds with ranked columns, this was the only boxed thing left in the panel and read as imported from another design. Nothing encoded relative size: a 1377-fact mount and a 9-fact mount were the same visual weight.
- **A (rejected as the whole answer, kept as the base)** — ranked borderless rows in the FacetPanel grammar: dot + mono name, a share bar under the name, then right-aligned tabular columns for facts / confidence / top domains / recency.
- **B (rejected)** — fold Repos into FacetPanel as a fourth column. Costs zero vertical space and deletes the section, but has nowhere to put confidence, top domains, recency or the write marker, and squeezes the other three columns to a quarter of the width.
- **C (CHOSEN)** — A, plus `changes_7d` / `changes_30d` / `changes_90d` as a three-tick activity meter.
- **D (rejected)** — repos as a wrapped rail of source-badge chips, ~60px. Counts stop being comparable and a fifteen-mount lens wraps into a hue soup.

**Rationale**
C spends data the panel already fetches and throws away: `LensRepoStats` has carried changes_7d/30d/90d since the union-stats endpoint shipped and `LensRepoRow` rendered none of them — turning the list from an inventory into a pulse. B lost because a mount is not a peer of a domain string: it has an identity (write target, sync state, its own recency) the facet grid would delete. D lost on scale.

**The choice**
Ranked rows, sorted total DESC then name ASC (the same tie-break `FacetPanel.ranked` uses, and for the same reason: JSON object order must not decide which of two equal mounts leads). Per row: hue dot, mono name, write marker, ONE top domain; then facts, confidence, a 3-tick meter, relative last_commit. A zero-fact mount renders dimmed with `—` rather than a claimed 0.00 confidence. ~211px for six mounts, down from ~330px.

**NO share bar — the one place this list deliberately BREAKS with FacetRow.** A bar was built, shipped, seen in place and removed. Six hairlines under six mono names read as a SECOND ranked block rather than a footnote to one: the section competed with the facet columns for attention and pulled the eye down the panel, past the Highlights the panel exists to show. Magnitude lives in the facts column; the ranking carries the rest. The 380px name-cell cap went with it — it existed only to stop the leading mount's bar reading as a horizontal rule at full panel width. Everything else stays shared with the facets (type scale, tabular numbers, hues), which is what still makes the section read as continuous rather than imported.

The general lesson, worth more than the specific pixels: visual consistency with a neighbouring block is achieved by sharing its type scale and alignment, NOT by repeating its strongest graphical device. Repeating the device makes a peer where a subordinate was wanted.

**Picking a mount** dispatches ONE `FOCUS_LENS_SOURCE` action — it drives the SOURCES selection (not an `ADD_FILTER` repo: chip; both narrow the union and the two INTERSECT, so the chip would leave two controls over one scope), switches to the flat list, clears the open fact, and pushes exactly one nav entry so Back undoes the whole move. See [[nav-entry-captures-ways-of-looking]].

**Non-scope**
THREE ticks, not a sparkline: the payload carries three cumulative buckets, not a time series, and a curve through them would invent data. Does not authorize dropping the second top domain elsewhere — the column narrows to one value only because the meter takes its width here. Does not make `repo:` chips dead: the category still exists and still intersects with sources; only the REPOS SECTION stopped minting them. And it does not say share bars are wrong generally — FacetRow keeps its own.
