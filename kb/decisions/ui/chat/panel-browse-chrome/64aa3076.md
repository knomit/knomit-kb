# The chat's side panel renders a fact with Browse's fact view, the one component (FactView): the same fact shows the same thing in both, except that the panel has a × in the band and no retract button; what navigates lands in Browse on that fact and Back returns to the chat

User decision (2026-11-07), in the user's words: "we already have a perfectly functional component that renders facts EXACTLY how we want them... in the right hand panel for chat view, when we have to render a fact, use that component."

So there is no separate chat fact rendering. FactView (web/src/FactView.tsx, extracted whole from RightPanel) is the fact view. Browse mounts it inside RightPanel; the chat's side panel mounts it through PanelFact in ChatSidePanel. What it reads is a FactReading, a Pick of AppState (which fact, from where, at which commit, whether it can be written), and the state selectors (selectAnchorCommit, factHistoryAnchor, edgeAnchorCommit, isReadOnly and the rest) take a FactReading, so both go through the same rules. Browse hands its app state; the panel builds a reading for its fact. Everything else the view can do is a prop. ChatFactView was deleted. A reader sees and can do the same on a fact in both: version, cited by, cites, same motif, materialize, retracted badge, history and anchored reads, tags, references.

THE TWO DIFFERENCES FROM BROWSE IN THE BAND, and the only two. (1) A visible × in the band's action group (bandAction), which Esc also does. (2) No retract button (hideRetract), the user's decision: Browse keeps its retract button. Both are inputs on the one component, not a variant. Consequence: do NOT add a chat rendering, a chat band or a chat copy of any control. A new difference goes through a prop on FactView, and only if the user asks for it. Do NOT bring the user questions about how a fact renders in the panel: it renders as in Browse.

Also around the component, not in it: no path/Open in Browse row, since the path is in the band; no back or forward arrows and no back stack in the panel (the reader selects from the chat; from a fact opened out of a commit, the way back to the commit is its chip in the answer). While there is no band (loading, an error, no version at the commit) bandAction sits in a bar of its own, so × never disappears. The view's own Loading and error lines are Browse's.

What navigates goes to Browse for that fact, and Back returns to the chat: the version; cited by, cites and a motif (onOpenPanel hands the panel over instead of opening it under the band; Browse passes none, so its cells open in place); a tag or origin chip (it lands with that filter on, in one Back step); a reference in the body (Browse on that fact at the edge's pinned commit). A fact read at a commit lands at that commit. A fact read live lands live, except the version, which lands at the newest version as Browse's walker does. Materialize stays in place and uses Browse's view; materializeInUrl={false} keeps its options out of the query string, which Browse reads at mount, so a chat cannot leave Browse opening materialized. Retracted handling is the view's own: an anchored read always uses ?fallback=before, so the panel entry has no `removed` flag, and the badge shows as in Browse.

How the landing works: APPLY_NAV with fromChat stamps the departure entry it pushes (NavEntry.fromChat); NAV_BACK popping it sets state.chatReturn, and App then pins that chat (SELECT_PINNED). One route for the keyboard, the Library's chevron and the timeline's back, because they all dispatch NAV_BACK. The chat stays mounted while Browse shows, so it returns with its scroll and panel as they were. A chat that has since ended is not pinned: the reader stays in Browse on what back restored. selectTrail stops at a fromChat entry, so what Browse held before is not a crumb. Across repos the landing switched Browse's context, which empties navStack, and back leaves it on the new repo with the chat on screen. APPLY_NAV carries factPanel, FactView serves state.factPanelRequest once and clears it (FACT_PANEL_SERVED), or a later return to the fact would reopen the panel.

A test renders one fact through RightPanel and through ChatSidePanel, live, at a commit and retracted at it, and compares the DOM with only the × and the retract button removed: web/src/FactView.shared.test.tsx. It is what fails if the panel stops using the component.
