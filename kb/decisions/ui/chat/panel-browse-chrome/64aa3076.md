# The chat's fact panel wears Browse's own chrome, has no back stack and no Open in Browse, and its controls land in Browse on that fact, at the commit it was read at; Browse's back returns to the chat

User decisions (2026-11-07), made against a screenshot of Browse's band.

The panel has no back stack: no arrows, no ⌘[ hint or key, no 'N of M opened'. The reader selects from the chat. Consequence accepted: from a fact opened out of a commit, the way back to the commit is its chip in the answer. Esc still closes.

The fact view has no header row and no Open in Browse: the path is in the band, and the × sits in the band's right-hand group. A commit or a tool result has no band, so it keeps a header with its crumb and ×. The hover card on a fact link keeps its own Open in Browse: this decision was about the panel.

The fact chrome is Browse's, not a copy. RightPanel's chrome was extracted to FactChrome.tsx: FactPage (the band over the scrolling body, the IntersectionObserver that lets the title flow up into the band), FactEdgesGroup (cited by, cites, motif cells) and FactActions (date, VersionWalker, the retraction badge, materialize, retract). Browse and ChatFactView both render them. The panel passes no onRetract, so retract is absent (the user did not ask for it); 'same motif' came with the shared group and stays, as Browse has it. Do not add a panel-only band: a second copy is how the two came to disagree before.

What each control does in the panel: materialize opens MaterializeView in place, with Browse's control and code, at the commit read, with Browse's default options and without writing its URL parameters (Browse reads those at mount). It takes over the screen in focus mode, as in Browse, and its own back returns to the band. The version (v3) lands in Browse's timeline on that fact; cited by and cites land with that connections panel open; a motif lands with its motif panel open. For a fact read at a commit all of these land at that commit. A fact read live lands live, except the version, which lands at the newest version as Browse's walker does. VersionWalker takes `exact` in the panel at a commit: a commit its list does not hold draws no vN rather than the newest one's.

Back: a landing is a real entry. APPLY_NAV with fromChat stamps the departure entry it pushes (NavEntry.fromChat); NAV_BACK popping it sets state.chatReturn, and App then pins that chat (SELECT_PINNED). This is one route for the keyboard, the Library's chevron and the timeline's back, because they all dispatch NAV_BACK. The chat stays mounted while Browse shows, so it returns with its scroll and panel as they were. A chat that has since ended is not pinned: the reader stays in Browse on what back restored. selectTrail stops at a fromChat entry, so what Browse held before is not a crumb: back from there is the chat. Across repos the landing switched Browse's context, and back leaves it on the new repo with the chat on screen.

One-shot panel request: APPLY_NAV carries factPanel, RightPanel serves state.factPanelRequest once and clears it (FACT_PANEL_SERVED), or a later return to the fact would reopen the panel.

At-commit data, audited against a real repo (conf 0.80 vs 0.90, sources 3 vs 7, cited by 0 vs 4, no motif vs one, at a commit vs HEAD): title, type, kind, origin, confidence, sources, domain, date, text, refs and edge counts are all that commit's. KNOWN LIMITS the server cannot provide: a motif's count ('same motif N') is the corpus at the branch tip, also in Browse; vN comes from the first page of the fact's history (50), so past 50 versions it is counted from that page, also in Browse. Materializing a fact the commit RETRACTED answers 404 in both Browse and the panel: the materialize route takes no fallback, though the view shows the last version.

Does NOT mean the panel can act on Browse's state: it passes a no-op dispatch to the shared components.
