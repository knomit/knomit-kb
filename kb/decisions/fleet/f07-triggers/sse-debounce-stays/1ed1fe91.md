# The SSE/index batcher stays a debounce: the push window's fixed-from-first-fire semantics are NOT retrofitted onto commitObserver

User ruling (2026-09-28, verbatim): "no, SSE it's OK the way it works."

Options considered: change commitObserver (index sync + SSE `status` pings, 1 s, re-armed on every Notify) to the push window's fixed countdown from the first commit, for consistency — REJECTED; keep it a debounce — CHOSEN. The two batchers serve different needs: status pings and index sync are fine coalesced after a burst ends, while the push must go out a bounded time after the first write.

Non-scope: this does not make the push window a debounce; the two intentionally differ (kb/gotchas/repos/observer/debounce).
