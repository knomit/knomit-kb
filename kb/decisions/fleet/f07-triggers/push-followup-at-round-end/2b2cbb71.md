# A push fire that lands while a round runs opens its countdown when that round ENDS (not at the fire's own instant); accepted as merged in #343

User ruling (2026-09-28, verbatim): "343 will be merged as is." — on the worker's stated interpretation of D-window's 'fires that arrive while that round runs start the next countdown'.

Options considered:
- CHOSEN (as merged) — the fire fills the 1-slot channel; the loop, the only reader, sees it when the round returns and opens the countdown then. The follow-up round starts 1 s after the running round ends. Exactly one follow-up, never zero, never N.
- NOT TAKEN — timestamp the first fire during the round and start the follow-up at max(round end, fire + 1 s): slightly lower latency, but needs a timestamped slot instead of a struct{} channel, for sub-second gain.

Tested by TestPush_FollowUpDuringRound (a fire at fake 1.2 s during a parked round → exactly one follow-up after its own countdown).
