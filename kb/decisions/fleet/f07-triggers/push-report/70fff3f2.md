# D-report: a push fire records `kicked` and never reports whether the round succeeded; success/failure stays in the repo's sync/push status

User ruling (2026-09-28, verbatim): "3) agreed" — to 'the fire only asks for a round and is logged as kicked; whether the round succeeded is shown where it is today'.

Options considered:
- CHOSEN — fire-and-forget: outcome `kicked` (counted as a fire), no SSE event, no commit; the round's result lives in last_sync/last_push status, the push_error broadcast, the banner and the log.
- REJECTED — reporting push success on the fire: the dispatcher would have to hold the fire open across the network (which it must never do), and one round may serve many fires from many triggers.

Non-scope: a `kicked` row means 'asked', not 'pushed'.
