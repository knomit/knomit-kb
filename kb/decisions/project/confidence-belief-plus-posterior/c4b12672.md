# Facts carry two numbers: agent-owned confidence (belief) plus system-owned calibrated posterior

Decided 2026-07-04 during the stochastic-target roadmap rethink, immediately after the two-layer (library/librarian) decision (kb/decisions/project/fact-value-two-layer/00577977.md).

Problem: 'calibrated truth' as the corpus layer's promise forces confidence to have real semantics. A calibrated probability must move mechanically (settlement verdicts, verifier votes, confluence, drift are likelihood updates), but roadmap principle 4 says belief is agent-owned — confidence changes only through reasoned, evidence-cited acts.

Options considered:
1. Two numbers (CHOSEN): agent-owned confidence stays reasoned and commit-based (principle 4 intact); the system additionally maintains a system-owned posterior computed mechanically from evidence events (verification votes, settlement outcomes, confluence with ancestry-disjoint support, drift, contradiction mass). Retrieval and the emit layer rank on the posterior. Both numbers are visible; divergence between them is itself a signal and feeds the re-examination queue.
2. One posterior, agents supply evidence only — rejected: repeals principle 4 and makes human authorship awkward (a human's asserted 0.95 would be immediately overruled by the machine).
3. Scalar confidence + corpus-level calibration only (bench reliability curves, review items on miscalibration, no per-fact machinery) — rejected as the primary design because it gives up per-fact calibrated ranking, though corpus-level reliability curves remain in the bench regardless.

Rationale: preserves the belief-agent-owned / state-system-owned principle by classifying the posterior as state (like freshness); confidence-vs-posterior divergence becomes a mechanical re-examination signal no single-number design produces; per-fact posteriors are what the librarian layer needs for ranking.
