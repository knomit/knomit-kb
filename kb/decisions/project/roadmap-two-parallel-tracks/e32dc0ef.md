# Roadmap restructures into two parallel tracks: library (truth) and librarian (effect), librarian starting day one

Decided 2026-07-04, fourth decision of the stochastic-target rethink.

Options considered:
1. Two parallel plants (CHOSEN): Library track = today's Phases 0–4 probabilistically reinterpreted (oracles emit evidence not verdicts; verification/settlement/confluence update the system-owned posterior; compiler discipline kept — it was always about corpus structure and remains right). Librarian track = reader profiles, prior-delta, emission forms, dosage, effect measurement — starting at day one instead of Phase 5; run_ab.py and knomit-bench model mode are its embryonic instrumentation. Convergence: posterior feeds librarian ranking; effect data feeds library priorities.
2. Reinterpret in place (keep six phases, overlay stochastic semantics) — rejected: keeps the librarian sequenced last, quietly re-asserting that effect is a backend concern, contradicting the two-layer decision.
3. Probabilistic reboot (belief-network core, emission as decision theory) — rejected as a structure: discards load-bearing sequencing (graph before posteriors, measurement before tuning) and invites heavy machinery ahead of evidence; its right ideas (posterior engine, evidence framing) land inside the library track.

Key correction this encodes: the original roadmap's real error under the stochastic-target lens was sequencing the entire stochastic-reader problem (emission, effect) LAST — not the compiler discipline itself.
