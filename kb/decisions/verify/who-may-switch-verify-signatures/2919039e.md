# Anyone who can merge to a KB's main may turn verify_signatures on, off or to enforce; there is no operator role and the gate does not police the switch

DECISION (F09 PR 5, D2, user ruling 2026-09-27). The switch is an ordinary ontology edit. Whoever can merge into the knowledge base's main can change it, and the acceptance gate reads the mode at main's tip without asking who set it.

RULING: recorded in the coordinating session's log as "D2 yes (anyone who can merge to main)", accepting the design's recommendation. No fuller verbatim wording of the user's was recorded. It follows from the transitive-trust ruling (kb/decisions/verify/transitive-trust/121a6499.md): repository write access is the trust boundary.

OPTIONS REJECTED:
- An `operator: true` flag on a fleet member record, with every enable or relaxation not made by an operator refused in every mode (the design's non-chosen option; its exact wording in revision 6 was overwritten).
- Earlier: whoever holds an F19 operator certificate; and the original `[verify].operator_key` in knomit.toml as root of trust (retracted with PR 5).

NON-SCOPE: this does not make the switch unguarded locally. A knomit release still may not change it: the boot preset refresh only preserves root attributes (kb/invariants/repos/ontology/refresh-preserves-root-attributes/77ad0332.md).
