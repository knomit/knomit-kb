# A knomit certificate's role is the AUTHORITY of its URI SAN — knomit://instance/<label> or knomit://operator/<label> — not a private X.509 extension, because a 2.25 UUID-arc OID cannot be written or parsed by Go

CONTEXT. F19 revision 2 put the certificate role (instance | operator) and the F13 election capability (`host: true|false`) in a private X.509 extension, first under a placeholder PEN arc and then — by ruling — under a UUID-derived arc of 2.25 (no registration needed).

OPTIONS (decided 2026-09-22, master session, F19 phase 2):
1. Private extension under a 2.25 UUID arc. IMPOSSIBLE in Go, measured on go1.26.6: `pkix.Extension.Id` is `asn1.ObjectIdentifier` = `[]int`, and a v4 UUID's integer is at least 2^76, so the extension cannot be expressed in `ExtraExtensions`. Worse, it cannot be READ either: `crypto/x509/parser.go:233` (`parseExtension`) calls `ReadASN1ObjectIdentifier(&ext.Id)`, which returns false on an arc wider than int, and the parse fails with `x509: malformed extension OID field`. A hand-built certificate carrying one would be unparseable by every Go peer, our own verifier included. `x509.OID` holds big arcs, but only `Certificate.Policies` uses it.
2. Private extension under an IANA Private Enterprise Number. It fits `[]int`, but costs days of latency for one integer.
3. Carry the role in the URI SAN, which `VerifyInstanceChain` already parses and cross-checks. CHOSEN.

SHAPE (as of PR #513): `knomit://<role>/<label>`; the older shape carried `-<fp8>` and is gone. Go's `url.Parse` puts `<role>` in `u.Host` (the URI AUTHORITY), not the first path segment, and `crypto/x509` domain-validates that authority at parse time (which `instance` and `operator` pass). The path is exactly `/<label>`. `<label>` is restricted at issuance to `[A-Za-z0-9._-]` so it never needs escaping, and is opaque: no fp8 and no cross-check (ErrSANMismatch is deleted). Identity.Fingerprint is computed from the leaf's public key; Identity.Host is the label verbatim. The leaf CommonName is the label too. pki.SAN(role, label) lost its fingerprint argument. pki.Issued gained Label (json 'label'), which cmd/oauth_sign issuedStanding returns instead of trimming the SAN.

WHY NOT 'agent': the branch is `agent/<host>-<fp8>`, but the SAN names a ROLE and the branch names a namespace (the branch is agent/<host>-<fp8>, built once at first boot). Reusing the word would invite the misreading that the SAN's path equals the branch name.

CONSEQUENCES. (a) Leaf certificates carry only standard extensions — `TestIssueInstance_LeafChainsToRootWithRoleInSANAndIsLogged` asserts an allow-list (2.5.29.14/15/17/19/35/37), so the non-critical-extension trap from the original plan cannot arise. (b) The F13 `host: true|false` capability is NOT in the certificate at all; it moves to the F10 peers fact, where the operator can change it without re-issuing. (c) A role change means re-issuance (the SAN changes), which matches the intent that a role is a coarse, slow-changing TYPE; permissions are grants rows, never certificate contents.

MISREADING TO AVOID: this does not mean 'the SAN carries permissions'. It carries a type (instance or operator) and a display label. What a principal MAY do is answered by auth.Allowed from grants.
