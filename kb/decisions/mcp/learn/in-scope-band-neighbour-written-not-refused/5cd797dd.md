# A same-subject candidate inside learn's merge scope and below Dedup is WRITTEN with a response note and a `near:` commit line, not refused (user ruling, #638); candidates outside the scope, or at or above Dedup, are still refused

**Context.** #638 raised Dedup (gemma 0.82 to 0.905, nomic 0.92 to 0.95). Same-category siblings in [0.82, 0.905) therefore stopped merging and fell into the same-subject gate, whose band is (ReflectNovelty, Dedup) = (0.69, 0.905) on the shipped model and (0.85, 0.95) on the nomic fallback. Those siblings would have been refused whenever they share a non-generic entity.

**Options.** (a) Refuse them, as the gate does for any in-band candidate. (b) Keep the old 0.82 as the band's upper edge for that case: a second, underived cosine, which violates same-subject-band-endpoints (0c5a0529) and corpus-property-constants (63e6ecca). (c) Write the candidate and tell the caller.

**Chosen: (c), by the user's ruling in #638 review.** The user rejected (a), in their words: "this will mean anything remotely similar will be completely refused, and a lot of back and forth". Raising Dedup from measurement was kept; no new cosine was added.

**Behaviour.** A candidate inside learn's merge scope (inMergeScope: the case-insensitive prefix of the incoming fact's category directory, the same reach as applyDedupMerge's search) and below Dedup is not refused. The fact is written at its own path. The `notes` of the knomit_learn response carries one line per such fact naming the nearest neighbour, its cosine, and "if it states the same claim, fold it into that fact with knomit_update and retract this one" (the new fact is already written when the note is read). The commit message carries one extra line, `near: <path> (0.86)`; it lists each distinct neighbour once, on a single line however many facts have one. The scope reaches descendants and prefix siblings, never the parent directory. Candidates outside the scope (cross-category, including the parent directory), or at or above Dedup but not taken by the merge, are still refused. The bypasses (retract in the same call, distinct_from, citing the candidate in refs) apply first and suppress the note.

**What this does NOT mean.** It is not "the gate is off for siblings": a cross-category pair at the same cosine is refused. A true restatement in [0.82, 0.905) is appended, not merged, and the caller is told; review's judged prune sees it because same-directory pairs co-cluster. Both band edges are still ReflectNovelty and Dedup.

**Known narrowing.** The merge's LIKE treats `_` in a category as a wildcard; inMergeScope treats it literally, so a candidate reachable only through that wildcard is refused, not written. It errs toward refusing; `_` categories are rare.
