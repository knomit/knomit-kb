# A credential change resets the fetch and push sync breakers of affected repos (scoped exception)

Shipped behaviour as of the linking PR (#722); see kb/invariants/repos/sync/breaker-reset/b77c7618.md for the mechanism.

Options considered: accept up to roughly 30 minutes of lag until the open breakers retry on their own; reset breakers on a credential change.

Rationale: the feature's purpose is that replacing a credential takes effect at once with no reconnect; a repo whose breaker opened because the old token failed would otherwise keep ignoring the new one.

Choice: replacing a secret's value, linking or unlinking a repo, and the light pasted-token swap reset the fetch and push circuit breakers of every affected repo and then wake its sync. This is a deliberate, scoped exception to the existing breaker decision in breaker.go; nothing else resets breakers.

Non-scope: not a general license to reset breakers on other events such as renames, expiry changes or URL edits.
