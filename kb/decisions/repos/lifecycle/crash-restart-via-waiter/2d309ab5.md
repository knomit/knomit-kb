# A crashed worker stage restarts as crashed → waiter goroutine waits the life's WaitGroup → drained → backoff timer → reenter, every step checking the generation; the driver itself never waits

Issue #411. Life.Go recovers a panic in a worker goroutine and posts crashed{stage, gen}. handleInternal(crashed): ignore unless the stage's life has that gen and is not already pending; cancel the life, mark pending[stage]=gen, publish (Serve/Sync report Running=false while pending), and start a goroutine that does l.wg.Wait() and posts drained. drained (still pending, machine not terminal) arms time.AfterFunc(CrashBackoff, post reenter). reenter (still pending with that gen) Exits the drained stage and Enters it fresh.

Why a waiter and not a wait in the driver: a crashed stage's sibling goroutines may be parked on lockBranch or a network read; waiting on them in the driver would freeze every event for the repo, including Unmount. Why the generation on every step: an Unmount, rewind or explicit restart between the steps replaces or drops the life, and a late drained/reenter must not touch the new one.

The index job is the exception: it recovers its own panic into an indexDone error (index_state error), so an index panic never restarts the Index stage in a loop. DefaultCrashBackoff is 5 s (class-2 budget, Options.CrashBackoff overrides; tests use 10 ms).
