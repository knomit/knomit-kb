# The engine's posterior is the WEAKEST live support's confidence, cut to W·S/(S+C) only when the contradicting SumProduct mass C is not outweighed by the support mass S — chosen because it lands in the pilot worlds' gold bands for 169 of 230 questions and one support of 0.9 reaches the 0.5 bar

posteriorOf in internal/repos/engine_reason.js: I = the live supports (verdict supports or restates, role evidence or premise, and — since 32096bf5 — no derived fact whose premise is retracted or judged contradicts), minus any synthesis/insight that cites another member of the set (its weight composes through its members). W = min confidence over I. S = Σ confidence×sources over I; C = the same over the live `contradicts` facts. posterior = W when C < S (the contradiction was weighed and outweighed and is named in the answer body), else W·S/(S+C) (at most W/2). Hypotheses, methodology and pragmatic facts never enter.

Why (fix-later item 4 of PR #498): the PR 1 formula S/(S+C+1) left one support of confidence 0.9 at 0.47, under the 0.5 bar, so a single strong support could never answer. Shapes were compared against the gold answer-confidence bands of the expert-scenarios pilot worlds (230 answerable questions, data repo at f696456): weakest-link 169 in band, mean 121, product 75, noisy-OR 51; adding the outweighed-contradiction rule costs 7 (162) because the gold keeps confidence when the contradiction is resolved, which this rule cannot see. The trust bar is the asker's request property, a placeholder until E05 fits it: DefaultReasonTrust = 0.5 in Go (internal/repos/engine.go, applied by ReasonOptions when Trust is outside (0, 1]), read by the script as `run.trust || 0.5` (TRUST in engine_reason.js). There is no symbol named DEFAULT_TRUST. Non-monotonicity: dropping a contradiction or the weakest support can only raise this posterior, so a fact whose judgment failed is never read as unrelated (it blocks EMIT; see the judgments convention).

Pinned by TestReasonJS_PosteriorCalibration (one 0.9 support = 0.9; synthesis over a member adds nothing; outweighed contradiction leaves W; not outweighed gives 0.28 for the fixture). Misreading to avoid: this is not the compiler set's proposal-22 posterior engine and not evidence_weight; it is session-local and evidence_weight is not used in it.
