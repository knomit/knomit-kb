# EMIT files the answer beside the earliest-read live synthesis/insight it rests on, else under the MAJORITY topic of the supports (majority category inside it), trying the next place when a topic refuses; the answer is always a synthesis (origin authored), a lone `restates` writes nothing, and refs cite supports, methodology and rules but never contradicting facts

Placement (placesFor in internal/repos/engine_reason.js, since PR 3): candidate places, best first: (1) each live derived support (synthesis, insight) in read order — the answer refines that line; (2) the majority topic of the supports (ties to the earliest-read) with the majority category inside it; (3) the other topics. writeWithPlaces tries up to 3 in order: a write-path refusal (ValidateFact, the topic's rules, the refs gate) moves to the next; when all refuse, the fact is returned in unwritten_facts and the run ends on a gap naming every refusal. placeOf only places paths in the WRITE repo under its own ontology root (which may be several segments; kb://<own id>/ is local) and under a topic its ontology declares (fix-later 9). Over the pilot worlds' gold answers, derived-first matches 221 of 255 topics against 216 for the majority rule alone.

Type: always synthesis with origin authored (omitting origin on a synthesis means distilled); PR 1's 'one support -> insight' was dropped (an insight connects several trusted facts; the gold types for one-support answers are synthesis or the fact's own type). One fact judged `restates` and nothing else is returned as existing_answer and nothing is written (dedup, not append).

Refs are why we believe it: the live supports, the relevant methodology and the relevant policies. A contradicting fact is named in the body ('Weighed against') and NOT cited: the scenario gold lists contradicted claims as forbidden citations, and a ref is a reason to believe. This departs from E02 §7's list (which includes contradictions in refs) and is a ruling candidate.

The GAP hypothesis uses the same placement over its partial support (live supports, else live relevant evidence, else the first live evidence read), cites them (at most 8), and carries domain and entities from them.
