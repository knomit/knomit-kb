# Fact-chrome panels that do not fit the pane are CLAMPED IN-TREE to the fact pane's rectangle and scroll inside, not portalled with a viewport clamp and not left to be cut off

**Options considered** (after PR #323 fixed the left-edge clip, a right/bottom clip remained: both panels have a fixed width (360 / 420) and height and sit inside the fact view's overflow:hidden ancestors):
1. **Clamp in-tree.** Keep #323's `left:0` anchor on the edgesGroup span. On open and on resize, measure the fact pane's rectangle and set the panel's maxWidth = pane inner right edge − panel left, and maxHeight = pane bottom − panel top. The list region scrolls (overflowY:auto), so the header and the `esc` chip stay visible.
2. **Portal + viewport clamp.** Render into document.body with position:fixed from the trigger's rect and clamp to the viewport. This escapes every overflow:hidden ancestor.
3. **Accept.** Leave the clip. It is only reachable in narrow or short layouts (below ~970px viewport with the list dragged wide).

**Rationale:** the user wants long content to SCROLL rather than be cut off, which rules out (3). (2) moves the panel out of the DOM subtree its behaviour depends on. The `menuRef` hover keep-open and `useDismiss` treat trigger and panel as one region, so both would need re-homing, and the panel would need repositioning on every scroll and resize. The same hazard class is recorded for ancestor-scoped attributes: `data-nodrag` must sit on an ancestor of BOTH trigger and menu (a top-bar rule today, but a portalled panel escapes any such ancestor). (1) keeps all of that intact and needs only a measurement.

**Choice:** (1) clamp in-tree, measured against the fact pane element (the overflow:hidden wrapper in RightPanel's fact view), NOT the viewport. The pane is what clips, and the viewport is larger than the pane whenever the list rail is open.

**Non-scope:** this does not make the panels responsive in general or change their nominal widths. The clamp only ever SHRINKS a panel to fit what is visible. It also does not authorize portalling other in-tree popovers, and it does not decide anything for top-bar menus.
