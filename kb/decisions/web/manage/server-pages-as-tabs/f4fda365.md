# Manage mode puts server-level pages (Overview, Sessions) in a tab strip across the top of the pane; the rail lists only things you own (repositories, lenses); Sessions uses BroadcastIcon, never the lens layers glyph

**Context (2026-09-14):** PR #192 added a Sessions page to Manage and placed it as a second header-less row under Overview, above the REPOSITORIES and LENSES sections. The maintainer: 'the UI looks weird, Overview and Sessions on top of Repositories and Lenses'. The rail was mixing two axes — pages ABOUT the server and lists of things you OWN — in one column, and the only rows without a section header were the two that were not entities. The Sessions glyph was LayersIcon, i.e. the Lenses glyph.

**Options considered (mocked up at the dialog's scale):**
A. Name the top group with a section header ('This server') in the rail's own idiom; smallest change.
B. Server pages as a tab strip across the top of the Manage pane (Overview | Sessions, with a live-count badge); the rail becomes entities only; tabs dim when a repo or lens is selected.
C. Overview stays on top, Sessions pinned as a status footer at the bottom of the rail.

**Choice: B (maintainer).** Rationale: it separates the two navigation axes structurally rather than by label or position, and it is the home for every future server-level page (activity analysis, authentication, jobs) — the maintainer expects several. Cost accepted: the most rework of the three and a second navigation axis in the pane header.

**Glyph: BroadcastIcon** (already in icons.tsx) — clients connected to a server; reads as live without implying a heartbeat. Rejected: BotIcon (says 'agents', wrong for direct HTTP clients), a new pulse glyph (hints at a heartbeat the presence design deliberately lacks), a new plug glyph (prongs merge at 13px).

**Consequence for future work:** a new page about the SERVER (not about one repo or lens) goes in the tab strip, not the rail. A new kind of owned entity goes in the rail as a section with an uppercase header and a + button. Do not put a header-less row in the rail.
