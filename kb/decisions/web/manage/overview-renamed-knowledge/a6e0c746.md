# The Manage landing tab is labelled "Knowledge", not "Overview" — it shows only the knowledge bases (repositories and lenses) and is the one tab that carries the entity rail; internal ids (Selection kind 'overview', repomgr-overview, ManageOverview) keep their names

**Context (2026-09-15):** once Sessions and Logs sat beside it in the Manage tab strip, the maintainer asked whether "Overview" was still the right name, 'since it's showing only the knowledge bases (repos and lenses)'. Its content is the 'Needs attention' list and the 'How things are wired' table, both about repos and lenses, and under kb/decisions/web/manage/rail-only-for-entity-pages it is the only tab beside which the entity rail renders. "Overview" promised a summary of the whole server and delivered one kind of thing.

**Options considered:**
1. Keep "Overview". Rejected: misleading beside server pages.
2. "Knowledge bases". Rejected by the maintainer as too long for a tab.
3. "Libraries". Rejected: "Library" already names the browse-mode fact tree (web/src/Library.tsx, view 'library'), so the same word would mean repos-and-lenses in Manage and the fact tree in browse.
4. "Repositories". Rejected: excludes lenses.
5. "Knowledge". CHOSEN (maintainer: 'Knowledge it is'). It is what the rail lists, it reads naturally in the strip (Knowledge | Sessions | Logs), and inside Manage there is no collision with browse mode.

**Scope of the rename:** the user-visible tab label and the page heading, plus the tab glyph (HomeIcon no longer fits a tab that is not a home). NOT renamed: the Selection kind 'overview', the test ids repomgr-overview / manage-overview, the ManageOverview component and file, and comments that use 'Overview' as the concept of the landing page. Renaming identifiers would churn tests and facts for no user-visible gain.

**Misreading to avoid:** "Knowledge" is the Manage tab for CONFIGURING knowledge bases; the knowledge itself is browsed in browse mode. Do not add facts, stats or search to this tab on the strength of its name (the ManageOverview header comment already forbids statistics there).
