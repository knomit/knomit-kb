# The chat transcript follows a streaming answer only while the reader is at the bottom; a wheel or touch scroll up stops following at once, an up key only once the transcript itself scrolls up, a scrollbar drag past 24px too; nothing moves the view until the reader comes back down, clicks 'Jump to latest ↓', presses End or sends; a chat off screen keeps whichever state it had

Decision (user picked it from a mockup, 2026-11-09; revised after review of PR #812). Before this, ChatPanel set `scrollTop = scrollHeight` on EVERY change to `state.turns` or `state.approvals`, unconditionally, so a reader who scrolled up during a streaming answer was pulled back to the bottom by the next delta.

The rule, all of it:
- FOLLOWING (the start state): after each render that changes turns or approvals, and on any ResizeObserver growth of the transcript, ONE write per animation frame sets scrollTop to the bottom (rAF-coalesced).
- LEAVING is decided by the reader's intent first. These stop following AT ONCE, whatever the distance from the bottom: a wheel event with deltaY < 0 on the transcript, unless ctrlKey is set (pinch-zoom) or |deltaX| > |deltaY| (a sideways swipe over a wide table); a touch drag that moves the content down (the finger moves down by more than 2px).
- An UP KEY (ArrowUp, PageUp, Home, Shift+Space, with focus on the body or inside the chat column and NOT in a text field) does NOT leave on its own. With focus on <body>, for example after clicking plain text in the side panel, the key may scroll some other box. Measured on review: the key alone stopped following, the transcript stayed put and new text streamed out of view under the pill. The key ARMS a leave instead. The transcript's next scroll event confirms it if scrollTop went down by ANY amount, even inside the slack, and a scroll that did not go up does not consume it. The arm lapses after UP_KEY_WAIT_MS = 300ms. While armed, pins are held, because our write would cancel the browser's keyboard scroll; the lapse runs the held pin. End, the pill and a send clear it.
- Two exceptions apply to wheel and touch: the transcript must have something above to scroll to (scrollTop > 0), and a box inside the transcript that would take the scroll itself (the thinking summary, a tall block, anything with overflow-y auto/scroll and scrollTop > 0) means the transcript did not move. A scrollbar drag has no such event, so the fallback is: a scroll event where scrollTop DECREASED by more than 1px while the bottom is more than FOLLOW_SLACK_PX = 24px away.
- Why intent first: while a fast answer streams, a gentle scroll up of less than 24px between frames stayed inside the slack and the next frame re-pinned it, so the reader could never escape (measured in Chrome on the first version: 0 of 3 attempts with 3-8 wheel steps of -10px stopped following; after the change 3 of 3 did, and scrollTop then held while the page grew ~100px).
- While not following, no code writes scrollTop; new output is appended below.
- SCROLL ANCHORING: `overflow-anchor: none` on the transcript WHILE FOLLOWING (we pin the bottom ourselves, and an anchoring adjustment during a reflow such as a window resize could lower scrollTop and read as the reader leaving), `auto` WHILE READING (it keeps the text being read still when something above it changes size: chips resolving, groups collapsing). One source: the transcript's inline style from the `follow` state.
- RE-ARMING by hand needs a move DOWN: a scroll event that lands within 24px of the bottom with scrollTop larger than the last one, or one exactly at the bottom (gap <= 1). A small scroll UP that stays inside the slack does not re-arm.
- The pill `Jump to latest ↓` floats centred over the bottom of the transcript, just above the composer, whenever not following, streaming or not. It uses the composer's raised surface (#171717) with a 1px #2e2e2e border, radius 999, 13px text, shadow 0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.5). Its focus ring is the global `button:focus-visible`, not `.k-bare`, whose :focus-visible sets border-radius 4px.
- Following resumes on: the pill (smooth, instant under prefers-reduced-motion); End outside a text field and inside the chat column; any send (submit, Retry, Continue go through `send`, which jumps instantly).
- ON SCREEN (a chat shown again after another chat, a repo, or its own Settings tab): it keeps the state it had. Following, it lands at the bottom, including what streamed while it was hidden, and keeps following. Reading, it comes back at the same scrollTop (restored from the last one seen, since a hidden box reads 0) with the pill showing. Scroll events while hidden (clientHeight 0) are ignored.
- StrictMode (main.tsx) mounts, cleans up and mounts again with the same refs: the unmount cleanup cancels the queued frame AND resets its handle to 0, or pin() would return early forever. ChatFollow tests run every case plain and under StrictMode.

Consequence: never reintroduce an unconditional scroll-to-bottom in a render effect; route any new 'jump to the newest' through jumpToLatest, any new pointer input that certainly scrolls the transcript through leave(), and any key-like input that MAY scroll something else through armUp(), so the follow flag, the pill and the anchoring stay in step.

Not meant: this is not 'any scroll event away from the bottom stops following' (see the companion gotcha), and not 'coming back on screen always jumps to the bottom' (that was the first version, replaced).
