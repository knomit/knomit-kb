# The chat transcript follows a streaming answer only while the reader is at the bottom; a wheel step up, or a scroll up past 24px, stops following and nothing moves the view until the reader scrolls back down, clicks 'Jump to latest ↓', or sends

Decision (the user's ask, 2026-11-09): while an answer streams, a reader who scrolls up to read keeps their view, new text keeps appending below, and a 'Jump to latest ↓' button takes them back. Before this, ChatPanel set `scrollTop = scrollHeight` on EVERY change to `state.turns` or `state.approvals`, unconditionally, so the next delta pulled the reader back down.

The rule, all of it:
- FOLLOWING (the start state): after each render that changes turns or approvals, and on any ResizeObserver resize of the transcript or its column, ONE write per animation frame sets scrollTop to the bottom (rAF-coalesced). No write while the chat is hidden (clientHeight 0).
- LEAVING: a wheel event with deltaY < 0 on the transcript stops following at once, at any distance from the bottom, provided scrollTop > 0 (something above to scroll to). Any other scroll, such as a scrollbar drag, stops following when scrollTop DECREASED by more than 1px and the bottom is more than FOLLOW_SLACK_PX = 24px away.
- Why the wheel stops following at once: during a fast stream, a gentle scroll up of less than 24px between frames stayed inside the slack and the next frame re-pinned it, so the reader could not escape. In Chrome, before the change, 0 of 3 attempts with -10px wheel steps stopped following; after it, 3 of 3 did.
- While not following, no code writes scrollTop; new output is appended below.
- RESUMING by hand needs a move DOWN: a scroll event within 24px of the bottom with a larger scrollTop than the last one, or one exactly at the bottom (gap <= 1). The scroll a wheel step up makes also lands inside the slack and must not re-arm following, which is why direction matters.
- SCROLL ANCHORING: `overflow-anchor: none` on the transcript while following, and `auto` while reading, both set from the `follow` state in its inline style.
- The pill `Jump to latest ↓` floats centred over the bottom of the transcript, just above the composer, whenever not following. It uses the composer's raised surface (#171717) with a 1px #2e2e2e border, radius 999, 13px text, shadow 0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.5). Its focus ring is the global `button:focus-visible`. Clicking it scrolls to the bottom (smooth, instant under prefers-reduced-motion) and resumes following.
- A send (submit, Retry, Continue) jumps to the bottom and resumes following.
- A HIDDEN chat (display:none, under Settings or another target) keeps the place the browser keeps. Scroll events while clientHeight is 0 are ignored. When the chat is shown again, the ResizeObserver fires and re-pins it if it was following.
- There are NO keyboard, touch or on-screen rules. Keyboard and touch scrolls go through the scroll fallback, and there is no End shortcut.

Consequence: never reintroduce an unconditional scroll-to-bottom in a render effect, and route any 'jump to the newest' through jumpToLatest so the follow flag, the pill and the anchoring stay in step. The traps this guards against are in the companion gotcha.

Not meant: this is not 'any scroll event away from the bottom stops following'. Our own writes and content growth never count.
