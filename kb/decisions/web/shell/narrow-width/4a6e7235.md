# Master ruling on PR #534: the knomit wordmark and branding stay untouched at 375px; the label collapse is fixed by min-width and a scrolling strip, not by hiding the wordmark text

The #534 review offered three options for the collapsed target label: scroll the tab strip and keep a label min-width; hide the 'knomit' word mark text at 375px (keeping the K glyph, which frees ~45px); or shrink tab padding further. The master session chose the first and ruled that the wordmark, branding and visible copy are not changed by a layout fix; the brief for the fix says 'no new visible copy'. This is a master-session ruling, not a stated user preference.

CONSEQUENCE: do not hide or shorten the wordmark to win width at a phone size; to free space in the top bar, tighten gaps and padding or let the strip scroll. A change to branding needs the user to ask for it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the K glyph and wordmark are not forbidden from ever responding to width; the ruling is that a layout bug fix does not decide it.
