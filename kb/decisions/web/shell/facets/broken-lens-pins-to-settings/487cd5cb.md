# A lens with a broken mount is pinned to a Settings page with a callout, the mount editor and Delete (option b), not made a normal target (option a)

Context (PR E review, blocker): a lens with a mount whose repo has no store was unreachable. Its rail row was disabled, the Manage panel that used to list lenses was gone, 'Mounted in' rescued back to a repo, and the server refuses to archive a repo any lens mounts (ErrRepoInUseByLens). So a missing repo mounted by a lens could be neither archived nor unmounted from the UI.

Options: (a) make the broken lens a normal target (enter it as the browse context); (b) pin it over the context to a Settings page that explains the problem and offers the repair. Every read under a broken lens answers 503 (a lens binds all members or none), so (a) fires reads that all fail; (b) is smaller and safer.

Choice: (b), made by the dev-master session during the review and CONFIRMED by the user on 2026-10-12 ('ok'). The rail row is enabled (still disabled while a connect commit is locked), keeps the reason as its title, and calls `onPin({kind:'brokenLens', name})`. The page is `LensSettings` with a `broken` prop: a 'Mounts' problem block with an amber callout naming each missing mount (state and the server's detail), advice to unmount it under Read mounts or restore the store (or, if the write target itself is missing, restore it or delete the lens), then the ordinary mount editor and the Danger zone with Delete. A repaired lens becomes the context and the reader stays on its Settings. facetsFor gives the pinned target Settings only.

Source of the options: the status card's 'Decisions the user may want to revisit' (PR E, reaching a lens with a broken mount) and the PR E verdict, finding 1.

Revisit only if you want a broken lens to be browsable as a normal target (option a). WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it is not that the lens is entered and rescued; a broken lens is never the context.
