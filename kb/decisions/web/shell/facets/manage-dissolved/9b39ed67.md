# Manage is dissolved: per-target facets (Browse, Activity, Settings) with a left rail replace its tab strip and rail split, and server-only pages sit behind the gear

Context: Manage mixed two axes, what is ABOUT a repo or lens and what is ABOUT the server, in one mode with a tab strip (Knowledge, Fleet, Sessions, Logs, Authorizations) and an entity rail beside only the Knowledge tab. Options: (1) keep Manage and refine it; (2) one axis per level: pick a TARGET in the rail, then a facet of it (Browse, Activity, Settings), and move pages about the server behind the gear. Chosen: (2), per the facets spec (.claude/future/ui/facets/README.md) and plan section 6, decision 1.

What went: RepoManager.tsx, ManageOverview.tsx and ManageSessions.tsx (deleted), ManageLogs.tsx (became LogsFacet.tsx), the Knowledge table and the top-bar repo switcher routes. What stayed: ManageFleet.tsx and ManageOAuth.tsx keep their file names; their components are `FleetPage` and `AuthPage`. The repo and lens settings pages are `RepoSettings`, `LensSettings`, `ArchivedSettings`, `UnavailableSettings` and `PendingCreateSettings`, hosted by `SettingsFacet`/`SettingsPage`.

The global sessions view was removed (accepted): Activity shows a target's sessions only.

Consequence: a new page about the SERVER goes behind the gear as a server page; a new page about a repo or lens is a Settings block or a facet. Do not reintroduce a mode that hides the rail and tab strip.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the internal names that still say Manage (ManageFleet.tsx, ManageOAuth.tsx, manageStyles.ts) are leftovers of file naming, not a surviving Manage mode.
