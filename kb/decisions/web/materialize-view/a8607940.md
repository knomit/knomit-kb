# Materialize view (2026-10-29 rulings): icon-only scroll-text button left of retract swaps the fact view; one article, infinite scroll over the server cursor, options in the URL, Export .md is the whole document

User rulings for the materialized-fact view (web/src/MaterializeView.tsx): (1) ENTRY: an icon-only 'scroll-text' button titled 'Materialize' in the fact header's right cluster, LEFT of retract; its glyph (ScrollTextIcon in icons.tsx) must not duplicate the lens glyph (LayersIcon) or the repo glyph (BookIcon). (2) Clicking SWAPS the fact view for the materialized view; a Back control returns. (3) One continuous article; each fact a section whose heading level steps with walk depth (h2 at depth 0, h6 floor); compact meta line (path, confidence, pinned commit); builds_on as inline back-links that scroll to the section; frames: ancestors, then the frame, then what it stands on. Marks: cross-KB = repo badge plus 'latest · frozen'; 'changed since cited' sits on the citing fact's builds-on link; an unpinned walk says NOTHING about a gone target. (4) STREAMING: never fetch everything in one shot. Page 1 at once, next page when the reader nears the bottom (sentinel with a 600px margin), skeleton tail while loading; end states: done (marker with totals), limit reached ('Raise to N and continue'), page failure (inline Retry, sections kept); loading/error/empty for page 1. (5) OPTIONS: Max depth default 4 max 10, Max facts default 200 max 500, Cross-KB, Unpin. Raising ONLY the count is meant to continue in place, but on real data it usually cannot (the walk includes breadth-first and orders depth-first, so a larger cap inserts facts between existing ones) and falls back to a restart that records and restores the reader's place; every other change (including LOWERING the count) restarts from a new first call, old text dimmed until the new page 1 arrives, then scroll back to the section that was at the top, or the nearest earlier one with a notice if it is gone. Options live in the URL (shareable, not persisted). Materialize as of the viewed commit/branch. (6) EXPORT .md downloads the WHOLE document at the chosen options from the agent-document route, never a partial file; the button shows progress and a second click cancels; failure writes no file and offers Retry; saved through web/src/saveFile.ts (desktop webviews do not honor download links); filename <slugged title up to 48 chars at a word boundary>-<id8>.md. (7) At ~900px the controls wrap with no horizontal overflow. Does NOT mean: a cursor call may carry options (the backend refuses them), or that Export uses the scrolled-in sections.

Correction (review of #684): 'Raise to N and continue' therefore behaves as a place-keeping restart in practice. See kb/conventions/web/materialize-view/743e7ddc.md. The first pages call also sends limit=5 (cursor calls send only the cursor).

Label ruling (user, 2026-10-29): the limit-reached action reads "Show up to N", not "Raise to N and continue", because in practice a raise restarts the walk and keeps the reader's place rather than continuing in place.
