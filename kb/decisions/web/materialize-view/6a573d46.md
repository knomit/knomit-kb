# Materialize polish (user, 2026-10-29): full width, outline section numbers, collapsed Builds on / Sources chips, no MATERIALIZED label, button reads Export, opt-in Focus mode

Six rulings on the materialize view after first use. (1) RESPONSIVE, FULL WIDTH: the article and its pane carry no max-width and no centring margin (the 780px cap is gone, as #678 removed Settings' cap); long lines are accepted. (2) SECTION NUMBERS read like document sections, from the walk TREE: the root is the unnumbered top (chosen over '0': the document title already says it is the top); its children are 1, 2, 3, their children 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, and so on. A fact reached through several parents is written once and keeps its FIRST-visit number; the other parents' builds-on links show that number. FRAMES: the ancestors are an unnumbered preamble (the group label 'Ancestors'), the frame is the unnumbered top, and numbers start at the frame's children. Heading level still follows walk depth (node.depth), not the number's depth. A forward link to a section that has not streamed in yet shows no number until it arrives. (3) 'Builds on · N' and 'Sources · N' are small chip buttons (aria-expanded), CLOSED by default and not remembered; clicking expands the list in place below the text. A closed Builds-on chip carries an amber dot when any cited version changed since cited. (4) The 'MATERIALIZED' badge is gone. (5) The button reads 'Export' (no '.md'; a menu with PDF may come later). (6) FOCUS (an experiment the user wants to SEE before deciding): a 'Focus' toggle in the materialize bar, off by default, hides the shell's repositories rail, the library column with its splitter and the browse toolbar (branch + search); Esc, the toggle and Back end it. The app's top bar with the brand and facet tabs stays. It may become the default if the user approves. Does NOT mean: the numbers equal the ordinals n (they are tree positions), or Focus is remembered.

Round 2 (user, 2026-10-29), amending rulings (2), (3) and (5)/(6): (a) Builds on / Sources go back to the pre-PR label treatment (small uppercase mono text, no border or chip) but stay collapsible buttons (aria-expanded, closed by default, not remembered) with a disclosure arrow (a chevron that points right when closed and down when open) and the count; the amber changed-since-cited dot stays on the closed Builds-on label. (b) The section number moves OUT of the meta line and INTO the heading as a prefix: "1. Title" for a top-level section, "1.1 Title" below it; the root and a frame's ancestors stay unnumbered; heading level still follows depth. A builds-on link names its target the same way ("1.1 Title"). (c) Focus and Export are icon-only buttons in the fact header's icon style (bare, 2px padding, 15px glyph, dimmed until hovered, k-bare focus ring) with tooltip and aria-label "Focus" / "Exit focus" and "Export"; busy Export swaps the glyph for the app's own spinning arc (the one the search box uses), the tooltip carries the KB so far and its aria-label becomes "Cancel export"; a second click still cancels.

Round 3 (user, 2026-10-29): (a) In Focus the app's top bar stays (brand, repo name, gear, server tabs) but the Browse / Activity / Settings facet tabs (and the divider before them) are hidden, since they are not things to act on while reading; Esc, the toggle and Back restore them (the hiding rule keys on data-mat-focus, which all three clear). (b) Builds-on links show NO section number, neither prefix nor suffix: only the target's title (and its short id); numbers appear in the section headings only. This also settles forward links to sections not yet streamed in: there is nothing to wait for. Still open with the user: Focus as the default, and Esc while a text input has focus.
