# Materialize view polish, FINAL rulings (user, 2026-10-29): full width, outline numbers in the heading, collapsible Builds on / Sources labels, icon-only Focus and Export, Focus ON by default, bare back chevron

The materialize view's rulings after first use, as merged in #687 (these are the FINAL rules; earlier rounds were revised, see the last paragraph).

1. FULL WIDTH: the article and its pane carry no max-width and no centring margin; long lines are accepted.
2. SECTION NUMBERS, in the HEADING only, as a prefix: "1. Title" for a top-level section, "1.1 Title" below it. The root fact and a frame's ancestors are unnumbered; for a frame the ancestors are an unnumbered preamble, the frame is the unnumbered top, and numbering starts at the frame's children. The number is the position in the walk TREE (see conventions/web/materialize-view/63b76f58): a fact reached through several parents keeps its first-visit number. Heading level still follows walk depth (node.depth), not the number's depth. Nothing else shows a number: not the meta line, and NO builds-on link (a link shows only its target's title and short id).
3. BUILDS ON · N and SOURCES · N are small uppercase mono labels (no border, no chip), implemented as buttons with aria-expanded, CLOSED by default, not remembered, with a chevron that points right when closed and down when open; clicking expands the list in place below the text. A closed Builds-on label carries an amber dot when any cited version changed since cited.
4. No 'MATERIALIZED' label in the bar.
5. BACK is a bare chevron (no text, no border): the same markup as LibraryHeader's library-back and TimelineNav's timeline-back (ChevronLeftIcon, 13px, #888, 20x26); aria-label and tooltip 'Back to fact'. It leaves Materialize and restores everything.
6. FOCUS and EXPORT are icon-only buttons in the fact header's icon style (bare, 2px padding, 15px glyph, dimmed until hovered), together in one action group with Focus LEFT of Export. Tooltips and aria-labels: 'Focus' / 'Exit focus' and 'Export'. A busy Export swaps its glyph for the app's own spinning arc, the tooltip carries the KB so far, the aria-label becomes 'Cancel export', and a second click cancels (never a partial file).
7. FOCUS is ON by default: opening Materialize enters it and it takes over the screen. The app shell's repositories rail, the library column with its splitter, the browse toolbar (branch + search) and the Browse/Activity/Settings tabs are hidden; the app's top bar (brand, repo name, gear, server tabs) stays. With Focus OFF NOTHING is hidden: the view only replaces the right-hand panel. The hiding is one stylesheet rule keyed on data-mat-focus on <html>, which the toggle, Esc, Back and leaving the view all clear. Focus is not in the URL options and is not persisted (a reading preference, not what is materialized).
8. ESC: while Focus is on, Esc never reaches the shell (its Escape clears the filters, which would close the open fact and the view). Pressed while a text, number or any other input (or textarea/select/contenteditable) has focus, it only blurs that field and keeps Focus; pressed anywhere else it exits Focus. As merged, EVERY input counts as a field, checkboxes (the Cross-KB and Unpin switches) included.
9. Count raise button reads 'Show up to N' (in practice a raise restarts the walk and keeps the reader's place; see conventions/web/materialize-view/743e7ddc). Export is the whole document, saved under the server's Content-Disposition name.

SUPERSEDED, do not act on these: the 780px centred article; section numbers in the meta line ('§1') or on builds-on links; Builds on / Sources as bordered chips; the '< Fact' text back button; Export reading 'Export .md' and text-labelled Focus/Export buttons; Focus OFF by default or placed right of Export; Esc always exiting Focus even inside a field. Does NOT mean: numbers equal ordinals n (they are tree positions), or Focus hides the whole shell when it is off.
