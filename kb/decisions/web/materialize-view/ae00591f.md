# Materialize tuning (user, 2026-10-29): 'more' replaces Go deeper, floating back-to-top, no restore banner, Export asks where to save, rail toggle inert in Focus, clamped steppers without 'of N', numbers only to three levels with a guide line below

Rulings on top of the polish round (6a573d46). (1) The end-of-article 'Go deeper' button reads 'more': no border, the app's quiet text action (the facet panel's '+N more': small, muted #5f6a7c, brighter on hover). A floating up-arrow at the right of the article ('Back to top', tooltip + aria-label) appears once the pane is scrolled past 600px and scrolls it to the top. (2) The amber banner 'X is not in this walk any more. Showing the nearest earlier section...' is REMOVED (the restore still lands on the nearest earlier section, silently): the content's own markers say what matters. (3) EXPORT MUST ASK WHERE TO SAVE, never save to a place the app picked: desktop uses the native save dialog via NativeService.SaveFileAs (suggested name, user-chosen path, returns '' on cancel); Chrome/Edge use showSaveFilePicker (suggestedName); a cancelled dialog writes nothing and shows nothing. Safari/Firefox have no picker: the first click says 'This browser cannot ask where to save. The file will go to your browser's download location.' with Download / Cancel BEFORE anything is fetched (not silent; the least-bad option, since a plain download is the only thing those browsers allow). (4) With Focus on, the top bar's rail toggle stays visible but is inert (disabled + aria-disabled, no click, not focusable; its pressed state is untouched), and Cmd/Ctrl+B does nothing; outside Focus it works as before. (5) Max facts and Max depth lose their 'of 500' / 'of 10' text and are clamped to the server's range (depth 0..10, facts 1..500; materialize.Options.Validate): typing clamps as you type, blur and Enter clamp and commit, the steppers stop (disabled) at the ends, and nothing out of range reaches the server. (6) DEEP NUMBERS, option A: only the first three outline levels are numbered ('1.', '1.1', '1.1.1'); deeper sections show NO number but a thin vertical guide line in the muted line colour (#2a3040), one 14px indent step per level beyond three, and the heading's tooltip carries the full position ('1.2.1.3.2'). The full number is still computed internally (numberSections) and never changes as pages append. The root, a frame's ancestors and every builds-on link stay unnumbered. Does NOT mean: Focus hides the rail toggle (it only disables it).
