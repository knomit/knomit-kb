# Materialize tuning (user, 2026-10-29), FINAL: 'more' and back-to-top, no restore banner, Export asks only on the desktop (native dialog in Downloads) and downloads in browsers, rail toggle inert in Focus, clamped steppers, and nesting by TREE depth (ladder, 44px tick gutter, sticky path, no indentation)

Rulings on top of the polish round (6a573d46). These are the FINAL rules; what they replaced is named at the end.

1. END OF ARTICLE: the 'Go deeper' button reads 'more': no border, the app's quiet text action (small, muted #5f6a7c, brighter on hover). A floating up-arrow at the right of the article ('Back to top', tooltip + aria-label) appears once the pane is scrolled past 600px and scrolls it to the top.
2. The amber banner '... is not in this walk any more. Showing the nearest earlier section ...' is REMOVED (the restore still lands on the nearest earlier section, silently).
3. EXPORT: the DESKTOP app ALWAYS asks, with the OS native save dialog (NativeService.SaveFileAs), opened in the user's Downloads folder (paths.DownloadsDir: ~/Downloads on macOS, the Downloads known folder on Windows, XDG_DOWNLOAD_DIR or ~/Downloads on Linux) with the suggested file name; cancelling writes nothing and shows nothing; the app never writes into its own folder or exports directory for Export. BROWSERS do a plain download into the browser's configured download location, identically in every browser: NO showSaveFilePicker, no 'Save as' step, no 'this browser cannot ask' confirmation. Export is the whole document, never partial.
4. With Focus on, the top bar's rail toggle stays visible but inert (disabled + aria-disabled, not focusable; pressed state untouched). Cmd/Ctrl+B in Focus is still OPEN with the user (currently ignored).
5. Max facts and Max depth lose 'of 500' / 'of 10' and are clamped to the server's range (depth 0..10, facts 1..500): typing clamps as you type, blur and Enter clamp and commit, the steppers stop at the ends. Esc in a stepper field keeps what was typed (the user confirmed).
6. NESTING follows the walk TREE depth, never the payload's `depth` (BFS distance; see gotchas/web/materialize-view tree-depth): NO indentation at any depth (every section starts at the same left edge). Heading ladder, level = tree depth + 1, levels past 5 repeat L5: L1 24px bold, L2 19px, L3 15.5px bold, L4 14px small caps, L5 13.5px italic muted; body stays 14px. A fixed 44px depth gutter (34px under 1100px) shows up to 6 stacked ticks, one per tree level; a deeper section shows 6 with the last in amber. A sticky 28px section path at the top of the article names the section at the top of the viewport (titles clipped to 30, whole path middle-truncated to 190 chars and the pane width, keeping the first ancestor; items click to scroll; works with Focus on and off). Numbers only for tree depths 1-3 ("1.", "1.1", "1.1.1"); every non-root heading's tooltip says 'Position 1.2.1.3.2'; builds-on links stay unnumbered; no breadcrumbs. Prototype: variant C at its defaults.
7. ESC while Focus is on: only text and number fields (and textarea/contenteditable) hold it (it blurs the field and keeps Focus); a checkbox, radio, button, range or select does not, so Esc right after clicking Cross-KB / Unpin exits Focus at once.

SUPERSEDED, do not act on: the showSaveFilePicker path and its 'Save as...' stale-click step and the Safari/Firefox confirmation; the indented guide-line for sections deeper than 3; every input counting as a field for Esc.
