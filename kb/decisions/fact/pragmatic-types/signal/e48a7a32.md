# `signal` is a pragmatic LEAF TYPE, not a new kind — coordination content that is consumed, never weighed

Coordination content (tasks, messages, acks, membership claims, leases) travels as facts because facts are what this system moves, not because it is knowledge. `signal` is the leaf type that says so. It sits under the PRAGMATIC kind alongside policy and heuristic; it is NOT a third Kind, and Kind.AllowsType remains the only switch that decides which leaf types a kind admits.

Consequences a consumer must act on:

- A signal is CONSUMED once. Its `confidence` is meaningless — never weigh it as evidence, never let it raise or lower anything else's confidence.
- Signals never seed the review pipeline. This is inherited, not added: the seed filter excludes by KIND, so every pragmatic type is already out. Nothing in F01 adds a type-level exclusion, and nothing should.
- The verb is the TOPIC (`tasks`, `inbox`, `acks`, `peers`, `missions`, `leader`), never a sub-type. There is deliberately no message/claim/ack type; adding one is the mistake this single type exists to prevent.
- The addressee is the PATH (`inbox/<slug>/`). The sender is the commit author. The thread is `refs`. The task identity is an ENTITY — which is what keeps the entity-anchored same-subject stage from ever anchoring on a signal.

What this does NOT mean: it does not mean signals are exempt from dedup. Dedup still applies to them until the opt-out ships separately (F02), so F01 alone must not be pointed at a live coordination repo. It also does not mean pragmatic facts gained an expiry or a retention rule — there is none.

Why a type and not a kind: a new Kind would have to be threaded through every consumer that switches on kind, and the merge types in the synthesis output path (mergedFact, distillFact) carry no kind at all. Reusing the pragmatic kind inherits the exclusion those types' kind-blindness makes necessary, at the cost of one map entry.
