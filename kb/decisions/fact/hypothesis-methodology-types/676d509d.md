# hypothesis and methodology types are INFERRED (the code's word; earlier called 'fabricated') — distinct from the 8 observed/derived types, and written by agents through knomit_learn, not only by the pipeline

Two new epistemic types: 'hypothesis' (a prediction derived from synthesis facts; carries inherent uncertainty) and 'methodology' (a reasoning-process lesson from hypothesis outcomes, lives in kb/meta/reasoning/). Both are FABRICATED — distinct from the 8 observed/derived types (observation, concept, process, principle, pattern, reference, synthesis, insight — insight was added in PR #70, between synthesis and hypothesis) which represent observed reality or sound derivations from it. CRITICAL: knomit_learn batch type validation REJECTS batches mixing observed types with fabricated types in a single call — a single cognitive operation cannot span both (insight counts as observed for this check). TYPE SAFETY INVARIANTS: (1) hypotheses must NOT be evidence for other hypotheses; (2) distill must NOT synthesize from hypotheses; (3) RAPTOR ScopedCluster EXCLUDES hypothesis-type facts via ExcludeTypes; (4) distill of methodology-only inputs MUST preserve type=methodology; (5) confidence inherits on promotion, NOT recomputed from scratch; (6) embeddings are type-blind (title+body only) so dedup MUST be type-aware to compensate; (7) Upsert's default type='observation' MUST NOT silently downgrade hypothesis/methodology; (8) review dedup pairs: observations ALWAYS win over hypotheses regardless of confidence.

Updated 2026-10-12 (drift sweep against HEAD 2104fd6d): the code's own word for these two types is INFERRED, not 'fabricated'. `validateBatchTypeConsistency` (internal/edge/mcp/learn.go:216-241) names the groups 'observed' (everything else, including insight and synthesis) and 'inferred' (hypothesis, methodology), and rejects a knomit_learn batch that mixes them with 'cannot mix observed types (observation, concept, etc.) and inferred types (hypothesis, methodology) in a single learn call'; its stated reason is provenance ambiguity: observed facts record something the agent saw, inferred facts something it reasoned to. Read 'fabricated' above as 'inferred'. WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it does not mean hypothesis or methodology facts are pipeline-only or machine-only. They go through the ordinary knomit_learn path, an agent may write one by hand (the knomit-remember skill's type=hypothesis does), and the only restriction is that a single call cannot mix them with observed types; origin (authored, distilled, discovered) is a separate axis. Also re-checked at HEAD: review dedup still makes a non-hypothesis fact win over a hypothesis regardless of confidence, and a hypothesis loser never counts as corroboration (internal/corpus/synthesize/dedup.go:56-96). Not re-verified in this sweep: invariants (1)-(2), (4)-(7) in the list above; treat them as unconfirmed against HEAD until /knomit-harden covers them.
