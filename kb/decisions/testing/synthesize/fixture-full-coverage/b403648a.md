# Decided (knomit#298, 2026-09-26, PR #304): the synthesize test fixture seeds its title-vector axis to FULL coverage with an unexpirable budget and asserts it, rather than raising titleBackfillBudget, making partial coverage an error in production, or retrying the assertion; a new test pins that production keeps returning partial coverage without error

**Context.** TestStructural_RareTokenStaysClosed failed once on a slow windows-2025 runner with `actual: ""` because seedShortlist ran the real backfill under the production 15 s latency budget and the pair under test sat in the last batch (kb/gotchas/testing/synthesize/structural-fixture-title-backfill-budget). 41 call sites of seedShortlist and one direct two-batch caller were exposed.

**Options.** (a) Raise titleBackfillBudget: it is a resource constant about one review session's latency, documented as such (restatement.go:31-39); a test's needs are not a reason to change what production spends. (b) Make partial coverage an error in ensureTitleVectors: reverses a deliberate production contract (partial is reported in the health output and finished by later sessions) to serve a fixture. (c) Retry or widen the assertion in the failing test: hides the class in one test and leaves the other 40 call sites exposed. (d) Loop the fixture until coverage is complete and assert it: CHOSEN, with `time.Hour` as the budget (the loop exits when nothing is missing; livelock is unreachable because PutTitleVectors writes the key the missing-query checks and every failure returns an error), a `require.NotNil(emb)` guard (a fixture with no embedder would pass have == total == 0 over an empty axis), and the same switch in the one direct two-batch caller.

**The guard against re-litigating.** `TestEnsureTitleVectors_ExpiredBudgetReturnsPartialCoverageWithoutError` (budget 0: no batch runs, coverage reported as it stood, no error) exists so that option (b) fails a test if anyone tries it later. It is the deterministic half of TestEnsureTitleVectors_StopsAtTheLatencyBudget's 'partial is not an error' assertion, not a replacement for that test (which also asserts progress and that later sessions finish).

**What this does NOT mean.** A `title backfill incomplete (have/total)` failure is not a bug in the fixture: it is the runner being too slow to embed the corpus in an hour, or the store failing to persist, and the message says which counts to look at. It does not mean every direct caller of ensureTitleVectors in tests must use time.Hour: one-batch fixtures cannot lose facts to a stall and were left alone knowingly.
