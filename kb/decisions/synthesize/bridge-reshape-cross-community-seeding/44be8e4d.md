# Reshape oversized bridge token-groups by cross-community SIMILAR_TO seeding, then cohesion growth

Decision for the Phase-4 general-case bridge generator.

**Options considered:** for a token carrying more than maxMembers facts, (a) emit the whole group (status quo — produces grab-bags); (b) drop oversized tokens entirely; (c) extract the densest cohesive subset by a naive greedy 'grow from the densest neighbourhood'; (d) extract the cohesive subset by SEEDING from the most-similar CROSS-community SIMILAR_TO pair, then growing by the fact that best preserves cohesion while keeping sep>=2.

**Rationale:** a naive densest-subset (c) collapses *inside* one cluster, because Louvain groups by exactly that similarity — the densest neighbourhoods are intra-community, defeating the bridge's whole purpose. Cross-community seeding (d) enforces sep>=2 by construction while still maximizing cohesion. On the trunk stress corpus (793 facts, 37 clusters) this rescued all 12 baseline grab-bags into tight cross-community cohesive subsets with 0 dropped; cross-community seeding was the essential refinement.

**The choice:** (d). Cohesion-gated reshape with cross-community SIMILAR_TO seeding; one best subset per token (multi-subset deferred). Deterministic tie-breaks on path.
