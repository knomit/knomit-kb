# Dedup merge tiebreak: non-hypothesis always wins; then confidence; then sources. Union domains+entities

mergeFacts (dedup.go) tie-breaks merge pairs in this strict order: (1) non-hypothesis ALWAYS wins over hypothesis regardless of confidence (hypotheses carry uncertainty, can't beat observed/derived facts); (2) within same category: higher Confidence wins; (3) Confidence tie: higher Sources wins. After winner is chosen: Domain and Entities are merged as UNION via fact.UnionStrings (dedupes and orders); Sources = a.Sources + b.Sources (sum both). applyGreedyMerges sorts candidate pairs by similarity DESC and enforces 'each fact participates in AT MOST ONE merge per pass' — prevents transitive merges within a single dedup round (transitivity would require re-running dedup until fixpoint, which we explicitly don't). defaultDedupThreshold = 0.92. Anti-pattern: implementing your own merge ordering somewhere — always go through mergeFacts.
