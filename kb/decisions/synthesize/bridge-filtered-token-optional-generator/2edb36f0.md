# Filtered-case bridges: full token-optional generator via greedy seed-and-grow over the scoped SIMILAR_TO graph

Decision (2026-06-27, via AskUserQuestion) for Phase 5 of the bridge-quality mechanism — how the filtered (scope-active) discovery case generates bridges.

**Options considered:** (a) FULL token-optional generator — when a domain/entity scope is active, thread the ScopeFilter into the builder and enumerate cross-community cohesive sets DIRECTLY from the SIMILAR_TO graph over the scoped pool (greedy seed-and-grow reusing the Phase-4 reshape machinery: seed the densest cross-community SIMILAR_TO pair, grow by cohesion to maxMembers, remove used members, repeat until no cross-community pair remains); token-optional (shared sub-token = label, else the scope is the label); within-scope spec; Item-2 scope-token exclusion; a token-optional renderDiscoverPrompt variant. (b) LIGHTER — keep the token-anchored builder when scoped, add only Item-2 exclusion + within-scope spec, no semantic-first generator, no prompt variant. (c) PAUSE Phase 5.

**Rationale:** chose (a). It is the designed intent in the mechanism spec and fills the real gap (b) leaves: scoped pools where related facts span sub-communities but share NO single token surface zero bridges under token-anchored enumeration. Reusing the Phase-4 reshape seed-and-grow keeps one cohesion-extraction algorithm and stays deterministic. Cost: scope must be threaded into the builder (today it is applied only upstream to filter the seed pool), BridgeSeedSet.Token becomes optional, DiscoverWorkPayload gains a scope label, and renderDiscoverPrompt needs a token-optional variant.

**The choice:** full token-optional filtered generator (option a). ~5 TDD tasks (Phase 5, Tasks 17-21).
