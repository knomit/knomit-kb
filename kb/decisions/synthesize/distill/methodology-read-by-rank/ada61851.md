# Methodology candidates are read BY RANK, not by score threshold — the top-ranked one always, the rest triaged on title, with no numeric literal in any prompt

Retrieval already filters methodology candidates at the per-repo `methodology_min_score` (default 0.15) and caps the merged list at `methodologyTopK = 3`. Until PR #188 every prompt then re-thresholded that same list at a fixed `score ≥ 0.50` — a corpus-property constant living in prompt text, which `anti-patterns/corpus-property-constants` forbids.

It was measurably miscalibrated. Across 15 distill items in one review session the observed candidate scores ran 0.54, 0.47, 0.46, 0.41, 0.37, 0.36, 0.34, 0.33, 0.28, 0.27, 0.26, 0.25, 0.24, 0.22, 0.20, 0.19, 0.16 — EXACTLY ONE candidate in 15 items cleared the bar. The one that did changed its synthesis materially, from a share-flavoured headline into an existence claim carrying an explicit "establishes nothing about prevalence" caveat.

THE SHIPPED RULE, at all four sites: read the TOP-RANKED candidate unconditionally, before producing the synthesis — its title is not enough to judge whether it applies. For the remaining candidates, a title is enough to decide whether to OPEN one, and never enough to decide whether it APPLIES: open any whose title suggests it bears on the work, and judge only after reading.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. It is not "read every candidate". That was the first draft and it was rejected as the mirror of the bug being fixed: the 0.50 literal UNDER-read (1 forced read per 15 items), a universal mandate OVER-reads (up to `methodologyTopK` per item, on distill AND reflect AND hypothesize). The rank rule sits between them and costs one forced read per item. It is also not a licence to judge the remainder from titles — that distinction between TRIAGE and JUDGMENT is the load-bearing half, and it is pinned by a test.

FOUR SITES, THREE PROMPTS, because the literal was duplicated: `distill_user.txt`, `reflect_user.txt`, and `hypothesize_strategy.go` twice (workflow step 2 and the section header). The reflect path re-thresholds the same already-filtered list for identical reasons — it uses the same `MethodologyMinScore()` and the same `methodologyTopK` as distill — so scoping a fix to `internal/synthesize`'s distill path alone leaves the corpus lying in two other places. Three further stale references survived in `internal/store`, including non-test source.

ENFORCED BY: `methodology_load_test.go` pins `NotContains "≥ 0.50"` plus `Contains "Read the top-ranked candidate"`, `"Always — its title is not enough"`, and `"never enough to decide whether it APPLIES"`, at both the distill and reflect sites. Those assertions replaced an older pin on the bare word `"EVERY candidate"` whose message recorded the ORIGINAL decision — that letting the model choose whether to read at all is the failure mode. That rationale exists nowhere else, so the assertions were rewritten rather than deleted; the forcing language survives, and only its scope narrowed.
