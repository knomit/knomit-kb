# Distill is ADDITIVE over a cluster of observations and zero retractions is the normal outcome — the retract limb's one live case is a member whose provenance lives in its refs, not its body

Distill's retract limb produced ZERO retractions across 8 syntheses in two full review sessions. The cause was a rule that could not be satisfied: fidelity required carrying every compound condition and caveat from every input, and retraction was permitted only when the synthesis "preserves ALL of its conditions and caveats". At 3–6 members that pair is unsatisfiable, so distill could only ever grow the corpus.

TWO THINGS WERE FIXED, and they are different. First, conditions now split by what a consumer would act on: LOAD-BEARING detail (compound preconditions, scope qualifiers, "WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN" clauses) is carried verbatim because it exists to block a known misreading; PROVENANCE detail (dates, versions, CVE numbers, affected-product lists, measurements) must NOT be copied — the synthesis is the claim and the members are its evidence, reachable through refs.

Second, the retract limb was given a REACHABLE case. The first attempt merely renamed the problem: "retract only a member with no unique provenance detail" is entailed by the fidelity rule above it — if the synthesis may not copy provenance, then every member carrying a date or source has unique provenance by construction, and nearly every real fact carries one. The exemption swallowed the rule.

THE LIVE CASE, as shipped: retract a member only when the new synthesis restates its WHOLE claim AND the member's provenance is carried by its REFS rather than its BODY — typically a prior synthesis or a digest. Carry that member's refs into the new synthesis's refs, so retracting it orphans nothing. A member whose BODY carries its own measurement, date, source, or affected list stays.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. Near-total non-retraction over a cluster of observations is INTENDED, not a defect still to be fixed: distill is additive there, and duplicate load is prune's job. The prompt states that zero retractions is normal precisely so the model does not read its own zero-retraction record as under-performance and start retracting things it should not. Do not "improve" the rate.

SEPARATE AND TAKING PRECEDENCE: a hypothesis confirmed by the batch is retracted even though it is not a restatement, because that is a TYPE TRANSITION rather than subsumption. This carve-out is load-bearing, not cosmetic — a strict "ONLY when it is a restatement" clause silently forbids what the hypothesis-handling section mandates, and an unretracted confirmed hypothesis stays typed as an unconfirmed prediction, which the same prompt then bars from being an input to any later distill. The fact goes permanently inert.
