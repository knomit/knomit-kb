# Motif bridging activates per corpus on a COMPUTED statistical-validity floor of 3 recurring clusters — the original coverage condition was measurably falsified

**The decision.** A corpus enumerates motif bridges only once it carries ≥ 3 RECURRING CANONICAL MOTIF CLUSTERS in the post-`AcceptSeed` BRIDGING POPULATION (`motifActivationFloor = 3`). Computed every session from current counts; there is no config setting, and its absence is grep-able (`grep -riE "motif.*activat|activat.*motif" internal/config/` → 0 hits).

**WHAT THE FLOOR COUNTS — four components, and dropping any one inverts the meaning.**
1. **CLUSTERS, not motifs and not facts.** Canonical clusters after alias resolution.
2. **RECURRING (df ≥ 2), not volume.** A corpus with 500 one-off motifs is below the floor. Recurrence is the whole signal; a hapax names nothing shared.
3. **CANONICAL.** Two spellings of one regularity on one fact are ONE carrier, not two.
4. **The post-`AcceptSeed` SEED POOL, not authored facts.** `AcceptSeed` filters to epistemic, and a quarter to a half of every corpus's motifed facts are pragmatic and can never bridge.

Each of (2), (3) and (4) is pinned by its own test, because each was a way to get the count wrong.

**Options considered.** (1) The blueprint's ≈30% motif-coverage condition — REJECTED, measurably falsified: coverage is a backfill rate limit, and the only research corpus clearing 30% is the one the annex names as its NEGATIVE result. (2) A configured per-corpus switch — rejected as a setting nobody can calibrate. (3) A computed population floor — CHOSEN.

**Why a POPULATION and not a proportion — and why this is legal under MN13.** MN13 forbids corpus-property constants (absolute cosines, expected rates) as fixed values. This is declared as a THIRD class: a statistical-validity floor. It asserts nothing about any corpus's distribution — it is a minimum count below which there are too few shared-motif pairs IN EXISTENCE for the axis to do anything but spend slots on noise. Below it the mechanism DOES NOTHING, which is what distinguishes this class from a threshold someone picked.

**Level-triggered, so the asymmetry favours caution.** A corpus below the floor is not switched off, it is NOT YET ON, and it turns itself on as recurrence accumulates — no flag, no migration, no user action. Too high costs a delay the corpus resolves by itself; too low spends slots on a vocabulary that cannot support them. Below the floor it enumerates nothing AND costs zero index calls — the resolver is already built and the seed pool already in hand.

**Two honesty guards, both load-bearing.** (1) `motifActivation.Evaluated` — a motif-free corpus short-circuits BEFORE the floor is consulted, so "never asked" and "asked, below the floor" would otherwise be the same struct and the health line would call a motif-free corpus *inactive*. (2) A degenerate-cut strict fallback, closing a dead band where the disjointness gate was silently off on hapax-dominated young corpora.

**The cost this floor imposes on its own measurement — disclose whenever citing activation.** K=3 suppresses knomit-kb's one far bridge (`configure-what-varies`), verified with a throwaway K=1 build. The gate working as designed, and the reason the far lane's n is 2 rather than 3. The phase measured a population, set a floor on it, then judged the population — in that order — and had to be asked what the floor removed from the thing being judged.

**The correction that nearly went the other way:** counts were first taken over AUTHORED facts (component 4 above). Counts fell 6→4, 9→6, 2→1, 2→0, moving the candidate range from 3…6 to 2…4. Had it not been caught, K would have been set on a population the axis cannot act on.
