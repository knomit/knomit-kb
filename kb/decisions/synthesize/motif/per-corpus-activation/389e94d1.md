# Motif bridging activates per corpus on a COMPUTED statistical-validity floor of 3 recurring clusters — the original coverage condition was measurably falsified

**The decision.** A corpus enumerates motif bridges only once it carries ≥ 3 RECURRING canonical motif clusters (`motifActivationFloor = 3`), counted over the post-`AcceptSeed` BRIDGING POPULATION — not over authored facts. Computed every session from current counts; there is no config setting, and its absence is grep-able (`internal/config/` has zero hits).

**Options considered.** (1) The blueprint's original ≈30% motif-coverage condition — REJECTED, measurably falsified: coverage is a backfill rate limit, and the only research corpus clearing 30% is the one the annex names as its NEGATIVE result. (2) A configured per-corpus switch — rejected as a setting nobody can calibrate. (3) A computed population floor — CHOSEN.

**Why a population and not a proportion.** Below the floor there are too few shared-motif pairs IN EXISTENCE for the axis to do anything but spend discovery slots on noise. Below it the mechanism does NOTHING, which is what distinguishes this class from a threshold someone picked. It is level-triggered: a corpus below the floor is not switched off, it is NOT YET ON, and it turns itself on as recurrence accumulates — no flag, no migration, no user action. The asymmetry therefore favours caution.

**Two honesty guards, both load-bearing.** (1) `motifActivation.Evaluated` — a motif-free corpus short-circuits BEFORE the floor is consulted, so "never asked" and "asked, below the floor" would otherwise be the same struct and the health line would call a motif-free corpus *inactive*. (2) A degenerate-cut strict fallback, closing a dead band where the disjointness gate was silently off on hapax-dominated young corpora.

**The cost this floor imposes on its own measurement — disclose when citing activation.** K=3 suppresses knomit-kb's one far bridge (`configure-what-varies`), verified with a throwaway K=1 build. That is the gate working as designed, but it is why the far lane's n is 2 and not 3.

**The correction that nearly went the other way:** counts were first taken over AUTHORED facts. The bridging population is the review seed pool, which `AcceptSeed` filters to epistemic — a quarter to a half of every corpus's motifed facts are pragmatic and can never bridge. Counts fell 6→4, 9→6, 2→1, 2→0, moving the candidate range from 3…6 to 2…4. Had it not been caught, K would have been set on a population the axis cannot act on.
