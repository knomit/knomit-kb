# Motif bridging splits by SIMILAR_TO adjacency into near→forward→synthesis and far→backward→hypothesis, with inverted scoring on the far lane

`BridgeMotif` is a peer grouping key of entity/domain in the review engine. Enumeration is PURE over injected resolvers — no LLM, no index (MN2) — with gates applied in order: df band → subject-disjointness → separation ≥ 2.

**The lane split.** Candidates divide by SIMILAR_TO adjacency. NEAR → forward → synthesis, through the existing `bridgeQ` scoring. FAR → backward → hypothesis, with scoring INVERTED as `W·(1−meanSim)` — on the far lane, dissimilarity is the signal. Per-lane sub-budgets are 8+8 at high effort, additional to the entity/domain 48.

**Options and rationale.** A single undifferentiated lane was rejected because the existing bridge gate stack selects for near-duplicates by construction (cohesion and separation are computed on the same SIMILAR_TO graph), so a far-mechanism pair scored by `bridgeQ` always loses to a same-event restatement. Exact-first ranking across the lanes was considered and rejected.

**Status of the evidence — state this whenever citing the lane split.** The far lane's only direct evidence is NEGATIVE at n=2: both far pairs the corpora actually served were judged TOPIC rather than MECH by a blind judge, in two independent runs. Judge variance was MEASURED, not assumed: 83.3% agreement across all four verdicts and 95.8% on the MECH boundary over 24 common pairs — real, modest, and insufficient to explain the far-lane result.

**The lane-split SEMANTICS are carried forward, not settled.** The binary one-edge near/far rule silently deletes sparse-similarity groups at the cohesion floor. Measured EMPTY on every corpus — not one candidate dropped for any cause — so the redesign is re-deferred on ABSENCE OF POPULATION, not on evidence that it is fine. `FarOversizeDropped`/`FarOtherDropped` counters ship so the denominator exists when a corpus produces one.
