# Emergent-discovery roadmap lives in .claude/future/ as 12 best-solution proposals under three pillars

**Options considered:** (a) proposals framed as retrofits riding existing infrastructure, ranked by integration effort (quick wins first, side tables to avoid schema changes, watermark tricks to avoid state fields); (b) proposals describing the best end-state design, treating current architecture as mutable per the pre-release solution-quality principle.

**Rationale:** The designer explicitly invoked kb/principles/anti-patterns/solution-quality-pre-release: pre-release there is no migration penalty (the SQLite index rebuilds from git), so effort-ordered tiers and minimum-change designs are anti-patterns. First-draft proposals were rewritten after violating this.

**The choice:** The roadmap at .claude/future/ (00 storyline + 12 proposals) is organized as one target architecture with three pillars: (1) one structure substrate — an anchor graph in the existing property graph (proposals 01-03, 05, 07); (2) local judgment primitives — a cross-encoder entailment model beside embeddings (04, 08, 09); (3) epistemic lifecycle as first-class state — verification, structured settlement, methodology calibration (06, 10-12). Proposals 10-cross-peer-bridges and 11-usage-graph-seeding from the original brainstorm were explicitly de-scoped by the designer. Status: drafts pending refinement with a different model; nothing implemented yet.
