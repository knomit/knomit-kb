# PR E ruling (2026-10-21): callers get concrete child handles, the parent's seam re-exports and Service forwards into merge, facts and remotesync are deleted, vcs-backed Service operations stay the store's own API; E1–E4 have landed (6244da58, 2a5bcadf, f41adda1, f3c32912); E5, E6, E7, E7b and E8 are planned

RULING (project owner, 2026-10-21), on PR E of the internal/store refactor. It is a plan with a status: part 3 is built (E1), the E2 precondition is built, parts 1 and 5 are built for the facts child (E3), parts 1 and 3 are built for the merge child (E4), the rest is planned.

THE RULING, in six parts:
1. Delete every parent `*_seam.go` re-export, `store.RemoteIndex`, and every Service forward into merge, facts and remotesync. Callers get the concrete child handles and import the owning package.
2. Keep the vcs-backed Service operations as the store's own API. vcs stays under `store/internal/`, so nothing outside the store can write raw git objects or refs around the write path (the lock order, the signer, the derived-state sync and NotifyCommit under the lock).
3. Move the test seams into `internal/store/storetest` through a vcs registration hook guarded by `mustBeTest`, so a test outside the store reaches a vcs-backed seam without a Service forward.
4. Move the roughly 64 vcs/sessions/sqlite value types into one importable leaf package inside the store, so no aliases remain in the parent.
5. Replace `store.FactIndex` with a facts test hook.
6. Leave FactQuery, GraphStore, HistoryQuery, MethodologyMatcher, SearchIndex, MotifIndex and AbstractionIndex for a follow-up PR E8.

STATUS (updated after the E4 merge). E1 to E4 have landed (6244da58, 2a5bcadf, f41adda1, f3c32912); E5, E6, E7, E7b and E8 are planned.
- E2 merged into dev as 2a5bcadf (PR #573). It stopped facts.Repo and merge.Repo from embedding *vcs.Repo (they hold it in a named unexported field vr; remotesync.Repo is `struct{ *merge.Repo; vr *vcs.Repo }`), because handing the child handles out under part 1 would otherwise have leaked every vcs.Repo method (LockBranch, NotifyCommit, CommitSigs, Storer, SetRefForTest) to the caller by method promotion. TestChildRepos_DoNotEmbedVcsRepo (internal/store/child_repo_no_embed_test.go) pins this for a pointer or by-value embed.
- E1 merged into dev as 6244da58 (PR #574). Ruling part 3 is built: internal/store/storetest exists (13 functions), the Service carries no Testing* or *ForTest method, and the registration hook (vcs.RegisterServiceRepo / ServiceRepoForTest, plus the same shape in facts and merge for RawWrite and SetExperimentActivity; HISTORICAL: the facts hook went in E3 and the merge hook in E4, only the vcs hook remains) is in place, each getter guarded by mustBeTest. Guards B1 (storetest in the shipped-binary forbidden list of harness_isolation_test.go) and B2 (TestTestSeamsNotReachedFromProduction) are in place. No exported Service accessor for a child handle was added by E1; those arrived in E3 (Service.Facts(), Service.FactsRepo()) and E4 (Service.Merge()). See kb/architecture/store/storetest/43a60c87.md, including its known gap (no guard checks that the getters still call mustBeTest).
- E3 merged into dev as f41adda1 (PR #575): the facts child is handed out (see the E3 paragraph at the end).
- E4 merged into dev as f3c32912 (PR #576): the merge child is handed out (see the E4 paragraph at the end).
- Planned, not built: E5 (remotesync), E6, E7, E7b and E8 (parts 1, 2, 4 and 6 above for the remaining children; part 6 is E8). Nothing in this fact claims the remotesync seam file, RemoteIndex, the Service forwards into remotesync, or the leaf types have changed yet.

CONSEQUENCE: the test seams no longer exist on Service (E1), so a new test door goes in the owning child's testseams.go behind that child's mustBeTest plus one storetest function, never as a Service method. Since E3 the facts seam (facts_seam.go), service_facts.go, the Service forwards into facts and store.FactIndex are gone: call svc.Facts() and svc.FactsRepo() and import knomit/internal/store/facts. Since E4 merge_seam.go, Service.Experiments, Service.PushedBranches and store.ExperimentIndex are gone too: call svc.Merge() and import knomit/internal/store/merge. The remotesync seam (remotesync_seam.go), store.RemoteIndex, the Service forwards into remotesync and the other parent seams still exist and are still correct to call until E5 to E7 remove them; do not cite this ruling as the current state of those. When adding a new store child handle, do not embed *vcs.Repo in it (E2), and do not add a re-export or Service forward for it (the no-facades convention, kb/conventions/go/package-boundaries/no-facades/44ec3b17.md).

MISREADINGS: (a) 'Service forwards are deleted' covers the forwards into merge, facts and remotesync only; the vcs-backed Service operations stay (part 2). (b) The follow-up E8 covering FactQuery, GraphStore, HistoryQuery, MethodologyMatcher, SearchIndex, MotifIndex and AbstractionIndex is deferred, not rejected; do not remove or rewrite those interfaces as part of E3 to E7. The AbstractionIndex name is still limited by TestConformance_ReviewPipelineOnly's by-path allowlist. (c) 'E1 landed' means the TEST seams moved; it does not mean any non-test Service forward was deleted (E3 deleted the facts ones only). (d) 'E3 landed' covers the facts child only and 'E4 landed' the merge child only; the remotesync and other seams and forwards remain.

See kb/architecture/store/sub-index-up-not-sideways/360ce7a5.md for the handle shapes at the E2 merge.
