# PR E ruling (2026-10-21): callers get concrete child handles, the parent's seam re-exports and Service forwards into merge, facts and remotesync are deleted, vcs-backed Service operations stay the store's own API; E2 has landed, E1 and E3-E8 are planned

RULING (project owner, 2026-10-21), on PR E of the internal/store refactor. It is a plan with a status, not a description of built code.

THE RULING, in six parts:
1. Delete every parent `*_seam.go` re-export, `store.RemoteIndex`, and every Service forward into merge, facts and remotesync. Callers get the concrete child handles and import the owning package.
2. Keep the vcs-backed Service operations as the store's own API. vcs stays under `store/internal/`, so nothing outside the store can write raw git objects or refs around the write path (the lock order, the signer, the derived-state sync and NotifyCommit under the lock).
3. Move the test seams into `internal/store/storetest` through a vcs registration hook guarded by `mustBeTest`, so a test outside the store reaches a vcs-backed seam without a Service forward.
4. Move the roughly 64 vcs/sessions/sqlite value types into one importable leaf package inside the store, so no aliases remain in the parent.
5. Replace `store.FactIndex` with a facts test hook.
6. Leave FactQuery, GraphStore, HistoryQuery, MethodologyMatcher, SearchIndex, MotifIndex and AbstractionIndex for a follow-up PR E8.

STATUS (2026-10-21). E2 HAS LANDED, merged into dev as 2a5bcadf (PR #573). It stopped facts.Repo and merge.Repo from embedding *vcs.Repo (they hold it in a named unexported field vr; remotesync.Repo is `struct{ *merge.Repo; vr *vcs.Repo }`), because handing the child handles out under part 1 would otherwise have leaked every vcs.Repo method (LockBranch, NotifyCommit, CommitSigs, Storer, SetRefForTest) to the caller by method promotion. TestChildRepos_DoNotEmbedVcsRepo (internal/store/child_repo_no_embed_test.go) pins this for a pointer or by-value embed. The other parts are PLANNED, not built: E1 and E3 to E8 had not landed at the time of this fact; parts 1 to 6 above are the plan for them, and nothing in this fact claims the seam files, RemoteIndex, the Service forwards, storetest or the leaf types have changed yet.

CONSEQUENCE: until those PRs land, the parent's seam files and Service forwards still exist and are still correct to call; do not cite this ruling as the current state of the code. When adding a new store child handle, do not embed *vcs.Repo in it (E2), and do not add a re-export or Service forward for it (the no-facades convention, kb/conventions/go/package-boundaries/no-facades/44ec3b17.md).

MISREADINGS: (a) 'Service forwards are deleted' covers the forwards into merge, facts and remotesync only; the vcs-backed Service operations stay (part 2). (b) The follow-up E8 covering FactQuery, GraphStore, HistoryQuery, MethodologyMatcher, SearchIndex, MotifIndex and AbstractionIndex is deferred, not rejected; do not remove or rewrite those interfaces as part of E1 to E7. The AbstractionIndex name is still limited by TestConformance_ReviewPipelineOnly's by-path allowlist.

See kb/architecture/store/sub-index-up-not-sideways/360ce7a5.md for the handle shapes at the E2 merge.

STATUS UPDATE (PR E1, branch refactor/store-e1-storetest, head 19b703db; becomes true only when that PR merges): ruling part 3 is built. internal/store/storetest exists, the Service carries no Testing* or *ForTest method, and the vcs registration hook (vcs.RegisterServiceRepo / ServiceRepoForTest, plus the same shape in facts and merge for RawWrite and SetExperimentActivity) is in place. Guards B1 (storetest in the shipped-binary forbidden list) and B2 (TestTestSeamsNotReachedFromProduction) are in place. Remaining planned: E3 to E8. No exported Service accessor for a child handle was added by E1; those arrive in E3/E4. See kb/architecture/store/storetest/43a60c87.md.
