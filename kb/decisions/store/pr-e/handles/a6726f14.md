# PR E ruling (2026-10-21): callers get concrete child handles, the parent's seam re-exports and Service forwards into merge, facts and remotesync are deleted, vcs-backed Service operations stay the store's own API; E1–E5 have landed (6244da58, 2a5bcadf, f41adda1, f3c32912, b4d232ee); E6, E7, E7b and E8 are planned

RULING (project owner, 2026-10-21), on PR E of the internal/store refactor. It is a plan with a status: part 3 is built (E1), the E2 precondition is built, parts 1 and 5 are built for the facts child (E3), parts 1 and 3 are built for the merge child (E4), the rest is planned.

THE RULING, in six parts:
1. Delete every parent `*_seam.go` re-export, `store.RemoteIndex`, and every Service forward into merge, facts and remotesync. Callers get the concrete child handles and import the owning package.
2. Keep the vcs-backed Service operations as the store's own API. vcs stays under `store/internal/`, so nothing outside the store can write raw git objects or refs around the write path (the lock order, the signer, the derived-state sync and NotifyCommit under the lock).
3. Move the test seams into `internal/store/storetest` through a vcs registration hook guarded by `mustBeTest`, so a test outside the store reaches a vcs-backed seam without a Service forward.
4. Move the roughly 64 vcs/sessions/sqlite value types into one importable leaf package inside the store, so no aliases remain in the parent.
5. Replace `store.FactIndex` with a facts test hook.
6. Leave FactQuery, GraphStore, HistoryQuery, MethodologyMatcher, SearchIndex, MotifIndex and AbstractionIndex for a follow-up PR E8.

STATUS (updated after the E5 merge). E1 to E5 have landed (6244da58, 2a5bcadf, f41adda1, f3c32912, b4d232ee); E6, E7, E7b and E8 are planned.

CONSEQUENCE: the test seams no longer exist on Service (E1), so a new test door goes in the owning child's testseams.go behind that child's mustBeTest plus one storetest function, never as a Service method. Since E3 the facts seam (facts_seam.go), service_facts.go, the Service forwards into facts and store.FactIndex are gone: call svc.Facts() and svc.FactsRepo() and import knomit/internal/store/facts. Since E4 merge_seam.go, Service.Experiments, Service.PushedBranches and store.ExperimentIndex are gone too: call svc.Merge() and import knomit/internal/store/merge. Since E5 (b4d232ee) remotesync_seam.go, consensus_branch.go, service_sync.go, store.RemoteIndex and every Service forward into remotesync are gone too: call svc.Remote() (a *remotesync.Index) and import knomit/internal/store/remotesync. The other parent seams (fleetrec, verify, vocab, index, fsck, gitserve, vcs) still exist and are still correct to call until E6 and E7 remove them; do not cite this ruling as the current state of those. When adding a new store child handle, do not embed *vcs.Repo in it (E2), and do not add a re-export or Service forward for it (the no-facades convention, kb/conventions/go/package-boundaries/no-facades/44ec3b17.md).

MISREADINGS: (a) 'Service forwards are deleted' covers the forwards into merge, facts and remotesync only; the vcs-backed Service operations stay (part 2). (b) The follow-up E8 covering FactQuery, GraphStore, HistoryQuery, MethodologyMatcher, SearchIndex, MotifIndex and AbstractionIndex is deferred, not rejected; do not remove or rewrite those interfaces as part of E3 to E7. The AbstractionIndex name is still limited by TestConformance_ReviewPipelineOnly's by-path allowlist. (c) 'E1 landed' means the TEST seams moved; it does not mean any non-test Service forward was deleted (E3 deleted the facts ones only). (d) 'E3 landed' covers the facts child only, 'E4 landed' the merge child only and 'E5 landed' the remotesync child only; the other children's seams and forwards remain (E6, E7).
