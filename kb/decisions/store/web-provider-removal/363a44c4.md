# W0f deleted TopicLister: listTopicDir and topicFactByPath are package functions in internal/edge/web, and topic-browse tests run on real stores, failing ListDir through facts.Index.SetListDirFaultForTest

W0f (PR #612, merged as 9b06d2ca; re-read there) deleted TopicLister, defaultTopicLister and the storeProviders.topicLister member. The old default bodies became package functions in internal/edge/web/handlers_topics.go:
- listTopicDir(ctx, ri, branch, path): ri.WithRead, then svc.Facts().ListDir(ctx, branch, path). A nil svc (repo not open) yields no entries and a nil error; a store error is returned unchanged.
- topicFactByPath(ctx, ri, branch, path): ri.WithRead, then svc.FactQuery().GetByPath(ctx, branch, path). A nil svc yields a nil fact and a nil error.

handleTopics, handleTopicNode, handleTopicFacts, topicHandler and handleHALLensTopics call them directly. webInterfaceAllowlist went from 7 to 6 names: FactReader, FactWriter, commitsProvider, factSubProvider, originProvider, RefResolver.

TESTS (handlers_topics_test.go): observeListDir installs a pass-through facts.Index.SetListDirFaultForTest hook that always returns nil and records, per store and under a mutex, the paths ListDir was asked for; it replaced the stub's listPaths probe. failListDir fails ListDir of exactly one path (every other listing passes) and counts hits; the tests assert the problem title, the injected error text AND an atomic hit count of 1 (the convention in kb/conventions/testing/web/9407c9da.md).

CONSEQUENCE: a new topic-browse test builds a real store (newRealStoreManager, or topicsRepo for a non-default ontology root) and observes or fails ListDir through the hook; it does not reintroduce a lister seam.

DOES NOT MEAN: GetByPath has a fault hook. It has none: topicFactByPath's error is dropped by the callers (type/title enrichment is best-effort, the entry is listed without them), and that drop stays untested, as it was under the stub. Also does not mean the remaining six interfaces are the recommended pattern; W0g to W0i delete them.
