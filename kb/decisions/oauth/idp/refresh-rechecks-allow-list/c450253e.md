# A token family approved through the identity provider refreshes only while its id is still on [oauth.idp].allowed_subjects: every family records who approved its request (oauth_family_approvals), and a refresh of an idp:github:<id> family whose id is gone — or whose provider is no longer configured — answers invalid_grant and REVOKES the whole family; access tokens already out run to their 2 h TTL

OPTIONS (F19 phase 3c; worker W9, reviewer S10, master ruling 2026-09-24): (a) document that removing an id from the allow list stops only NEW approvals, and leave live families and grants alone; (b) revoke every family of an unlisted github-* subject at boot; (c) check at refresh.

CHOSEN: (c), documented as well. (b) keys on the subject pattern, which an operator can also type on path 1 (`--as github-5` is a legal subject), and it acts only at a restart. (c) keys on PROVENANCE: Store.Collect copies the pending row's decided_by onto the family (FamilySpec.ApprovedBy, side table oauth_family_approvals; absent for families minted before 3c = operator-approved), and Issuer.refreshAllowed, installed on the Store by NewIssuer with or without a provider, refuses a family whose approved_by is idp:<provider>:<id> unless <provider>-<id> is currently allowed and <provider> is the configured one. Store.Refresh asks after the client, resource and scope checks (a refused attempt by someone else must not cost the real owner) and before rotating; a refusal revokes the family — every access token, refresh token and unconsumed code — and returns ErrNoLongerAllowed, which /oauth/token answers as invalid_grant.

CONSEQUENCES AN OPERATOR MEETS: (1) After removing an id and restarting (the allow list is config, read at boot), that person's current access tokens keep working until they expire (2 h default); the next refresh fails and ends the family. For immediate cut-off, also revoke their tokens and `knomit grants revoke host:github-<id>@token <perm>`. (2) Grants for host:github-<id>@token stay (history, and R5's first-approval rule), but a new token needs a new approval, which needs the allow list. (3) Removing [oauth.idp] entirely makes every path-3 family unrefreshable. (4) Families approved on paths 1 and 2 are never affected by the allow list.
