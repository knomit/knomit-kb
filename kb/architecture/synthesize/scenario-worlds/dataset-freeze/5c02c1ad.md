# The expert-scenarios dataset froze on 2026-10-12 at 372 worlds and 738 questions, every world passing two consecutive whole-tree loader runs at knomit 51ca7d6d with the dedup and same-subject gates on

The expert scenario dataset (separate git repo expert-scenarios, repo id b663d206b50a, frozen at its commit 03b492e on main) was frozen on 2026-10-12 by the end-of-wave sweep. Contents: 372 worlds and 738 questions, of which 210 are gold outcome gap and 37 partly (the rest are answer).

Pools. Training pool (worlds/): 346 worlds = 17 reasoning families x 2 rounds of 10, plus 6 pilots (692 questions, 195 gap, 29 partly). Held-out families (heldout-families/): 16 worlds of F15 prior-conflict and F18 derived-fact-audit (32 questions, 13 gap, 7 partly). Held-out ontology (heldout-ontology/): 10 worlds in the heldout-statute-law ontology, spread over F01, F02, F06, F07, F11 (14 questions, 2 gap, 1 partly). The training families are F01-F13, F16, F19, F20, F21. Per ontology, training pool: code-service 71, fleet-mission 68, general-sciences-markets 105, ops-runbook 102 worlds; held-out families: 4 worlds in each of those four ontologies.

Deferred: F14 world-scoped and F17 since-bookmark have family READMEs under families/ but NO worlds in the freeze; the dataset does not exercise world switching or the since-bookmark move.

Validation state: every world passed TestScenarioSpecDir (EXPERT_SPEC_DIR=<dir>, timeout off, real EmbeddingGemma so the dedup merge and same-subject gates are ON) at knomit 51ca7d6d (format v1.1, the merge of feat/expert-eval-runner), twice in a row after the sweep's last spec change, one run per ontology directory; a whole ontology directory takes over 15 minutes. The MANIFEST validated-at column reads 51ca7d6d for every world.

Consequence: treat the dataset as immutable and as valid only against a loader at or after 51ca7d6d (an older loader refuses must_name). A world edited after the freeze is no longer the frozen world (see the splits fact); a loader run with EXPERT_SPEC_EMBED=0 proves structure only, not that the world passes.

What this does NOT mean: 372 worlds is not 372 training worlds (26 are test-only, 34 are validation) and gap share is not uniform across families: gap per family ranges from 2 of 44 questions (F06) to 18 of 44 (F04) in the training pool.
