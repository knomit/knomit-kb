# A judged restatement pair is DELETED from restatement_pairs when its verdict lands; only pairs retired without a verdict stay standing — deletion IS the re-offer exclusion

When a shortlist judgement lands, the verdict is recorded in `restatement_verdicts` (branch_id-scoped sqlite; its schema comment says losing these rows is safe — empty means optimistic cold-start) and the pair row is deleted from `restatement_pairs`. A pair displaced or retired WITHOUT a verdict stays standing and can be offered again later.

Consequence: on an established branch, a judged pair is never re-offered — RestatementPairsByMatchKind needs no join to `restatement_verdicts` because deletion is the exclusion mechanism. The re-judging exposure exists only on a fresh agent branch or after DB loss, where pairs are re-detected and the branch-scoped verdicts are absent (this is the narrow coupling to issue #134 durability).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: "the pair query has no verdict join" does not mean judged pairs get re-offered — a reader auditing the SELECT alone will draw exactly that false conclusion.
