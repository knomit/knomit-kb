# internal/corpus/synthesize/restatement holds restatement.go, identity.go and the tests that do not need Pipeline or Reviewer; tests that drive NewReviewerWithOptions or NewPipeline stay in the parent, and the parent reaches the child only through exported names with a real user

At 52604c5d (PR #601, step 13c, merged; re-read there) package internal/corpus/synthesize/restatement holds restatement.go, identity.go and their white-box tests, including the 20 TestStructural_* (identity_test.go and restatement_structural_allowance_test.go), the MN-conformance guards and its own TestMain. Its non-test imports are step, domain/fact/textnorm and the store model/vocab; the only same-layer layering key is restatement -> step.

Tests that call NewReviewerWithOptions or NewPipeline (plan, session, standing-work) stay in the PARENT: the child cannot import its parent. The parent reaches the child only through exported names. Production users: PlanShortlist, ResolveShortlistPair, RecordShortlistVerdict (review_strategy.go, progression.go), ClusterKeyPrefix. Test-only exports kept on purpose, with reasons in the PR body: StructuralAllowance (the Pipeline-driven test sizes its fixture as allowance+3 and a literal would silently stop testing the backlog if the allowance were raised), EnsureTitleVectors and RefreshShortlist (the parent seedShortlist mirror needs the one-hour backfill budget of knomit#298, not production's titleBackfillBudget, so PlanShortlist cannot stand in). Review made ItemPriority, MaxShortlistItems, Health and RecordHealth unexported again by moving or rewriting their tests.

CONSEQUENCE: do not export a child name merely because a parent TEST wants it; first try moving the test into the child or driving the production export (the 4 avoidable ones were). ResolveShortlistPair returns the unexported *judgedPair: the parent only passes it through to RecordShortlistVerdict; do not export judgedPair to 'fix' that.

DOES NOT MEAN: that the child is behaviour-different; the move changed restatement.go and identity.go only by the package clause, 11 renames and call-site qualifiers.
