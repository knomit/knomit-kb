# pipeline_sessions has phase column with CAS-based transitions

pipeline_sessions has TWO independent columns: `status` (lifecycle: active|completed|abandoned) and `phase` (workflow: work|reflect|done). AdvancePipelineSessionPhase(ctx, id, from, to) does an atomic UPDATE ... WHERE id=? AND phase=? returning (rowsAffected==1, nil). The CAS is what gives idempotent enqueue WITHOUT a separate dedup query: when two continuations race after the last work item is answered, exactly one wins the work→reflect transition and inserts the single reflect item; the other re-fetches and finds itself already in reflect phase. This is load-bearing once reflect has side effects (per reflect-response-schema design): once-per-session enqueue prevents duplicate methodology proposals.
