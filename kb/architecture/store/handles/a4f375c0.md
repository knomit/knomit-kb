# Guards A1 and A2 enforce the no-facades rule in package store: no re-export of an importable child, no re-export of a non-importable one without an outside caller, and no one-call Service forward into an importable child

CURRENT STATE (PR E7c, #585, merged into dev as 8bcd5449; re-read there). A1 TestStoreParent_ReexportsOnlyNonImportableWithOutsideCaller (test/archtest/store_reexports_test.go) parses the non-test files of internal/store (depth 1) and finds re-exports: type X = p.Y, const/var X = p.Y, and a function whose single statement calls p.F with exactly its own parameters in order, where p is an import of a store child. It fails when p is IMPORTABLE (anything not under store/internal/), and when p is non-importable but no file outside internal/store/** (tests included) selects store.X; an unexported re-export fails by definition. A2 TestStoreService_NoForwardIntoImportableChild fails on an exported *Service method whose single statement calls s.<field>.M (the field's declared type is in an importable child) or <importableChild>.F; the five survivors are in injectsStoreState with a reason (FleetAt, FileAt, FleetKeysOf, FleetMainLog, FleetSince: each passes the child s.rh.Storer(), which callers cannot obtain). Verify, PruneGeneratedRefs (s.integrity().M, a call on a call) and Handler (several statements) are not matched and need no entry.

WHAT A1 FOUND at E7c: only sqlite_conn.go's five unexported forwards (conn, beginTxIfNeeded, registerVec, float32SliceToBytes, groupingKey). They were deleted; package store calls store/internal/sqlite directly. The one forward left, SyncDSNParam, has outside callers (internal/repos, internal/web) and sqlite is not importable from there. vcs_seam.go has no re-export left.

NON-VACUITY CHANGED: the design's floor of 100 distinct outside store.X names is not reachable (the parent's outside surface is a handful of names after E3-E7b); the test instead needs 300+ outside files parsed, 5+ distinct selectors, and the live names Service, Open and SyncDSNParam to be seen.

MISREADING (named): A2 is SHAPE-BASED, so a green A2 does NOT prove that no Service pass-through exists. It matches an exported *Service method whose sole statement is `s.<field>.M(...)` or `<childpkg>.F(...)`. It does NOT match `return s.helper().M(...)` (a call on a call) or `m := s.mr; return m.M(...)` (a call through a local): reviewer sabotage of both shapes stayed green. Verify and PruneGeneratedRefs (s.integrity().M) are un-matched for the same reason, which is why they were not entered in injectsStoreState; that they are legitimate (they inject store state) is a reading judgement, not something A2 checks. CONSEQUENCE: review a new Service method by reading it; do not take A2 green as the no-pass-through proof. Open follow-up: close the call-on-call and local-variable holes.

DOES NOT MEAN: the vcs-backed Service operations are flagged (they call a non-importable child and are the ruled exception), nor that a Service method that builds a value first and then calls a child is a facade. Sabotaged red: type Mode = merge.Mode, var ErrZ = vcs.ErrBranchNotFound with no outside user, aliased-import alias, a func Register forward, s.mr / s.fr forwards, a fleetrec.At forward, and a stale allowlist entry.
