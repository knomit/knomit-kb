# Test J replay is ReasonOptions.Replay: reason.js's replay() rebuilds a gold state with the loop's own reads (every listed fact hydrated at HEAD, labelled, relevant ones expanded, gold moves marked taken) and asks ONE judgment through the loop's own functions (judgeRelevance, judgeSupport, chooseFrom, SYNTHESIZE, DECIDE), returning outcome `replay` and ReasonResult.Replay; nothing is written

repos.ReasonReplay {judgment: relevant|support|choose_move|synthesize|assess, facts: [{path, relevant, verdict}], moves: [{move, from, arg}], pending: [paths], claim}. Replay runs in mode H only. Because the judgment goes through the same functions a live run calls, the briefing is the live one from that state; differences from a live run are only in the fact text (always hydrated from explain, where a live relevance briefing uses the query row's snippet).

The items come from synthesize.ReplayItems(world, question): for each gold step in the chain's listed order, the state is the facts the earlier steps surfaced (Surfaces plus every labelled id; retracted facts excluded) labelled by the gold (goldRelevant: the chain's label, else yes for support/should-cite/partial-support; goldVerdict: the chain's own label — the last step's, as the grader reads gold labels — else supports for the support set, else unrelated) and the earlier steps' FIRST move alternative. A step yields choose_move (unless its only move is neighbours(question), the gather), relevant over its Relevant map and support over its Support map; then synthesize over the whole chain and assess on the gold claim when the gold has one. GradeReplay: per-label accuracy for relevant/support; choose_move correct when the chosen entry matches any gold alternative (moveMatches), with gold_on_menu and top_matches (the mechanical baseline); synthesize correct when it claims iff the gold outcome is not gap; assess against the gold label.

What this does NOT mean: hypothesize and seek are not replayed (hypothesize needs a judge for premise match; seek's settling-evidence recall needs a per-question settling fact the format does not name). choose_move accuracy is bounded by gold_on_menu: a gold move the harness never offers cannot be chosen, and with the gold oracle on the data-repo pilot set only 56% of gold moves are on the menu.

Precedence matters (review blocker B3, fixed at 20434db7): a question's support set may hold a fact the chain labels `contradicts` — every F7 world has one — and reading set membership first replayed that outweighed contradiction as a support in every later state. Do not reorder goldVerdict; pinned by TestReplayItems_B3_ContradictionInTheSupportSetStaysAContradiction. The test oracle (oracle.support) follows the same precedence (Z1). Also at 20434db7 (B2): the synthesize replay reports SYNTHESIZE's restates candidate as `existing` (st.existing || st.restating), because st.existing is set only in EMIT, which a replay never reaches; before that a restating gold state graded as "no claim" (TestReplay_B2_RestatingStateIsAnAnswer).
