# reason.js is a harness-owned state machine — GATHER, ASSESS, WIDEN, BACKTRACK, SYNTHESIZE, DECIDE, HYPOTHESIZE, SEEK, EMIT, GAP — where the harness runs every read and the model makes every judgment through one schema'd knomit.model call

internal/repos/engine_reason.js (PR 3 of the expert spike, changed by PR 5) runs one state per loop step over one explicit `st` object. GATHER: neighbours(question) via knomit.query. ASSESS: a `relevant` judgment on every newly surfaced fact (titles + snippet), then each relevant fact is read in full at HEAD (explain plus its walk's next pages until all depth-1 refs are in), then one `support` judgment per batch on the relevant evidence; a fact judged irrelevant gets verdict `unrelated` without a second call. WIDEN: the model picks one entry (or 0 = stop) from a menu of the ranked frontier, the harness executes it. Moves and their reads: neighbours(f) = query{text: title + first sentence}; entity/motif/domain = query{entities|motifs|applies_to: [one value]}; ontology = query{path: <root>/<topic>/<category>/}; kind_type = query{type: [t]}; refs_out(f) = the cached explain's depth-1 nodes; refs_in(f) = knomit.incoming; history(f) = explain at the previous revision's commit; supersession(f) = re-read at HEAD every depth-1 premise of the REFERRER f that is superseded and not deleted (f is the fact that cites the updated premise, not the premise itself). SEEK is a text query by the fact the model says would settle the hypothesis (recorded as move `seek`, which the graders ignore).

Frontier generation (PR 5, commit 2d0a8a24, all modes): expand() calls addKindMoves(), which offers kind_type(t) for EVERY type of the type table whose role is not excluded (so policy, principle, heuristic, concept, process and the evidence types too, never signal: ASSESS never judges a signal) and ontology(<root>/<topic>) for every top-level topic in the `ontology.topics` global. Before PR 5 only kind_type(methodology) and kind_type(hypothesis) existed, while the gold names policy 98 times, principle 44, concept 42, heuristic 40 and process 32 over the training pool. supersession(f) is anchored on the referrer f; before PR 5 it was anchored on the premise, which BACKTRACK's own supersession(anchor) and 67 of the training gold's 100 supersession moves contradict (19 of the 100 anchor on the updated premise alone and can no longer be generated; see the PR 5 review's fix-later N5).

Ranking: the frontier is ranked by scoreOf(e) = MOVE_BASE[move] x best + bias + min(count, 6) x 0.25 + (n - 1) x 0.5, where best is the highest over the entry's live origins of (1 for the question, else TYPES[type].rank, +0.5 when that origin's verdict is supports), n is the number of live origins, count the move's expected yield, and bias the largest bias any addMove gave the entry (refs_out of a derived fact +2, history +0.5, supersession +0.5, kind_type(hypothesis) -0.25) plus -1 for an entity/motif/domain the question already names, +4 for kind_type(methodology) until a methodology is in the read set, and KIND_FOUND (-0.5) for a kind_type whose type a relevant live fact already has. The menu shows at most 12 entries (MENU_SIZE) and at most 4 of one move kind (MENU_PER_MOVE). Before the menu, WIDEN runs deadLine(): a relevant derived fact whose premise is retracted or contradicted gets refs_out executed by the harness (or goes straight to BACKTRACK when the moves are spent) instead of a model choice.

SYNTHESIZE's restates shortcut (one support that already IS the answer) applies only with no live contradiction and a posterior at or over the trust bar, and goes through EMIT's freshness re-read before existing_answer is returned. EMIT first blocks on unjudged facts (re-judge once, else gap), then re-reads every support and a derived support's premises. These loop rules (the EMIT unjudged block and its re-judge, the freshness re-read, the restates guards) hold in every control mode (H, M2, M4, MF); the carried modes' labels answer only the ASSESS that applies them. EMIT's answer and GAP's hypothesis pass distinct_from naming every fact the run judged and does not cite (see invariants/repos/engine/emit-distinct-from), and GAP with no place for its partial support files the hypothesis under the majority topic of everything the run read (live facts), citing what it judged relevant there, instead of writing nothing.

Briefings: DECIDE's assess briefing gives each support its body up to BODY_CHARS (1200), as the support and synthesize briefings do (PR 5; it was SNIPPET_CHARS, 240, so assess judged a claim drafted from text it was not shown). Relevance, choose_move and hypothesize keep the 240-character snippet.

Type table, applied mechanically: pragmatic facts are obeyed, never weighed. Policy (role constrains) goes into briefings as a rule and, when relevant, into the answer's refs and its "Obeys" line (answerFact cites roleOf === 'constrains' only); heuristic (role biases) goes into briefings only, NEVER into the refs; signal is excluded; `principle` the TYPE is a premise weighed like evidence (the principles/ topic has no standing); methodology goes into every later briefing and the refs; a relevant hypothesis carries no mass and queues a SEEK on its settlement.

Budgets: ReasonOptions{Budget (wall clock, capped at MaxReasonBudget = 1h), MaxMoves (default 12: gather, widen moves, seeks), MaxSteps (default 60; when spent the loop stops exploring and closes, hard ceiling +12 then GAP), Trust (default 0.5)} via Engine.RunWith; the MCP tool still takes only `budget`.

Measured with the gold oracle on the validation slice (34 worlds, TestEvalRunOracle, PR 5): the gold move is on the ranked frontier for 176 of 201 replayed choose_move items (0.88) but on the 12-entry menu for 137 (0.68); the menu cut is now the main limit, not generation.

Consequence: a new move needs a queryFor/execute case, a MOVE_BASE entry and an expand() source; its name must be one of synthesize.ScenarioMoves or the graders will not count it. Misreadings to avoid: the menu choice is the model's, but the frontier ranking and the menu cut are the harness's and steer the model (E02's menu-bias risk); a move on the frontier is not offered to the model, only the menu is; supersession(f) names the referrer, not "f itself re-read".
