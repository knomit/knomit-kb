# effort=normal MUST reproduce pre-discovery behaviour byte-for-byte

At effort=normal the review and hypothesize pipelines MUST behave exactly as they did before emergent-fact discovery existed: bridgeSeeds returns nil, ZERO `discover` work items are enqueued, and no origin=discovered facts are written. This is the regression contract that let discovery ship on the live pipelines; it is regression-tested (TestReviewer_NormalEffort_DefaultPath asserts no discover items on the normal path) and the DiscoveryConfig default is `normal`. Anything that makes normal effort diverge — enqueuing a discover item, engaging bridgeSeeds, or even reordering the standard work items — is a defect. Discovery is opt-in via medium/high ONLY.
