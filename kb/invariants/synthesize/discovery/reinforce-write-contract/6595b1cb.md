# REINFORCE rewrites an existing fact under a MEANING-PRESERVING contract enforced at the stored-byte boundary — only refs/sources/evidence_weight may change, and a lossy target is skipped, not written

Discovery's third outcome, added with the motif axis (GATE rider 3). On a recall hit — the corpus already knows this — the seeds join the EXISTING fact's refs as an independent derivation path, `sources` increments, and `evidence_weight` recomputes. No new fact is minted.

**The contract, and where it is enforced.** Only `refs`, `sources` and `evidence_weight` lines may differ in the STORED BYTES. The check is at the byte boundary, not on the in-memory struct — that placement is the whole point, because the loss this guards against happens during the parse→serialize round-trip and both sides of an in-memory comparison are already truncated.

**Lossy targets are SKIPPED WITH A REASON, never written.** Dropped motifs and real origin changes disqualify a target. Measured skip rate on real corpora: 0% (knomit-kb), ~0% (agentic-engineering), **4.4% (core — precisely the facts whose provenance a rewrite would corrupt)**. The skip rate being non-zero on exactly the corpus with the known origin-reattribution class is the contract working.

**Extra agent-named refs are DISCARDED.** The model does not get to add citations here; it gets to record that its seeds derived the fact.

**Why both halves exist.** Two HIGH findings in review were this write path exceeding its mandate: silent motif deletion via the lenient-parser round-trip, and arbitrary model-named refs. Neither was visible from the struct; both are visible at the stored-byte boundary.

**Ruled NO, do not re-litigate without evidence:** REINFORCE does not boost confidence. Corroboration lives in `sources`/`evidence_weight`. Revisit only if measurement shows consumers ignore `evidence_weight`.

**Related, and NOT remediated:** prune's merge path shares this round-trip shape and has NO such contract. See [[parse-time-origin-normalisation-class]].
