# REINFORCE rewrites an existing fact under a contract checked LINE BY LINE against the stored bytes — refs/sources/evidence_weight may change, an absent origin may materialise ONLY as the parsed default, and everything else is a skip

Discovery's third outcome, added with the motif axis (GATE rider 3). On a recall hit — the corpus already knows this — the seeds join the EXISTING fact's refs as an independent derivation path, `sources` increments, and `evidence_weight` recomputes. No new fact is minted.

**THE CONTRACT, with every permitted case named.** `applyReinforcements` diffs the STORED frontmatter against the REWRITTEN frontmatter line by line, and refuses unless each differing key is one of:
1. `refs`, `sources`, `evidence_weight` — `permittedRewriteLines`, the three the reinforcement exists to change.
2. **`origin`, and ONLY when it was ABSENT from the stored bytes AND would materialise as exactly the parsed default.** If it would materialise as anything else the write is refused with `origin would materialise as …, not the parsed default …`. This is the measured semantic no-op, and it is a real fourth case — not covered by the three above.

Any other key added, changed or REMOVED is a refusal, and the BODY is compared separately and is never this path's to touch.

**Where the check sits, and why that placement is the whole point.** At the stored-BYTE boundary, not on the in-memory struct. The loss this guards against happens during the parse→serialize round trip, so both sides of an in-memory comparison are already truncated and would agree. A guard reading two post-transform values cannot see the transform.

**Lossy targets are SKIPPED WITH A REASON, never written.** Measured skip rate on real corpora: 0% (knomit-kb), ~0% (agentic-engineering), **4.4% (core — precisely the facts whose provenance a rewrite would corrupt)**. A non-zero skip rate on exactly the corpus carrying the known origin-reattribution class is the contract working, not a defect.

**Extra agent-named refs are DISCARDED**, and `Reason` is REQUIRED — absence of a reason is a rejection, not a blank field, because an equivalence nobody could justify in a sentence is the hallucinated one and an over-merge is invisible downstream.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN.**
- It does NOT mean sibling round-trip writers are safe. **Prune's merge path shares this round-trip shape and has NO such contract** — measured deleting a stored `origin: distilled` line end to end. See `kb/gotchas/fact/parse-time-origin-normalisation/dfa8768f.md`.
- It does NOT mean "only three lines may ever differ" — see case 2.
- It does NOT mean confidence rises. Ruled NO: corroboration lives in `sources`/`evidence_weight`. Revisit only if measurement shows consumers ignore `evidence_weight`.
