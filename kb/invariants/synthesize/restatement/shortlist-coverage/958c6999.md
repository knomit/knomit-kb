# A restatement cache-state entry means "scanned against the COMPLETE axis", never merely "visited" — marking a fact covered while the axis is still filling freezes its neighbours out permanently

The restatement shortlist diffs the live fact set against `restatement_cache_state` to find its delta. That table is the ONLY thing that says "already covered", so writing a fact's id into it is a claim that the fact has been scanned against the complete abstraction axis — not that a session looked at it.

**The rule.** Only mark a fact covered once it was scanned against the axis as it will finally stand. `refreshRestatementShortlist` therefore diffs against `LiveEpistemicFactsOnAxis` (facts that actually HAVE a title vector), not `LiveEpistemicFacts`.

**The consequence if you get it wrong.** Backfill is incremental under a latency budget, so early sessions see a partial axis. A fact scanned against a half-filled axis cannot see partners embedded later. Mark it covered anyway and its KNN never runs again — permanently, because the cache state is exactly what says "already covered". The hole is SILENT: nothing reports it, the coverage number looks healthy, and the pairs that were never discoverable are simply absent.

**The named misreading.** "Covered" does NOT mean "this session processed it", and it does NOT mean "this fact has a vector". A fact with a title vector may still have been scanned while its future partners had none. The question is always what the AXIS held at scan time, never what the FACT held.

Enforced by `TestDynamics_PartialBackfillClosesOverSessions`, which drives several sessions with partial backfill budgets and asserts the cache closes over them rather than freezing at session one.
