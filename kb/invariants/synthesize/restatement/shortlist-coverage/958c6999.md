# NOTHING is marked covered until the axis is COMPLETE and the fact was scorable — both components, or a partial scan becomes a permanent hole

`restatement_cache_state` is the ONLY thing that says "already covered", so writing a fact's id into it is a claim that the fact has been scanned against the COMPLETE corpus — not that a session looked at it.

**THE RULE, with every component named.** A fact id is written to the cache state only when BOTH hold:
1. **The axis is complete** — `complete := len(onAxis) == len(live)`. While coverage is partial, `covered` stays nil and NOTHING is recorded, for any fact.
2. **That fact was scorable** — a fact whose blended vector could not be read is excluded even on a complete axis (`unscorable`), because it was not really scanned either.

**The consequence while the axis is filling.** Every on-axis fact is rescanned EVERY session. The pairs found so far are still offered to the judge — they are just not treated as final. The cost is bounded to the fill period and vanishes the session coverage closes.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN.**
- **It does NOT mean "diff against on-axis facts and mark those covered".** The on-axis diff (`LiveEpistemicFactsOnAxis` vs `LiveEpistemicFacts`) is a SEPARATE mechanism answering a different question — which facts are in play to SCAN this session, since a fact with no title vector has no neighbours to find. Reading the on-axis diff as the coverage rule reproduces exactly the bug this invariant exists to prevent: facts scanned against a half-filled axis, marked done, frozen out forever.
- **It does NOT mean "a fact with a title vector has been covered".** A fact can be on the axis and scanned while its future partners had none. The question is always what the AXIS held at scan time, never what the FACT held.
- **It does NOT mean the two components are redundant.** Axis-completeness is global; scorability is per fact. A complete axis still produces uncovered facts.

**Why a partial-scan hole is PERMANENT and SILENT.** A fact scanned against a half-filled axis cannot see partners embedded later, and KNN asymmetry means the later fact's own scan may not find it either. Nothing reports the gap, the coverage number looks healthy, and the pairs that were never discoverable are simply absent.

Enforced by `TestDynamics_PartialBackfillClosesOverSessions`.
