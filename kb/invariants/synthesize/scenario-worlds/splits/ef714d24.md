# The expert-scenarios splits are frozen: test is every held-out world (26), validation is 2 seeded worlds per training family (34), train is the other 312, so consumers select by the split files and never by directory

Frozen 2026-10-12 in the expert-scenarios repo (commit 25ce826).

Rules. TEST = every world under heldout-families/ (F15, F18) and heldout-ontology/ (heldout-statute-law): 26 worlds, 46 questions; never trained on, never used to tune. VALIDATION = a family-stratified 10 percent of the training pool (worlds/): per training family (directory slug) max(1, round(0.10 x n)) worlds, which is 2 per family, 34 in all; each family's sorted ids are shuffled with ONE Python 3 random.Random(20261012) instance, families visited in sorted order, the first k taken. TRAIN = every other world under worlds/, the 6 pilots included: 312 worlds.

Identity. Each split entry carries spec_sha256 (sha256 of spec.yaml) and spec_hash (sha256 of spec.yaml, a 0x00 byte, then the bytes of its ontology_file; this is the eval runner's spec_hash, so an eval record can be matched to a frozen world). A record or file whose hash differs is not the frozen world. The splits never change: a world in a frozen split is never edited, and a fix is a NEW world id added to a later split file.

Consequences for consumers. (1) Select worlds by the split files, never by directory: the 34 validation worlds live under worlds/, so the eval runner's train pool (which admits everything under worlds/ that has no F15/F18 and no heldout- ontology file) will include them unless the consumer filters. (2) The test split spans TWO runner pools, heldout-families and heldout-ontology; run them separately (an output directory belongs to one pool) and report both. (3) Gap share of train plus validation is 195 of 692 questions (28.2 percent; E04 requires at least 25 percent gap and decline).

What this does NOT mean: 'everything under worlds/ is training' is false (34 of its 346 worlds are validation), and 'everything not under worlds/ is one test pool' is false for the runner (two pools). The runner's pool guard keeps held-out content out of a train run; it does NOT keep validation out of one.
