# The UI never sends an implicit credential choice: Unlink (RemoteCredential) and Restore of an archived repo with an origin (ArchivedSettings) show a required choice with nothing preselected, and confirm stays disabled until one is chosen and complete

Unlink on the Remote card's Credential row offers 'A pasted credential' (this server's SSH key on an SSH remote) or 'No credential (public remote)'; neither is checked at first, and Unlink stays disabled until one is chosen and, for pasted, its fields are filled. It then sends `{secret_id: null}` plus the pasted fields, or `{secret_id: null}` alone. Restore of an archived repo that has an origin offers Stored secret / Pasted credential / No credential with nothing checked and sends exactly one of `{secret_id}`, the pasted fields, or `{no_credential: true}`; a repo with no origin restores without the prompt. A refusal (say 422, the secret does not fit the origin) stays inside the prompt.

Consequence: do not add a default (for example preselecting 'No credential' or reusing the previous pasted token) to either flow; the API's explicit none stays allowed, but the UI must never reach it by accident. Pinned by RemoteStatus.credential.test.tsx and ArchivedSettings.credential.test.tsx.
