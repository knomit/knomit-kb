# The origin identity (meta row origin_identity) is originIdentity(stored URL): credential-free, spelling-normalised, recorded only after a Sync round whose fetch AND reconcile succeeded; with nothing recorded a round is not a switch

RULE (PR #544, merge 8a01d3ca on dev, read there). 'Same origin or not' is the meta row origin_identity compared with originIdentity(current origin URL). Three parts, all load-bearing:
(1) WHAT IT HOLDS. Always originIdentity(url), never a raw URL: credentials (user info, which may hold a token) are dropped, also from a URL that does not parse (everything up to its LAST '@') and from the scp form; scheme and host are lower-cased; a default port (https 443, http 80, ssh 22) is dropped; the path is cleaned ('/./', '/../', doubled slashes) and a trailing '.git' and slash are dropped; the scp form (user@host:owner/repo) equals ssh://host/owner/repo; a bare absolute path equals its file:// URL. No credential is ever written to the meta table or logged (the log line prints identities only).
(2) WHEN IT IS RECORDED. In remoteIndex.sync, after reconcileNowSwitched returned without an error, and only when the value changed. Not when the fetch succeeded, not just before the reconcile: a failed first round with a new origin must still read as a switch next round. If reconcileMain fails BEFORE moving main, a record written early would make round 2 'same origin', so it would rewind and drop the branch's own commits.
(3) UNRECORDED = NOT A SWITCH. A repository synced before the identity existed has nothing recorded; its first round is not a switch and records the identity at its end.

The identity has siblings that answer 'the same origin?' for other purposes (repos.sameFleetURL, the fleet's credential-change test; the web UI's own comparison); they are not required to agree with this one.

SPELLINGS THAT STILL COUNT AS A SWITCH: the path's case, http versus https, https versus ssh, another host name for the same server, a non-default port, a symlinked path. Consequence of a wrong 'switch': a normal merge, nothing lost; at worst a commit the same origin removed comes back once. A wrong 'same origin' is the dangerous direction (a rewind that drops), which is why every ambiguity errs to 'switch'.

MISREADINGS. 'The identity is the URL': no, a credential change or another spelling of the same origin is NOT a switch. 'Record it as soon as the fetch works': that is the mistake the failed-round test pins.

PINNED BY (store, sabotaged against the branch head by the author): TestOriginIdentity (spellings, and no credential in 8 parseable and unparseable shapes; red when an unparseable URL keeps its credentials, when the scp form is not mapped, when path cleaning or '.git' is dropped), TestSync_AnotherSpellingOrCredentialIsNotASwitch (red when the raw URL is the identity or every round is a switch), TestSync_FailedFirstRoundWithANewOriginIsStillASwitch (red when the identity is recorded once the fetch succeeded; the storeHooks.afterFetch seam fails a round right after the fetch). NOT PINNED (review 5 N1): recording just before the reconcile passes every test; only a failure inside reconcileMain before the ref moves would show it. The 'no credential recorded' assertion in the spelling test is not falsifiable for the record path; the credential stripping itself is pinned by TestOriginIdentity.
