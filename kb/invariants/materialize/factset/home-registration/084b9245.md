# Every FactSet call registers its home repo in the reader BEFORE any read (openFactSet, startPinned); a kept-plan page plans nothing, and a FactSet reader's foreign route refuses any repo without a frozen tip

In internal/corpus/materialize (since refactor/factset-pinned-seam): reader.registerHome(id12, branch, anchor) records the FactSet's home (only a binding mount: the write repo or a binding read mount; anything else is 'repo X is not available'); reader.homeMount opens it on first use with its anchor; mountOf and foreign resolve a registered home FIRST. fsCall.registerHome is called in openFactSet (every resume, page, seek, export) and in startPinned; fsCall.home() is the ONE place planning, the reservation and the read-ahead open the home, and it refuses an unregistered home.

WHY: a FactSet reader always has non-nil tips (an empty map without cross_kb), and reader.foreign admits no repo whose tip was not frozen. A page whose units are all kept plans (the LRU of 8) plans nothing, so nothing opens the home through planning; its bodies (reader.bodies) and RULE 1 marks (rule1) go through mountOf. For the write repo mountOf has its own branch, but for a non-write home (a lens member) it would fall to foreign and fail 'repo R is not available' (design B9).

CONSEQUENCES: a new FactSet entry point (F3's facade, a resume path) must call registerHome for every home it will read before serving anything. TestFactSet_Pinned_KeptPlanPageReadsTheHome pins it: a seek served from a kept plan (UnitsPlanned == 0 asserted) reads R's body at the anchor and R's RULE 1 mark now; sabotage 'register only when planning opens the home' fails with 'repo <R> is not available'.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: registration acquires no store (opening stays lazy), and it is not a reach check: the binding's own mounts are always readable (89b26b07); a home outside the binding is refused.
