# validateKindAndType is the SINGLE entry point used by both Parse and Serialize — symmetry guarantee

validateKindAndType(k, t) in kind.go is the SINGLE validation entry point shared by ParseFact AND SerializeFact. Behaviour: missing kind → normalize to DefaultKind; validate kind is well-known (Epistemic or Pragmatic); confirm k.AllowsType(t) (delegates to EpistemicTypes[t] or PragmaticTypes[t]). Returns the normalized Kind on success. SYMMETRY GUARANTEE: any Fact that round-trips through ParseFact → SerializeFact → ParseFact is guaranteed to carry a valid (kind, type) pair. Anti-pattern: validating in only one direction (e.g. SerializeFact tolerates invalid inputs to 'preserve compatibility') — silent serialization of malformed facts was a real prior bug, masked by Upsert's `default 'observation'` on read. Adding a new Type: also add it to either EpistemicTypes or PragmaticTypes map AND to the AllXxxTypes() helper.
