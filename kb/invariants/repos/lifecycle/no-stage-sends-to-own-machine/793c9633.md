# No code running under a stage's life may call Send on its own machine — Send waits for the driver, and the driver may be waiting for that life to drain; background work reports through internal posts (indexDone, crashed) only

Send queues an external event and waits for its reply; the driver handles it after the current event. If a stage goroutine Sends while the driver is in exitStages draining that very life (Unmount, a rewind, a restart), the driver waits for the goroutine and the goroutine waits for the driver: deadlock. Internal events are posts to an unbounded mailbox and never block, which is why background work reports only through them (indexDone, indexProgressed, crashed).

Enforced by TestMachine_NoStageSends (machine_test.go): it parses stages.go, sync.go, triggers.go, consensus.go, experiment_sweep.go, trigger_script.go, trigger_recipe.go and sync_mode.go and fails on any call to a method named Send. A new file whose code runs under a Life must be added to that list.

Consequence for features: a background actor that wants a lifecycle change (e.g. 'the sync loop saw the origin vanish, detach it') must not Send — it skips the tick and leaves the change to a foreground caller. Misreading: this does not forbid HTTP handlers or the create job (foreground goroutines) from Sending — that is the normal path.
