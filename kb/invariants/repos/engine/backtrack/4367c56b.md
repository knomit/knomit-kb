# The engine backtracks a line ONLY when refs_out of a relevant synthesis/insight shows a premise retracted at HEAD or judged `contradicts`; a premise that is merely superseded is history — its current version is read and judged instead — and abandoning a line drops every fact and frontier entry reached only through it

checkPremises runs in ASSESS right after refs_out(f) when f is derived (synthesis, insight), alive and judged relevant. A depth-1 explain node with deleted:true (retracted at HEAD) or a member whose verdict is contradicts sends the run to BACKTRACK: supersession(f) re-reads f at HEAD, the harness records a `support` step labelling f and the premise `unrelated` (marked `harness:`), abandon(f) marks f dead and propagates — a fact whose every origin (`via`) is dead dies too, the question origin never does — and the frontier filters entries with no live origin. The stack of lines (MAX_LINES 4, one per relevant derived fact) is popped, then the model's backtrack_choice judgment picks the next live frontier entry. More than MAX_BACKTRACKS (3) pops ends exploration.

A superseded child (explain superseded:true: the referrer reasoned over an older version) is NOT a reason to abandon: the harness offers supersession(child), which reads the current version at HEAD for judgment (E02 §5; the designer's 'supersession is history, not error').

Consequence: a retracted or contradicted fact never carries support, and a synthesis built on one is not cited. Misreading to avoid: an observation citing a retracted fact is NOT abandoned (its refs are citations, not lineage); only derived facts are.
