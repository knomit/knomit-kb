# A relevant synthesis/insight whose premise is retracted at HEAD or judged `contradicts` never carries support from the moment it is read, and its line is backtracked however it was reached; a contradicted premise survives the line with its mass, a merely superseded one is history, and abandoning a line drops every fact and frontier entry reached only through it

premiseFault(f) (internal/repos/engine_reason.js) reads the depth-1 nodes of a derived fact's (synthesis, insight) explain at HEAD, which hydrate() already holds with their deleted flags: a premise with deleted:true (retracted at HEAD) or one the model judged `contradicts` (and still live) is a fault. Three places enforce it:

1. The gate. supportPaths() excludes a derived fact with a premise fault, so it is never weighed in the posterior and never cited, from the moment it is read in full — before any refs_out, before any backtrack. A superseded premise (explain superseded:true) is NOT a fault: the harness offers supersession(child), which reads the current version at HEAD for judgment (E02 §5; the designer's 'supersession is history, not error').
2. The line. ASSESS runs checkPremises right after the model's refs_out(f); and WIDEN, before asking the model anything, runs deadLine() over every live derived fact judged relevant, however it was reached (gather, seek, any move). For a dead line whose refs_out has not run and while moves remain, the harness executes refs_out(f) itself from the cached explain (recorded `harness: <f> rests on a <cause> premise …`), so the line's members are judged and the next ASSESS sends the run to BACKTRACK; with the move budget spent it goes to BACKTRACK directly.
3. EMIT. The freshness pass re-reads each support with explainAll and, for a derived support, refreshes its depth-1 children; a premise retracted under the run sends it to BACKTRACK (counted as a freshness round, MAX_FRESHNESS).

BACKTRACK: supersession(f) re-reads f at HEAD, f is labelled `unrelated` (marked `harness:`), abandon(f) marks f dead and propagates — a fact whose every origin (`via`) is dead dies too, the question origin never does — and the frontier filters entries with no live origin. A RETRACTED premise is labelled `unrelated` (history). A CONTRADICTED premise keeps its `contradicts` verdict, is passed to abandon as `keep` and survives even when it was reached only through the line: its mass stays in the posterior's C and the answer body names it under "Weighed against" (E02 §5, §8). The stack of lines (MAX_LINES 4) is popped, then the model's backtrack_choice judgment picks the next live frontier entry. More than MAX_BACKTRACKS (3) pops ends exploration.

Consequence: a retracted or contradicted premise never lets the synthesis built on it carry support or be cited, whether or not the model ever chooses refs_out on it; and dropping a line never drops the contradiction that killed it. Pinned by TestEngineBlocker_B1_DeadPremiseWithoutRefsOut (the model never chooses refs_out; also F1: a fact reached only through the abandoned line is dropped, one reached through the gather too stays), TestReasonJS_PremiseGate (the gate, the superseded exemption), TestEngineOracle_FreshnessBeforeEmit (a premise retracted under the run) and TestEngineBlocker_B2_ContradictedPremiseKeepsItsMass (posterior W·S/(S+C) with C intact after the backtrack).

Misreading to avoid: an observation citing a retracted fact is NOT gated or abandoned (its refs are citations, not lineage); only derived facts are. And the gate does not need the backtrack: a dead line the run never backtracks (step budget) is still excluded from support.
