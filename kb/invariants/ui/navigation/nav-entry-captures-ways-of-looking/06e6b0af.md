# A NavEntry must capture every "way of looking" — sort AND lensSources — or Back returns the reader to a view they were never in; the refinements themselves still must not push

`NavEntry` (web/src/state.ts) carries `sort` and `lensSources` alongside path/filters/factPath, and `NAV_BACK` restores both on BOTH of its branches (the same-repo one and the repo-changed one).

**The rule has two halves and they pull in opposite directions.**

1. **Capture every way of looking in the entry.** A sort axis and a sources selection are not preferences — they change WHICH rows exist and in what order. An entry that restores the path but not these lands the reader in a composite state that never existed: correct folder, wrong rows.

2. **The refinements themselves do NOT push.** `SET_LIBRARY_SORT` and `SET_LENS_SOURCES` change state without `pushNav`. Flipping a sort tab or toggling a mount refines the view you are in; it is not a move. Making them push would mean six Back presses to undo building a three-mount selection.

The consequence of the two halves together: a state change that IS a move must be its own action that pushes, and it must set every field it touches in that one reducer arm. `FOCUS_LENS_SOURCE` (the summary's Repos section — "show me this mount") is the worked example: it sets `lensSources: [repo]`, `librarySort: 'recent'`, `factPath: null` and pushes ONE entry.

**How this was gotten wrong**, and the shape to watch for: the pick was first implemented in the COMPONENT as two dispatches, `SET_LENS_SOURCES` then `SET_LIBRARY_SORT`. Neither pushes, so the move left no history at all — Back restored the sort (from an older entry) while leaving the union pinned to one mount, and the reader had no way back to the full union except the dropdown. Two dispatches for one intent is the smell: the history has nothing to hook, and no single arm owns the resulting state. Collapse it into one intent-named action in the reducer — the same argument that made `NAVIGATE` win over `ADD_FILTER{path}` for entering a directory.

**Not what this says:** it does NOT say every state change should push. It says the entry must be COMPLETE, and that anything which moves must push exactly once. A new field added to AppState that changes which rows are visible needs a line in `pushNav` and in both `NAV_BACK` branches — adding it to AppState alone silently makes Back lie.
