# FactBody refs branch by scheme: schemeless = clickable fact path; http(s) = anchor; everything else = inert

FactBody's References section branches on the ref's scheme — getting this wrong leaks non-navigable URIs into the browser or into a fake knomit-fact navigation:

1. `http://` / `https://` → external anchor (`<a href={ref} target="_blank">`).
2. Schemeless (no `^[a-z][a-z0-9+.-]*:` prefix) → treated as a knomit-local fact path; clickable span that calls `onRefClick(ref)` to open ExplainView.
3. Anything else (`src://`, `file:///`, future schemes) → INERT grey span. NOT a clickable anchor (the browser cannot open a `src:` protocol) and NOT routed through `onRefClick` (which expects a knomit fact path — `src://` and `file:///` are source-file / filesystem refs, not facts).

Anti-pattern (the original bug): falling back to "if not http(s), treat as onRefClick target" — that fed `src://knomit/internal/...` into `api.fact()` as if it were a fact path. The minimum disambiguator is a regex scheme test; do not just check `ref.startsWith('http')`.

Regression: `FactBody.test.tsx` covers `src://` and `file:///` rendering as `<span>` (not `<a>`, no `href`, and `onRefClick` never called on click).

Related: knomit ref-scheme convention [[ref-scheme]] defines the four ref forms; [[ref-click-opens-explain]] decided that schemeless local refs open ExplainView at the parent's commit.
